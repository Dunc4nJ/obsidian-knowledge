# Brazil Potash Corp (GRO)

Brazil Potash Corp (GRO, NYSE American) — development-stage fertilizer mining company building the Autazes potash project in Amazonas Basin, Brazil. Per @victor_privin (Fermion) May 2026 reveal thread: ~$150M mcap vs ~$1B steady-state annual EBITDA potential, 2.4M tons/yr production, ~$79/tonne opex, ~75% EBITDA margins, 10-year binding take-or-pay 900K tons offtake (37.5% of planned capacity), all 21 environmental + installation licenses secured from Amazonas Environmental Protection Institute, Franco-Nevada royalty financing framework could unlock ~$150M construction capital. Brazil consumes ~22% of global potash demand but produces <1% of global supply. Logistics moat: ~70% lower transport cost vs foreign imports via Amazonas inland waterway access. Origin: Petrobras Petromisa identified basin 1970s-80s, 2006 Pre-Salt oil discovery redirected Petrobras capital, 2008 auction → Potássio do Brasil private company → now public as GRO. Executive Chairman previously ran world's largest fertilizer company; CEO ex-Rio Tinto multi-billion mining ops.
