# Ondas (ONDS)

Ondas Inc. (NASDAQ: ONDS, renamed from Ondas Holdings in January 2026) is an autonomous-systems platform consolidating defense and commercial drone, ground robotics, and counter-UAS capabilities. The Ondas Autonomous Systems (OAS) unit operates American Robotics (FAA-certified Optimus aerial security), Airobotics, Apeiro Motion (ground robotics + tethered UAVs), Roboteam (combat-proven UGVs), Sentrycs (Cyber-over-RF counter-UAS), and 4M Defense (multi-domain ISR + demining). Ondas Networks sells the 802.16s FullMAX software-defined-radio platform for Mission-Critical IoT. Ondas Capital deploys $150M into a "Technology Bridge" connecting Ukraine-origin combat-proven autonomy into US/EU markets.

2025 delivered $50.7M revenue (+605% YoY); 2026 guide is $375M+. Pro-forma cash sits at ~$1.5B after the January 2026 $1B raise, but the resulting 195.5M long-dated warrants ($20 and $28 strikes, 7-year expiry) plus 489M shares outstanding create a structural delta-hedging overhang that suppresses momentum. Stock is at $4.7B market cap, ~94x trailing P/S, vs. Morningstar's $4.83 fair value and Street consensus PT of $20 (123% upside).

Anchor catalysts: December 2025 prime contract for an autonomous border-protection system ("thousands of drones," multi-year), and April 2026 $50M demining award through 4M Defense for Israel's $1.7B Eastern Border Security Barrier (500km Jordan border).
