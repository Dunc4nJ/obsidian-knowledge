# UBS bulls CEVA on Apple modem content gains then edge AI and robotics ramp in 2027-2028

## Key Takeaways

- UBS frames [[CEVA (CEVA)]] upside as two stacked legs: an [[Apple (AAPL)]] internal-modem content ramp in 2026-2027 (taking share from [[Qualcomm (QCOM)]]), then an edge AI/robotics royalty wave in 2027-2028. The trade is to own the IP licensor when the customer-of-customer thesis converts to royalty dollars.
- The numbers UBS attaches to the Apple ramp are concrete: royalty growth jumps to ~12-15% Y/Y in 2027 (vs 8-10% pre-Apple) as Apple's internal modem rolls across the full iPhone lineup; Apple is roadmapped to cover both modem and Wi-Fi using CEVA IP.
- 2028 doesn't add much *modem* incrementality (full lineup already covered by 2027) — the next leg has to come from non-modem Apple licensing (AI in newer products) plus the broader edge AI/robotics ramp UBS expects to "ramp meaningfully into C2028."
- 2026 is a transition year: $50M licensing growth target, but tougher Bluetooth comps offset some royalty strength. Near-term licensing line has been stagnant — the meaningful royalty conversion lands in 2027+, which is the same timing arc as the [[Crux Capital 2026 edge AI shortlist - Ambarella CEVA and Ouster as vision chip IP and lidar plays|Crux thesis]] for CEVA.
- WriteDownCap's framing — "sneaky good robotics play everyone will talk about in 2027-2028" — pairs with the UBS work as a buy-the-quiet-period setup: the modem ramp is the bridge that gets you paid while waiting for the edge AI/robotics royalty curve.
