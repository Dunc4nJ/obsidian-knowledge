# CPS Technologies (CPSH)

CPS Technologies (CPSH, NASDAQ) — Massachusetts-based maker of aluminum silicon carbide (AlSiC) composite baseplates for SiC/GaN power modules. Solves thermal management + CTE-matching to ceramic substrates; 180 W/mK conductivity, CTE <10 ppm/°C vs copper's 17 ppm/°C delamination after 4,000 cycles. Only publicly traded US pure-play; A.L.M.T. (Sumitomo subsidiary) is global leader, Denka/Dowa/Plansee complete the Japan/Europe trio, Amulaire + Huangshan Googe + Jiangyin Saiying + Suzhou Haoli are China/Taiwan price competitors. Per @saso_equity 2026-05-24: 10x-from-here SiC/GaN-baseplate rerate candidate alongside NVTS/WOLF/VICR rerate precedent; CHIPS Act domestic-sourcing tailwind.
