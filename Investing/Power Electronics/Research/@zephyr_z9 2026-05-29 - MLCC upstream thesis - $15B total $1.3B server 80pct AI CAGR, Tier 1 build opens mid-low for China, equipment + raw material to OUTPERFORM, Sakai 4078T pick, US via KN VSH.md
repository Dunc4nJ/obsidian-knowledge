## Key Takeaways

- **Market sizing (load-bearing)**: $15B total MLCC market. **Server MLCC 2025 = $1.3B** split **$600M AI servers + $700M general servers**. **AI server MLCC growing at 80%+ CAGR** (the biggest growth slice in the entire passive-component complex). General server MLCC will accelerate to **30-40% CAGR** as agentic AI lifts CPU demand. **Smartphone/mobile MLCC NEGATIVE growth through 2026-27** — the demand mix is rotating violently from consumer to server. **Humanoids** flagged as future high-growth market — connect this to the existing Robotics sector ([[Harmonic Drive Systems (6324)]] / [[Nabtesco (6268.T)]] / [[Nidec (6594.T)]] / [[Leaderdrive (688017.SH)]]). **Book-to-bill >1 for most MLCC suppliers** — backlog is real.
- **Price-hike mechanism is structural, not opportunistic**: High **Nickel + Silver** raw-material costs hitting all segments. Supply-demand mismatch is most acute in **high-cap, high-voltage** segment (the auto + server slice). **High-end MLCC lead time >20 weeks** — multi-quarter customer ordering lock-in. Spot/distributor prices **+20-40% for low-cap consumer-device MLCCs** driven by hoarding + double-booking (especially in China). **OEM contracts have NOT yet seen large price hikes** — implies the next leg of upside is contract repricing into 2026-27 books.
- **Tier 1 vs Tier 2/3 capacity dynamic**: **Tier 1 (Murata + Taiyo Yuden + SEMCO) building AI server MLCC capacity**, creating the structural "Macronix effect" — leaders move upmarket toward AI/server demand, opening mid-to-low-end share for **Tier 2/3 + Chinese suppliers**. The macro setup mirrors classic semi-cycle leadership-rotation. Reader @HNbrbfSJvh19310 (Chinese) counters that this is already priced — **Sanhuan Group + Fenghua Hi-Tech** A-shares have **already 10x'd** on this consensus.
- **Murata expects blended ASP FLAT** — consumer ASP down, AI server expansion offsetting. **Blended-ASP-flat at +30-80% CAGR top-line growth is the operating-leverage thesis** — fixed-cost absorption + premium-mix shift drives operating profit even if headline ASPs stagnate.
- **LOAD-BEARING UPSTREAM-FLIP THESIS** (the punchline): **"MLCC production equipment & raw materials suppliers will be the biggest beneficiary of this CAPEX boom. MLCC producer stocks have performed well, and it is finally spilling to raw material/equipment producers. I expect them to outperform MLCC producers now."** This is the differentiating signal in this capture vs the NuttyCLD MLCC-makers framing — zephyr_z9 argues the next leg of upside has rotated one layer upstream.
- **Sakai Chemical (4078.T) is the explicit author pick** (via self-QT to a Feb 18, 2026 thread): supplies **dielectric powder for MLCCs (barium carbonate + barium titanate)** through the **Electronic Materials segment** — currently **14-15% of revenue but ~25% of operating profit, with EM's operating-profit share continuing to grow**. The "1 layer upstream of Murata" framing. Reader @Fu1Hal opened long position the prior day on Sakai per zephyr's earlier rec.
- **US-listed access via [[Knowles (KN)]] + [[Vishay Intertechnology (VSH)]]** per @kingswaycap — the canonical US Tier 1 MLCC-exposure plays for non-IBKR accounts. Mirrors the EOPSF-as-OTC-ADR retail-access pattern seen in the [[EOS Defence Systems (EOS.AX)]] hub. @Song_13i Chinese reply *"看好 $VSH"* (bullish on VSH) is the independent second voice confirming VSH as accessible exposure.
