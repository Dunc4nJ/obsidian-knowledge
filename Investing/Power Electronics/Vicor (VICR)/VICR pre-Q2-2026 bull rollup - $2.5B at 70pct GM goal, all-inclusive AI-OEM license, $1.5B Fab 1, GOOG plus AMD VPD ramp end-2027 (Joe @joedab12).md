# VICR pre-Q2-2026 bull rollup — $2.5B at 70% GM goal, all-inclusive AI-OEM license, $1.5B Fab 1, GOOG + AMD VPD ramp end-2027

Posted the day before Vicor's Q2 2026 earnings (reports premarket Jul 21), this is Joe (@joedab12)'s consolidated bull case from a concentrated holder who says he has direct access to the CEO. It stacks the recent operational/strategic updates into a single "why you're still early" argument and puts a rough timeline on the path from ~$700M to the raised $2.5B revenue goal.

## Key Takeaways

- **Raised long-term model to $2.5B revenue at 70% gross margin**, and management framed the ramp as potentially fast ("there are scenarios where it can happen remarkably quickly"). Joe's own bridge: the $1.5B Fab 1 is full by mid-2027 and $200–300M of annualized royalties come online next year, so ~$1.7B exiting 2027 is "basically a lock" absent a botched fab ramp; getting to $2.5B needs a second-source deal or Fab 2, plausibly a mid-2028 event, with ~$2.0B reachable on new royalties/licenses alone.
- **A new "all-inclusive license" was signed with an AI-chip OEM.** Joe reasons the counterparty is [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]] — outside existing customer [[Cerebras (CBRS)]], the only AI-chip OEMs are [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] and AMD, and he argues there isn't enough Fab 1 capacity yet to be serving NVDA.
- **Vicor's own IP-first "commoditize the position" strategy is the thesis' spine.** The transcribed CEO Q&A argues every AI system will need VPD (vertical power delivery) within ~2 years; NVDA has not yet deployed VPD and is power/GPU-constrained without it, while Google took the lead on first-gen VPD (enabling the TPU) despite expensive, hard-to-assemble early solutions — implying all hyperscalers eventually must follow.
- **Two anchor VPD ramps set for end of 2027**: one large hyperscaler (Joe: almost surely [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]]) and one OEM (Joe: AMD), and Fab 1 alone ($1.5B) is only enough to get those two started — hence the second-source talks with a "very large" company and the active shopping for Fab 2.
- **Structural moat/alignment markers**: CEO/founder owns 25%+ of the company; the ITC ruled in Vicor's favor last year against Delta Electronics, Foxconn and Quanta (Delta being ~4x the size of [[Monolithic Power (MPWR)]] and a favored Jensen supplier); and advanced-packaging complexity (CoPoS) makes VPD "almost a necessity."
