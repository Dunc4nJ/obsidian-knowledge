# VICR vs MPWR comp valuation — Fab 1 alone worth ~$600 per share, second source $800-1,000

Posted the night of Vicor's ~$14.3B-cap "huge move," this is Joe (@joedab12)'s numeric backbone for the [[Vicor (VICR)]] price targets: a direct comparison to [[Monolithic Power (MPWR)]] that strips out any credit for Fab 2 or the third-party license and values VICR on 2027 revenue alone. It is the valuation model the rest of his thesis hangs on.

## Key Takeaways

- **The core comp: [[Monolithic Power (MPWR)]] carries an $82B cap on ~$3.5B 2026 / $4.5B 2027 revenue (~55% GM) with essentially ZERO IP/licensing revenue, while [[Vicor (VICR)]] carries a $14.3B cap on $1.5B product + $100-120M of 100%-gross-margin recurring royalty (~70% GM) in 2027.** Same end market (power modules for AI compute), but VICR owns a debt-free, 100%-US fab whereas MPWR outsources 100% of manufacturing to Chinese/Korean/Taiwanese partners — so the higher-quality revenue trades at a fraction of the multiple.
- **"Fab 1 alone" math: give VICR ZERO value for Fab 2 and the license deal, value it purely on 2027 revenue vs MPWR (1.6B vs 4.5B), and it should command ~35% of MPWR's cap = ~$29B = just over $600/share.** This is the load-bearing floor claim — Fab 1 fully ramped (expected by year-end) is the only input needed to justify a triple from the ~$14.3B / ~$200 level.
- **Path to $800-1,000/share = Fab 1 fully ramped + a "fair deal" with a second-source/licensee (Joe floats [[Delta Electronics (2308.TW)]]) for demand that can't wait for Fab 2 in 2028.** The upside case is additive to the $600 floor, not a replacement for it.
- **Fab 1's $1.5B annual capacity is already spoken for by [[Cerebras (CBRS)]], one hyperscaler and one OEM (Joe's high-probability read: [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]] and [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]]); Fab 2 (Andover MA, at least as large) comes online 2028**, and demand is heavy enough that a third-party manufacturing/licensing deal is being put in place for volume that "CANT WAIT UNTIL 2028."
- **The attached Vicor quote frames the second-source strategy as capacity expansion, not desperation**: as the first ChiP fab approaches high utilization, Vicor is planning a second fab AND enabling an alternate source of high-current-density 2nd-Gen VPD modules so licensed OEMs and hyperscalers get best-in-class power from more than one source — i.e., multi-sourcing is a feature Vicor is selling to customers, not a concession. A reply from @Philip_pan2008 adds that MPWR is reportedly negotiating with VICR for VPD license authorization following an ITC suit.
