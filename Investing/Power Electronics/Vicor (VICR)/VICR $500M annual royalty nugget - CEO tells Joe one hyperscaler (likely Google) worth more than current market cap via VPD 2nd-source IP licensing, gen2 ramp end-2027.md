# VICR $500M annual royalty nugget — CEO tells Joe one hyperscaler (likely Google) worth more than current market cap via VPD 2nd-source IP licensing, gen2 ramp end-2027

A Jul 16 2026 exchange between Joe (@joedab12) (a concentrated Vicor bull who says he has direct CEO access) and @hewhogathers (KOD Capital) that quantifies the **royalty leg** of the VICR model with direct-CEO sourcing. Joe relays that at the recent annual shareholder meeting Q&A he asked the CEO about royalty potential, and the CEO framed one large hyperscaler — which Joe believes is [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]] — as a possible "$500m royalty nugget" per year. The thread also spells out the *mechanism* Joe thinks generates that royalty, and reconciles it with the [[VICR pre-Q2-2026 bull rollup - $2.5B at 70pct GM goal, all-inclusive AI-OEM license, $1.5B Fab 1, GOOG plus AMD VPD ramp end-2027 (Joe @joedab12)]] framing. Joe was rotating trading profits out of memory ([[Micron (MU)]], [[SanDisk (SNDK)]]) into VICR as this posted.

## Key Takeaways

- **The scoop, direct from a CEO Q&A**: Joe asked about royalty potential at Vicor's recent annual shareholder meeting, and per him the CEO said one large hyperscaler "could be [a] 500m royalty nugget with us." Joe adds the CEO privately told him that hyperscaler — Joe says [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]] ("he said one large hyperscaler") — is "worth $500m a year in annual royalties," and that **that $500M alone is worth more than VICR's current market cap.**
- **The royalty mechanism Joe infers**: royalties flow because Vicor **licenses its full VPD IP to a 2nd source of manufacturing**, and that 2nd source then produces the *additional* VPD modules to cover the hyperscaler's excess demand beyond Fab 1's ~$1.5B capacity — with Vicor collecting a royalty on that 2nd-source volume. Joe flags he is "not 100% sure" of the exact mechanism and hopes to get clarity on the upcoming (Q2 2026) call.
- **Timing anchor**: Joe believes this hyperscaler is ramping **gen2 VPD toward the end of 2027** and will be "a significant chunk of the full 1.5b capacity once it reaches full capacity" — so the $500M royalty is a longer-dated, post-gen2-ramp figure, distinct from the nearer-term $200–300M annualized royalties Joe models for 2027 in the [[VICR pre-Q2-2026 bull rollup - $2.5B at 70pct GM goal, all-inclusive AI-OEM license, $1.5B Fab 1, GOOG plus AMD VPD ramp end-2027 (Joe @joedab12)|bull rollup]].
- **Margin/valuation read-through**: asked whether $2.5B revenue at 55% gross margin for 2028 is a safe conservative model, Joe says no — he sees that as a 2028 *run rate* and at **70% gross margin, not 55%**, precisely because high-margin royalty/licensing revenue "is expected to grow fast from here." This royalty leg is what bridges VICR from a mid-50s% hardware GM to the ~70% blended target. For scale, FY25 royalty income was only ~$57.4M (see [[VICR 2025-Q4 earnings call - FY25 product rev $350.3M royalty $57.4M plus $45M patent settlement, Q4 book-to-bill above 1.2, Fab 1 nearing 80pct utilization|FY25 earnings]]) — so a single $500M hyperscaler royalty stream is a ~9x step-change on the royalty line.
- **Sourcing caveat**: this is a bull relaying management color secondhand, with his own inference of the licensing structure and his own attribution of "one large hyperscaler" to Google. The $500M figure is CEO-sourced (Q&A on record per Joe); the Google identity and the exact 2nd-source royalty plumbing are Joe's interpretation, not confirmed disclosures.
