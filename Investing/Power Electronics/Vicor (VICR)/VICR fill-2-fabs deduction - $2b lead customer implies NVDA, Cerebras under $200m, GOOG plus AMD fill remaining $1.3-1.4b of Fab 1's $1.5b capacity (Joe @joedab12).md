# VICR fill-2-fabs deduction — $2b lead customer implies NVDA, Cerebras under $200m, GOOG plus AMD fill remaining $1.3-1.4b of Fab 1's $1.5b capacity

The most rigorous "receipts" version of Joe (@joedab12)'s [[Vicor (VICR)]] customer-attribution deduction, backed by four verbatim earnings-call transcript screenshots. Starting from CEO Patrizio Vinciarelli's claim that one customer alone could "fill 2 fabs," Joe reverse-engineers the roster: that customer is worth ~$2b/yr to Vicor (has to be [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] Rubin); [[Cerebras (CBRS)]] — the publicized lead customer, IPO'ing Thursday — is worth under $200m; therefore [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]] and [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]] must be filling the remaining $1.3-1.4b of Fab 1's expanded $1.5b capacity. ## Key Takeaways

- **The "fill 2 fabs" quote is the load-bearing datum: on the call the CEO said "there's one in particular with tremendous opportunity in terms of volume. That one alone [could] fill 2 fabs."** With Fab 1 capacity then at ~$1b/yr revenue, one customer worth ~$2b/yr on next-gen VPD "HAS TO BE" [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] Rubin — though Joe is explicit that VICR does not yet have NVDA as a booked customer, and separately argues VICR doesn't currently have the capacity to serve NVDA.
- **Capacity progression is $1b → $1.5b inside Fab 1, NOT a second fab.** The step-up came from moving simpler process steps to an adjacent building ("this is NOT fab 2, this is fab1 ancillary building") and de-bottlenecking the historically capacity-limiting complex steps in the main fab — an all-inclusive $1.5b Federal Street / Fab 1 number the CEO says could go higher only via more advanced-product special capacity.
- **The subtraction that names GOOG and AMD**: assign at most ~$200m of the new $1.5b to [[Cerebras (CBRS)]] (Joe says even $100m "would be a stretch"), which leaves $1.3-1.4b for two other customers "who combined are about half the volume of NVDA" — Joe's "only logical conclusion" is [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]] and [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]], consistent with management's disclosed hyperscaler + chip-OEM meetings that directly triggered the capacity expansion.
- **Management corroboration for the two mystery customers is on the transcript**: VP of Global Sales Philip Davies confirmed the sales motion — bringing the global FAE team into "different locations where we have target hyperscalers and OEM chip companies located" for Gen 5 VPD boot camps — and the CEO confirmed the $1.5b expansion is sized to satisfy the Q3-referenced hyperscaler and OEM.
- **Dual monetization path preserved**: the CEO explicitly frames leverage "both by selling product and by collecting licensing" — the same second-source-licensee optionality that underpins the higher price-target rungs in Joe's other posts. Posted the week of the Cerebras IPO as a "is it too late to buy" rebuttal ("my answer is NO. I bought more today").
