# VICR Jun 2026 annual meeting recap — $2.5B at 70% GM goal, 3-week OEM license, gen2 VPD hyperscaler + OEM ramp next year, 2nd-source pick underway, Fab 2 + 650k sq ft, royalty ARR $90M toward $300-400M

The densest single Joe (@joedab12) note on Vicor: a first-person recap of the June 19 2026 annual shareholder meeting, including 15 minutes talking to Vicor sales/marketing chief **Phil Davies** before the official meeting and multiple direct quotes from founder-CEO Patrizio Vinciarelli. It pairs the operational updates with a full recording of the ~2-hour strategy presentation and Q&A. This complements Joe's later [[VICR pre-Q2-2026 bull rollup - $2.5B at 70pct GM goal, all-inclusive AI-OEM license, $1.5B Fab 1, GOOG plus AMD VPD ramp end-2027 (Joe @joedab12)|pre-Q2-2026 bull rollup]], which re-uses many of the same data points.

## Key Takeaways

- **Long-term model raised to $2.5B revenue at 70% gross margin — up from $1B at 60% just a year earlier.** Patrizio's direct quote on the goal: *"I see scenarios where this can happen remarkably quickly."* Joe reads that as pointing at the second-source deal, which he thinks could add ~$1B for the hyperscaler + OEM ramp that Fab 1 alone is only "enough to get started."
- **The May all-inclusive OEM license was negotiated in ~3 weeks, and — critically — it lets the licensee keep buying from other partners Vicor knows are infringing its IP.** Per Davies, this was the least-disruptive way to enforce the patents without breaking the current supply chain; the licensee transitions to more Vicor-fab product over time. One hyperscaler and one OEM plan to ramp **gen2 VPD next year** (Joe's guess: [[Alphabet (GOOGL)|$GOOG]] + [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)|$AMD]]), on top of [[Cerebras (CBRS)]] already ramping gen1 and moving to gen2.
- **Second-source manufacturing partner is being *chosen*, not chased — demand is so strong VICR gets to pick.** Talks are underway with another candidate meeting early July; Joe expects no announcement until late 2026 / early 2027 (not the next earnings call). He frames second-source as the key that unlocks $2.5B+, and argues landing [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] (worth ~$2B/yr to Vicor at this stage) could push the model to $4-5B+.
- **Capacity runway is enormous: a Fab 2 building offer is out on a structure ~equal to Fab 1, with room for an extra 650k sq ft — utilizing it would roughly 4x current fab footprint.** Fab 1 has been reconfigured to a $1.5B run-rate at some point in 2027, but Davies told Joe that's only "enough to get them started" for the gen2-VPD hyperscaler and OEM. There is also a customer (Joe's guess: NVDA) they may partner with to build a dedicated VPD fab.
- **Royalty/licensing ARR is compounding ahead of plan: $90M ARR as of Oct 2025, with prior "line of sight" to $180M in a couple years now expected sooner, and a ceiling "well above" $300-400M longer term.** Joe adds a later data point: one hyperscaler (he says Google) alone could eventually bring ~$500M in annual royalty income. For scale, he contrasts VICR (~$15B market cap, targeting $1.75B gross profit at $2.5B/70%) against [[Monolithic Power (MPWR)]] (~$75B market cap on ~$1.87B gross profit this year).
