# VICR May-26 all-inclusive OEM license reads as coming 2nd-source licensee - multi-source access language, Q2 guide raised $126M to $142M (Joe @joedab12)

## Key Takeaways

- **The May 26 2026 guidance-revision release is the load-bearing event.** Vicor raised Q2 revenue guidance from $126M to $142M on the back of "rising product revenues and royalties from an additional licensee," and disclosed that an OEM proactively secured an all-inclusive license covering all Vicor patents — power converter topologies, control systems, components, distribution architectures, Factorized Power (FPA) and Vertical Power Delivery (VPD). This is the concrete catalyst behind the whole thesis (see [[VICR pre-Q2-2026 bull rollup - $2.5B at 70pct GM goal, all-inclusive AI-OEM license, $1.5B Fab 1, GOOG plus AMD VPD ramp end-2027 (Joe @joedab12)]]).

- **Joe's core read: "multi-source access" is code for a 2nd source / third-party licensee.** CEO Patrizio Vinciarelli's quote — an all-inclusive license "enables OEMs and hyper-scalers to secure multi-source access to all of the power system innovations pioneered and patented by Vicor" — is interpreted as Vicor lining up a contract manufacturer/licensee to serve customers who don't fit inside Fab 1's ~$1.5B annual capacity. He points to the Q1 release (screenshot 2) where Vinciarelli says an "alternate source of high current density 2nd Gen VPD modules will give OEMs and Hyper-scalers redundant access to enabling VPD power system technology."

- **The re-rate trigger is the formal 2nd-source announcement, not the license itself.** Joe expects the market to start pricing this in only once Vicor announces a formal 3rd-party license / 2nd-source manufacturing agreement; he believes they've been working on it since early in the year and that Asian capacity is available. Frames "next few weeks" as the last window before the stock is "over 300-350 for good."

- **Exclusion-order leverage is the moat mechanism.** Vinciarelli explicitly ties licensing demand to ITC exclusion orders barring importation of computing systems from infringing suppliers — "respect for intellectual property is motivated by exclusion orders." Joe separately predicts an eventual company sale (CEO turning 80) contingent on winning the 2nd ITC case and securing [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] as a Feynman-generation customer.

- **Positioned as an AI last-inch power-delivery / power-density story** spanning [[Nvidia (NVDA)]], [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]] and [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]] rack roadmaps (a reply notes Nvidia shipping Rubin racks in two flavors — "less socamm and full" — meaning more racks = more last-inch power delivery = more license printing).

>
> Net income for the first quarter was $20.7 million, or $0.44 per diluted share, compared to net income of $2.5 million or $0.06 per diluted share, for the corresponding period a year ago and net income of $46.5 million, or $1.01 per diluted share, for the fourth quarter of 2025. Net income in the fourth quarter included $27.3 million of tax benefit due to the partial recognition of certain deferred tax assets in the period.
>
> Cash flow used for operations totaled $(3.9) million for the first quarter, net of a $28.6 million payment of an award for past litigation, compared to cash flow from operations of $20.1 million for the corresponding period a year ago, and cash flow from operations of $15.7 million in the fourth quarter of 2025. Capital expenditures for the first quarter totaled $12.4 million, compared to $4.6 million for the corresponding period a year ago and $5.5 million for the fourth quarter of 2025. Cash and cash equivalents as of March 31, 2026 increased 0.4% sequentially to approximately $404.2 million compared to approximately $402.8 million as of December 31, 2025.
