# VICR Q1-2026 earnings preview — 7-point Apr 21 checklist (Gen2 VPD demos, full Fab 1 utilization by year-end, 3rd-party mfg deal implies $300+)

Pre-earnings watch list from [[Vicor (VICR)]] bull Joe (@joedab12), posted 9 Apr 2026 ahead of the 21 Apr premarket call. Joe holds a concentrated VICR position and claims direct CEO access; he frames the whole report as a WHO-is-filling-Fab-1 story rather than a backward-looking numbers story, and lists seven specific management signals he is grading. This establishes the framework the rest of the coverage tests against.

## Key Takeaways

- **The report is a Fab-1-fill story, not a numbers story.** Joe grades seven forward signals on [[Vicor (VICR)]]'s Q1 2026 call (21 Apr, 7am ET premarket — the first morning call in years, deliberately timed as the two-week ceasefire expires that evening). Point 1: whether the Gen2 VPD (vertical power delivery) demos at Vicor's Santa Clara facility went well — on the prior call management said ALL the big customers were coming in to demo Gen2 VPD with Vicor head engineers.

- **Fab 1 fill = the whole thesis; he names the likely filler.** Fab 1 is capable of ~$1B product revenue / ~$1B ARR. [[Cerebras (CBRS)]] is the confirmed lead customer but "ZERO chance" it is big enough to fill the fab alone, so Joe expects a major hyperscaler in early ramp — most likely [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]], possibly [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]] or [[Amazon (AMZN)]], and explicitly NOT [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] (which would need far more capacity than Fab 1 holds). His tell that utilization is heading to 24/7 by year-end: 3rd-shift job postings on Vicor's site, with a "decent chance" the timeline gets pulled forward to Q3.

- **A 3rd-party manufacturing/licensing deal is the $300+ catalyst.** Point 4: progress on (or announcement of) a 2nd-source manufacturing partnership would signal a customer that cannot wait for Fab 2 (2028) — "the stock skyrockets to $300+." Joe explicitly ties the urgency to [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] Feynman launching in 2028 and needing its supply chain locked well before then.

- **Valuation anchor: line up 2027 vs [[Monolithic Power (MPWR)]].** Base case is Fab 1 at full $1B capacity by year-end; Joe argues you should put VICR's 2027 projections next to [[Monolithic Power (MPWR)]] and compare market caps to see VICR "is NOT expensive at all for what they have" — the same conviction pattern he had on [[Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI)]] during its 1.6T-driven run to $77.

- **Secondary signals he's grading.** Book-to-bill has improved three quarters in a row and he expects continuation — an explicit thesis-breaker if it reverses (point 7). Fab 2 site selection / an existing building for fast retrofit (no long-lead epi or EUV tools needed, so it can move quickly, point 5). ITC case progress — Vicor has already won suits against many major power-module makers and filed a 2nd suit in January; he wants management's confidence level to show (point 6).

- **Bear pushback captured in-thread.** Replies argue [[Vicor (VICR)]] management "has no idea how to talk to Wall St," the Q4 report was "mediocre/bad" with licensing momentum failing to match Q3 excitement (hard to argue >$10B cap until a $140M+ quarter with accelerating book-to-bill), and cite constant CEO/insider selling (the 79-year-old founder sold a few hundred K shares). Joe rebuts that critics are looking backward and missing the fab-fill story, and that the attached Vicor statement already confirms the 2nd-source path.
