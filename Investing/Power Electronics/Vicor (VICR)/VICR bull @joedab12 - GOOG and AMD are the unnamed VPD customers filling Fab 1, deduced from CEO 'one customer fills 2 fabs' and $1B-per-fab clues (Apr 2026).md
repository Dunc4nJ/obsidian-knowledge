# VICR bull @joedab12 - GOOG and AMD are the unnamed VPD customers filling Fab 1, deduced from CEO 'one customer fills 2 fabs' and $1B-per-fab clues (Apr 2026)

The canonical "GOOG + AMD are ramping [[Vicor (VICR)]] VPD" explainer that Joe (@joedab12) self-reposts as a must-read. It lays out the customer-identity deduction that anchors the whole thesis: reading management's oblique earnings-call language about capacity to name the unnamed customers.

## Key Takeaways

- **The core deduction (capacity math):** CEO Patrizio Vinciarelli said a single customer alone could "fill 2 fabs," and Fab 1 was disclosed as ~$1B/yr of revenue capacity — so one customer implies ~$2B/yr of VPD demand, i.e. [[Nvidia (NVDA)]]-scale volume. Joe reasons NVDA is the obvious ~$2B anchor, and asks "who is half the volumes of Nvidia?" — his answer: "it can only be" Google ([[Alphabet (GOOGL)]]), with [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]] as the OEM. Vicor's VPD (Vertical Power Delivery) is the product.
- **Cerebras is the named lead customer but a red herring on volume:** [[Cerebras (CBRS)]] is the officially-disclosed lead VPD customer and first to formally sign, but Joe argues its volume is "not even close to enough to fill that fab" — so the capacity-filling customers must be the still-unnamed hyperscaler (Google) and OEM chip company (AMD) that management references only obliquely.
- **Capacity is being raised, corroborating demand:** On the Q1 2026 call the CEO lifted Fab 1's ("Federal Street") earmarked run-rate from ~$1B to "at least $1.5 billion" all-inclusive, with a second fab and alternate sourcing named as next strategic steps — evidence VPD demand is outrunning current capacity rather than a hope.
- **A second corroborating tell (sales motion):** VP of Global Sales Philip Davies said Vicor is deploying its global FAE team to locations "where we have target hyperscalers and OEM chip companies located" for Gen 5 VPD boot camps — Joe reads this as Vicor embedding field engineers at the Google/AMD sites ahead of ramp.
- **Joe's price/trade view:** VICR headed to "$400+ in short order and much higher longer term," with pullbacks to $250-260 expected; he frames the setup as front-running the eventual BofA/Goldman note that will name the customers ("dont wait for the analysts to tell you Vicor landed Google and AMD before buying"). Adjacent picks he names in replies: [[Rambus (RMBS)]] (avg below 100) and [[Atomera (ATOM)]] (speculative, buy in the $7s).
