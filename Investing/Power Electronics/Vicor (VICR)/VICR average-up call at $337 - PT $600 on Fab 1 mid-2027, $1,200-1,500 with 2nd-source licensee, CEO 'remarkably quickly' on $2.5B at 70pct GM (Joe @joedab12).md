# VICR average-up call at $337 — PT $600 on Fab 1 mid-2027, $1,200–1,500 with 2nd-source licensee, CEO "remarkably quickly" on $2.5B at 70% GM

A concentrated-holder position-sizing call from Joe (@joedab12), who says he added [[Vicor (VICR)]] premarket at $337 and argues "the best stock to buy is the one you already own." It pairs a concrete trade with the CEO catalyst quote and puts explicit price targets on the two rungs of the ramp — Fab 1 maturity, then a second-source licensee. It restates the raised $2.5B revenue / 70% gross-margin goal from the [[VICR pre-Q2-2026 bull rollup - $2.5B at 70pct GM goal, all-inclusive AI-OEM license, $1.5B Fab 1, GOOG plus AMD VPD ramp end-2027 (Joe @joedab12)|pre-Q2 bull rollup]] and adds a valuation cross-check against [[Monolithic Power (MPWR)]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Position-sizing call with an explicit fill: added premarket at $337, averaging up rather than down.** Joe frames averaging up as historically "a lot more successful than averaging down," and a replier notes it would be his third average-up on the name — a stylistic tell of a momentum-following concentrated-holder cohort.
- **Two-rung price target: $600 "just on Fab 1 at full maturity mid-2027," then $1,200–1,500 with a second-source licensee** manufacturing the demand that can't fit in Fab 1 — i.e. the low target assumes only the ~$1.5B Fab 1 capacity, the high target assumes the second-source deal referenced in the pre-Q2 rollup actually lands.
- **CEO catalyst quote on the raised $2.5B / 70%-GM goal**: Patrizio Vinciarelli said "I see scenarios where it can happen remarkably quickly" — the same "remarkably quickly" framing surfaced in the pre-Q2 rollup, here attributed directly to the CEO as the timeline accelerant.
- **Valuation cross-check vs [[Monolithic Power (MPWR)]]** (nearest competitor): even in an IDEAL next-year scenario MPWR does ~$4B revenue at 55% margins yet carries a ~$75B market cap — Joe's implied argument is that VICR's higher-margin (70%) model at $2.5B leaves "a LOT of meat left on the bone" relative to how the market caps MPWR.
- **Near-identical double-post exists** (a second tweet with the same content), so treat this as one signal, not two independent data points.
