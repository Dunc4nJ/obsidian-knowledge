# SCHMID (SHMD) FY2025 call transcript - 60pct AI-optical order mix to 70pct EOY, panel-level packaging 3-4x by 2030, glass substrates ramp 2027 via TSMC Intel Samsung supply chain, 2026 guide €100M rev EBITDA over 12pct €140M intake

## Key Takeaways

- **2025 was a barbell year and FY2026 guidance frames a step-function: rev >€100M, adj EBITDA >12pct, intake ~€140M.** H1 2025 was €16M rev (tariff-driven order pushouts) → H2 rebounded to €50M / 8-13pct EBITDA / 8.5pct EBIT. Order intake closed 2025 at >€90M, backlog €51M; Q1 2026 came in at €18.2M rev / €13.6M intake / €49M backlog (typically the seasonally weakest quarter — Chinese New Year + European holidays). CFO Arthur Schütz says 2026 is back-end loaded; €60-70M backlog expected to carry into 2027. Medium-term EBITDA target = back to 2023 levels (~20pct EBITDA / 14pct EBIT). Capital structure cleanup: $30M convertible ($18M outstanding), $30M standby equity line, ~€31M shareholder-debt-to-equity conversion; pro forma debt ~€23M.
- **60pct of trailing-12-month order intake is AI-infrastructure / optical-module related and management targets ~70pct by EOY 2026.** Driver: shift from wafer-level to panel-level packaging as compute packages (e.g., 120×120mm) outgrow wafer geometry. Customer-deployed panel sizes: 310×310mm, 510×515mm, 600×600mm — qualified at TSMC, Intel, Samsung. Panel-level packaging TAM modeled to grow **3-4x by 2030**. CSO Roland Rettenmaier explicitly cites NVIDIA + AMD ~18-month cadence of larger compute-unit body sizes as the demand pull. Lines up with the glass-substrate cycle map captured in [[PhotonCap May 2026 - 15-company glass substrate cycle map frames 2026 as pilot-qualification phase with 2027-2030 volume ramp across AI accelerators HBM4 interposer and CPO]] and the LPKF YTD performance write-up [[PhotonCap 2026-05 - LPKF up 255 pct YTD as LIDE TGV process becomes glass substrate chokepoint for EIC-to-CPO packaging shift]].
- **Glass-core substrate ramp explicitly framed as 2027 inflection — TRUMPF TGV co-engineering, Corning pilot in US, Intel driving supply chain adoption.** Rettenmaier: "We do see this tipping point — the conversion — happening here in 2027." Concrete customer signal: Corning glass-core substrate project in US already running in small volume. Intel "driving the whole supply chain — not just in the US — towards the adoption of glass core substrates." TSMC moving to 310×310mm panels and considering glass implementation; Samsung supply chain via [[Corning (GLW)]] (named as a US pilot partner) and SEMCO (Samsung Electro-Mechanics). [[LPKF Laser (LPK.DE)]] is the direct read-across — SCHMID is the **CMP / panel-level wet-process equipment** complement to LPKF's LIDE (laser-induced deep etching) for TGVs; both serve the same glass-substrate cycle. - **SHMD positions as "yield premium" supplier — explicitly winning against low-cost Asian competitors on scrap-cost math.** "SCHMID equipment is recognized as the most stable, best quality, and highest yield equipment.
