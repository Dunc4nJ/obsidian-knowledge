# SCHMID Group (SHMD)

SCHMID Group (SHMD) — Nasdaq-listed (de-SPAC April 2024); German equipment maker for PCB, IC substrate, and panel-level packaging. InfinityLine H+/V+ workhorses plus C+/L+ (CMP) / P+ (panel-level packaging) next-gen platforms. 60%+ of order intake AI-infrastructure / optical-module related (moving toward 70% by EOY 2026). Co-engineers TRUMPF on through-glass-via (TGV) process for glass-core substrates. Customer indirect exposure to TSMC, Intel, Samsung via Ibiden, AT&S, Unimicron, SEMCO supply chain.
