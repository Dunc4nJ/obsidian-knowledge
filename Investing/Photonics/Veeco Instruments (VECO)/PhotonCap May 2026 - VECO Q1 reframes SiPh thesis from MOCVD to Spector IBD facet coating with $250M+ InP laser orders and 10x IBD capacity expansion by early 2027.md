## Key Takeaways

- **The Q1 gross-margin miss has a single discrete explanation.** Revenue $158.3M (-5.4% YoY), Non-GAAP EPS $0.14 at the low end of guidance, Non-GAAP gross margin 36.2% below the guidance range. Per CFO John Kiernan, BIS notified [[Veeco Instruments (VECO)]] that one LSA system shipment to a specific China customer fab required an export license — that single event cost ~$8M in top-line and was the most direct variable behind the margin miss. Q2 gross margin is guided to 38-40% with recovery expected.

- **The real headline was a separately-issued press release the same day: $250M+ multi-product InP laser orders.** Product mix spans Spector IBD, Lumina MOCVD, and WaferEtch. In Veeco's own wording, "a substantial portion of the orders are for the Spector® IBD" system, used for laser facet coating. Customers are leading manufacturers of 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers for hyperscale data centers — i.e. [[Coherent (COHR)]] and [[Lumentum (LITE)]] are the consensus inference. Deliveries begin in 2026 and significantly accelerate in 2027 (shipments start Q3 2026, ramp Q1 2027 per Q&A).

- **PhotonCap's thesis revision: Spector IBD is the core lever, not MOCVD.** The prior [[PhotonCap April 2026 - Six III-V deposition equipment companies behind COHR and LITE (Aixtron Veeco IQE Riber Oxford Instruments AXT) carry the asymmetric edge of the AI optical cycle]] framed Veeco's SiPh exposure mainly through MOCVD (where Veeco is the second source behind [[Aixtron (AIXA.DE)]]'s ~70-90% dominant share of advanced photonic MOCVD). Q1 data clarifies that the larger surprise is **one process step downstream** — InP laser facet coating via Spector IBD, where Veeco is the **incumbent** not the second source. CEO Bill Miller explicitly committed in the Q&A to a **10x Spector IBD capacity expansion by early 2027, with potential for another 2x beyond that**.

- **Veeco's InP-laser exposure decomposes into three process steps, with the lever in step 3.** (1) **Epitaxy (MOCVD)** — Aixtron incumbent, Veeco second source. (2) **Wet processing (etch / clean / WaferEtch)** — Veeco holds a strong position with multiple leader customers. (3) **Laser facet coating (Spector IBD)** — Veeco is the incumbent; this is where the core lever sits. The Spector IBD process tool is **already the process tool of record across leading EUV mask-blank manufacturers** — the same ion-beam tooling platform now extends into InP laser facet coating for AI optical interconnect.

- **Spector IBD is a dot-com-era DWDM franchise reactivating for the AI cycle.** The IBD line was originally built for the DWDM/long-haul optical buildout in the late 1990s / early 2000s and was largely dormant through the long telecom underinvestment period. PhotonCap's framing: a previously dormant IBD line reactivates into the AI cycle, and the same ion-beam tool now spans two distinct AI-cycle markets (EUV mask blanks + InP laser facet coating), which is the new thesis line in the paid update.

- **Segment mix confirms the photonics framing.** Compound semi at 12% of Q1 2026 revenue maps directly onto the prior "photonics ~11%" framing — covers SiPh InP laser + GaN power + microLED + AR/VR + LEO satellite solar cells. CEO commentary directly named 2026 segment growth: **semiconductor mid-teens YoY, compound semi roughly 50% YoY, data storage 2x YoY**. The PR's highlight paragraph explicitly called out "particularly strong momentum in silicon photonics."

- **Guidance + revenue cadence imply heavy H2 weighting.** Q2 2026: $170-190M revenue, 38-40% GM, $0.20-0.32 EPS. FY 2026 (unchanged): $740-800M revenue, $1.50-1.85 EPS. Math: Q1 actual + Q2 midpoint = 1H ~$338M; FY midpoint $770M implies 2H ~$432M, **+28% versus 1H**. Revenue recognition is concentrated in H2 and bleeds into 2027.

- **The balance sheet shows pre-recognition build-up.** Q1 vs Dec 31, 2025: AR rose $111M → $151M (+$40M), customer deposits in contract liabilities grew $19M to $69M, inventories ticked from $275M → $282M (+$7M), cash + ST investments roughly flat at $383M (vs $390M). AR growth = timing of collection on revenue already recognized; customer deposits = prepayments not yet recognized. Combined with the $250M+ orders and H2 guidance weighting, the picture leans toward revenue recognition concentrated in H2 2026 and into 2027.

- **Hidden thesis line PhotonCap puts behind the paywall**: how the previously dormant DWDM-era IBD line reactivates into the AI cycle, the **single ion-beam tooling platform spanning two AI-cycle markets** (EUV mask-blanks + InP facet coating), and Spector IBD ramp scenarios A/B/C with monitoring points.
