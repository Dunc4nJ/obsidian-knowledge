- **P(operating margins peak H2 FY26 and decline through FY27): 70%**

### Leg 2 — MemoryAI CXL KV Cache (real but small, structurally headwinded)
- GTC March 16 2026 unveil; production-shipping; 1 Tier-1 bank customer + 5 new AI/HPC logos in Q2 FY26
- 4U Altus chassis, **11 TB DDR5** per IR PR (NOT 22 TB), CXL 2.0 expander cards, NVIDIA Dynamo framework compatible, ICE ClusterWare orchestration
- Today: ~$30-80M of $750M IM revenue (5-10%); even tripling = 15-25% by FY27
- BIG STRUCTURAL HEADWIND: **NVIDIA supports zero CXL on any accelerator** (Blackwell/B200/GB200/Rubin). NVLink-C2C + on-package HBM is the architectural counter-bet. **~90% of inference fleet doesn't speak CXL.**

- True CXL 3.0 fabric pooling needs AMD Venice (2026) + Intel Diamond Rapids (2026/27)
- **P(MemoryAI captures 5+ Tier-1 customers by Q4 FY26): 25%**

### Leg 3 — PMA / Photonic Memory Appliance (real call option, but discounted)
- Marvell guides Celestial revenue: $500M run-rate Q4 FY28, $1B Q4 FY29 — that's CELESTIAL revenue, not PENG share of it

- Performance numbers (3.66× throughput, 6.2 pJ/bit, 22.8× DLRM) are **CelestiSim-simulated, validated against H100/H200 microbenchmarks but NOT against actual PFA silicon**
- **Marvell has NEVER named PENG** in any earnings call (Q3 FY26 Dec 2025, Q4 FY26 March 2026), 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, press release, or Hot Chips 2025 CTO talk

- Addressable Western photonic memory pool effectively shrinks to **Microsoft + Meta + Oracle + tier-2 GPU clouds** (Google internal, AWS leans electrical via NeuronLink/Trainium)
- AWS endorsement (Dave Brown, MRVL acquisition release) is real but commits AWS to NOTHING specific to PENG or any chassis SKU
- **P(Celestial-PENG wins ≥30% Western photonic memory pool share by 2029): 35%**
