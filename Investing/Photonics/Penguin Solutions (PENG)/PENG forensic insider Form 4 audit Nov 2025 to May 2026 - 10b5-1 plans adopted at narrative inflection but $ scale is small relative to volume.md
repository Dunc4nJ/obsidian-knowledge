## 4. Historical comparison (SGH 2023-2024)

SGH-era 10b5-1 plans existed (e.g., one filer sold 2,527 sh on 7/24/24 under a plan adopted **4/17/2023**). Pacheco's 7/21/25 sale of $1.58M was the largest discretionary sale in 18 months pre-rebrand. The Nov-11-2025 cluster of plan adoptions is **not unprecedented** — SGH insiders have always used 10b5-1 — but the simultaneity (Gates + Kuykendall same day) and proximity to the AI narrative ramp is the new wrinkle. **Aggregate Nov-25 → today selling ($983K) is materially smaller than Pacheco's single July-25 ticket ($1.58M).**

- **Cleanest "diversification" (mechanical):** Kuykendall — 6% trim, 738-4,000 share clips, classic CLO RSU monetization.
- **Cleanest conviction-bear flag:** Gates — 29% holdings cut in 60 days, but he runs Optimized LED (segment with `-15% to +15%` FY26 growth guide vs Memory `+10-20%`); his exposure to the PMA upside is structurally limited so the read-through to the AI thesis is weak.
- **Cleanest bullish tell:** Adams holding ~993K shares (~$33M) post-retirement with **zero 10b5-1 plan disclosed** and no discretionary sales — the pivot's architect is fully aligned through the upside.

## Verdict

**The insider pattern at PENG is NEUTRAL, with confidence ~70%.** The Nov-11-2025 plan adoptions look ominous on the timeline but the dollars are trivial ($983K total, 0.015% of 6-mo $ volume), the two sellers are mid-tier officers (one running the *non*-AI segment), the CEO/CFO have zero discretionary sales, and former-CEO Adams is sitting on his full ~$33M stake with no 10b5-1 in place. The bearish read requires Adams or Shaikh to file a 10b5-1 — until then, this is RSU-mechanics-that-look-bigger-than-they-are. The single missing bullish signal is a Shaikh open-market buy; absence of one keeps confidence from going higher.
