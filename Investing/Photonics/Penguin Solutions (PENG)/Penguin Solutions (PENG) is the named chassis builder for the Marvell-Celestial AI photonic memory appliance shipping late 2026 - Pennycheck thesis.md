# PENG is the named chassis builder for the Marvell-Celestial photonic memory appliance — Pennycheck thesis

Independent-research breakdown by [@pennycheck](https://x.com/pennycheck) arguing that [[Penguin Solutions (PENG)]] is currently priced as a "free call option" on the [[Marvell Technology (MRVL)]]–Celestial AI photonic memory ramp. The thesis is built entirely from primary-source disclosures (PENG Q2 FY26 earnings call, PENG 10-Q, Marvell 8-K, Gazettabyte technical reporting, Celestial Hot Chips 2025 / arXiv 2507.14000).

## Key Takeaways

- **PENG is the disclosed system integrator for the Photonic Fabric Appliance (PFA)** — a 2RU rack-mount chassis holding 16 of Celestial AI's Photonic Fabric Modules, delivering 33 TB unified shared memory and 115 Tbps all-to-all switching across 16 XPUs. Roy Rubenstein at Gazettabyte explicitly named Penguin Computing as the chassis builder. PENG's branded variant is the **PMA (Photonic Memory Appliance)**, formerly OMA, with initial shipments targeted late 2026 / early 2027.
- **The Marvell-Celestial deal already monetized PENG's Series B and C1 stake** — PENG received approximately $32M cash plus [[Marvell Technology (MRVL)]] shares from the December 2, 2025 close, recognizing a $27.5M GAAP gain in Q2 FY26. PENG also retains pro-rata exposure to up to $2.25B in contingent earnout if Celestial cumulative revenue under Marvell exceeds $2B by Marvell FY29 — Marvell has guided $500M annualized run rate by Q4 FY28 and $1B by Q4 FY29, meaning the earnout is meaningfully in the money under Marvell's own guidance.
- **CEO Kash Shaikh referenced PMA, the Celestial investment, and the active Marvell partnership in three separate prepared statements plus the most consequential Q&A on the Q2 FY26 call** — the partnership status is now on-mic, not speculative. Loop Capital's Ananda Baruah asked directly whether photonics is required for CXL scale-up and Shaikh confirmed the photonic memory appliance "takes CXL to the next level."
- **The disclosed PFA economics are a step-function vs NVLink/NVSwitch** — approximately 6.2 pJ/bit on the photonic fabric versus approximately 62.5 pJ/bit on NVLink/NVSwitch (allpcb.com analysis of Celestial disclosures), with sub-250ns remote-memory latency via PCIe-attached PF-NIC and a TSMC 5nm switch ASIC composing each module (8 Tbps switch + 2× HBM3e controllers + 4× DDR5 controllers).
- **There is a parallel Korean sovereign-AI stack hidden in the same filings** — SK Telecom's $200M strategic preferred investment closed December 13, 2024 (auto-converts at $32.81 above 150% threshold), the CES 2025 three-way agreement with SK Hynix on memory innovation, and the Haein cluster (1,000+ Blackwell GPUs at SKT's Gasan DC) integrated and deployed by Penguin Solutions for Korea's "Proprietary AI Foundation Model" project. Q2 FY26 10-Q already shows $33.1M six-month related-party revenue from the SKT affiliate.
