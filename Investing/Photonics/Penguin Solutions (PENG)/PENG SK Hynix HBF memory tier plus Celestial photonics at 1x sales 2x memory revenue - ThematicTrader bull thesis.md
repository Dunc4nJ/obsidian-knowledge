# PENG SK Hynix HBF memory tier plus Celestial photonics at 1x sales / 2x memory revenue — ThematicTrader bull thesis

Two-tweet [@ThematicTrader](https://x.com/ThematicTrader) (9 Ventures) thread on [[Penguin Solutions (PENG)]] — the parent thread (May 4, 2026, 16:28 UTC) sets the high-level long case, and the same-day follow-up (20:17 UTC) adds the SK Hynix High Bandwidth Flash (HBF) angle after a community prompt from [@pennycheck](https://x.com/pennycheck). Companion to the existing PENG notes in this folder — the [[Penguin Solutions (PENG) is the named chassis builder for the Marvell-Celestial AI photonic memory appliance shipping late 2026 - Pennycheck thesis|Pennycheck PMA thesis]] (photonic appliance) and the [[PENG MemoryAI CXL KV Cache server ships with Tier-1 bank win, FY26 guide raised to 1.5-1.6B - Stockinger institutional brief|Stockinger MemoryAI brief]] (electrical CXL).

## Key Takeaways

- **HBF surfaces as a third memory tier in PENG's stack.** The Pennycheck note covers HBM3e + photonic; the Stockinger note covers DDR5 + electrical CXL; this thread adds [[SK Hynix (000660.KS)]]'s **High Bandwidth Flash (HBF)** — NAND-based silicon offering HBM-class bandwidth with NAND-class capacity. HBF targets a different KV-cache slice than DDR5 (much larger but slightly slower), giving PENG a tiered memory hierarchy: HBM3e for hot working set → DDR5/CXL for warm pool → HBF for cold pool / extended context. None of the existing two notes had this dimension.
- **The $1.6B MC = 1x sales / 2x memory-revenue framing is the cleanest valuation case yet.** ThematicTrader projects $800M FY26 Integrated Memory revenue at 75% YoY growth, against a $1.6B market cap. That's the rarely-stated implicit math: the market is pricing PENG as if its memory business deserves a 2x sales multiple AND everything else (Advanced Computing, LED, OriginAI, photonic optionality, SKT recurring) is worth zero. Either of those is debatable; both being true simultaneously is the mispricing.
- **75pct YoY Integrated Memory growth is consistent with Stockinger's Q2 print.** Stockinger reported +63pct YoY for Q2 FY26 alone with the segment at 50pct of revenue; ThematicTrader's full-year 75pct = ~$800M extrapolates that trajectory cleanly. The two numbers triangulate rather than contradict.
- **Balance sheet detail not surfaced in the prior notes**: positive net cash position after accounting for debt; all debt is at ~2pct interest, maturing 2029-2030. This materially de-risks the platform-pivot narrative — there's no refinancing wall and no dilution pressure even if MemoryAI revenue ramps slower than guided. Active buyback also disclosed.
- **The @FuruB5134 reply is the bear case in one sentence — preserve it.** *"Penguin sits at the integration layer... value capture is more upstream (SKH + SNDK & MRVL/Celestial)... one might overestimate margin expansion."* This is the same Marvell-vertical-integration risk flagged in the Stockinger Synthesis but stated more sharply: the integrator-layer margin compression risk applies regardless of whether PMA, MemoryAI, or HBF is the product. ThematicTrader's reply ("first to market... proprietary technology behind that") is unsubstantiated — the proprietary-content question is the single most important diligence item across all three notes in this folder.
