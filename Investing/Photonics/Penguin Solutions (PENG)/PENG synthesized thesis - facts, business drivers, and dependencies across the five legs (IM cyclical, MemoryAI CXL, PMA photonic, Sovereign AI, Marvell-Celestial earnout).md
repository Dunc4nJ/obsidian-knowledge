## What the business is

PENG (NASDAQ: PENG, formerly SMART Global Holdings / SGH, rebranded 2024) is a US-listed AI infrastructure company organized in three reporting segments:

- **Integrated Memory** (~50% of Q2 FY26 net sales) — DRAM and flash memory modules under the SMART Modular brand, plus emerging CXL-based MemoryAI products. Customers include enterprise, telco, networking, and AI/HPC. Revenue is largely tied to DDR4/DDR5 module ASPs.
- **Advanced Computing** (~34% of Q2 FY26 net sales) — Penguin Computing HPC integrator business. Customers include hyperscalers (Meta RSC 2022 reference), DOE national labs (Sandia/LLNL/LANL tri-lab CTS-1, &gt;22 petaflops since ~2016), sovereign AI (SK Telecom Haein cluster), and one disclosed Tier-1 financial institution. Revenue is project-based and lumpy.
- **Optimized LED** (~16% of Q2 FY26 net sales) — Cree LED business acquired 2021. Stable, declining; described by management as a "cash cow" funding R&D.

Software layer: ICE ClusterWare (formerly Scyld ClusterWare, Scyld Software acquired 2003, descended from Beowulf cluster orchestration). Reference architecture branded "OriginAI."

## Five legs of the thesis

### Leg 1 — Integrated Memory cyclical ramp

**What it is**: Q2 FY26 +63% YoY ($172M, 50% of revenue), FY26 guide +65-75% YoY raised at the April 1 print.

**Olmstead verbatim on the driver**: *"the increase that we're seeing in the second half, that's majority pricing but demand is also very strong across telco, networking, AI-driven demand is just very strong. In fact, to get to the high end of that outlook really just refers to our ability to secure materials, which is really the only inhibitor we see right now to raising that outlook."*

**Decomposition** (per the wave-2 cycle agent's analysis of "majority pricing"): ~55-60 pts pricing / ~10-15 pts unit & mix / remainder CXL nascent. >70% of the upside is mechanically tied to DDR5 ASPs.

**What this leg depends on**:
- DDR5 contract ASP trajectory (currently ~2.5-3× the late-2024 trough; March 2026 retail saw first MoM decline -7.2% after 8 up-months)
- HBM-vs-commodity-DRAM wafer allocation (HBM consumes ~3× wafer capacity per GB vs DDR5; spillover from HBM lines once HBM additions slow)
- Capacity adds: Micron Idaho ID1 first wafer 2H 2027, SK Hynix M15X mass prod Nov 2026, Samsung Pyeongtaek P4 full ops end-2026
- Cycle peak forecasts: Counterpoint = late 2027; TrendForce = mid-2026 momentum peak; Morgan Stanley flipped late-cycle Nov 2025; SemiAnalysis = late 2027/2028; JPM = mid-2026 pricing improvements; Micron mgmt = three culprits won't ease until 2027

**Historical analog**: SGH FY22 → FY23 revenue $1.74B → $1.17B (-33%); Q4 FY23 net sales -27.7% YoY, stock -23.6% on print; SGH peaked $36.50 Jan 2022, -75% by late 2022 in post-COVID memory crash. PENG currently has $322M strategic inventory (+61% YoY) built at peak prices.

### Leg 2 — MemoryAI CXL KV Cache server

**What it is**: 4U Altus chassis, **11 TB DDR5 ceiling per IR press release** (Stockinger's "22 TB" was overstatement), CXL 2.0 expander cards, NVIDIA Dynamo framework compatible, ICE ClusterWare orchestration. Production-shipping since GTC March 16 2026.

**Disclosed customers**: One unnamed Tier-1 financial institution for "real-time AI parsing of massive datasets" + one "substantial CXL card order" from a generative-AI customer. 5 new AI/HPC logos in Q2 FY26.

**Estimated current contribution**: ~$30-80M of $750M IM revenue (5-10% of segment).

**What this leg depends on**:
- NVIDIA's stance on CXL — currently Blackwell/B200/GB200/Rubin support **zero CXL**; NVLink-C2C + on-package HBM is the architectural counter-bet; ~90% of inference fleet doesn't speak CXL
- AMD Venice (2026) and Intel Diamond Rapids (2026/27) for true CXL 3.0 fabric pooling
- CXL controller ecosystem timing — Astera Labs Leo is CXL 2.0 (3.0 sampling), Microchip SMC, Samsung CMM-D 2.0 (3.1 targeted year-end 2026), SK Hynix CMM-DDR5
- Microsoft Azure M-series CXL preview (the only named hyperscaler CXL deployment, production volumes 2H 2026) as the broader-ecosystem demand validator
- New customer additions beyond the single Tier-1 bank
- KV cache memory pressure from frontier models (Llama 3.1 70B with GQA at 128K context = ~40 GB KV cache per request; without GQA = 320 GB; frontier models at 1M-2M context push this 10-20× higher)

### Leg 3 — PMA / Photonic Memory Appliance

**What it is**: Branded variant of the Celestial AI Photonic Fabric Appliance (PFA). 2RU chassis housing 16 Photonic Fabric Modules (PFMs). Each PFM = TSMC 5nm ASIC (8 Tbps switch + 2× HBM3e controllers + 4× DDR5 controllers) + 2× HBM3e stacks + photonic interposer + fiber array unit, in 2.5D package.

**Disclosed specs**: 33 TB unified shared memory, 115 Tbps all-to-all switching across 16 XPUs, 7.2 Tbps optical port bandwidth per module per direction, sub-250ns remote-memory latency via PCIe-attached PF-NIC, ~6.2 pJ/bit photonic transceivers (vs ~62.5 pJ/bit on NVLink/NVSwitch per allpcb.com analysis).

**Performance claims**: 3.66× throughput / 1.40× latency on 405B LLM inference vs DGX-H100; 7.04× throughput on 1T LLM; 22.8× DLRM perf vs NVLink; 60-90% energy savings on collective ops; 49.7% MFU vs 13.6% for DGX-H100 on 128-4096 workload at 405B. **All numbers are CelestiSim simulation results, validated against H100/H200 microbenchmarks but NOT against actual PFA silicon** (paper Section 9 caveat).

**Marvell official roadmap** (per acquisition release + Hot Chips 2025 + Q3/Q4 FY26 calls):
- H1 2026: PFA modules + NICs sample to a hyperscaler
- H2 2026: Photonic Fabric chiplet integrated with xPUs/switches; "Penguin memory chassis is also expected in 2026" (Gazettabyte attribution, not Marvell's words)
- H1 2027: First commercial scale-up product to hyperscaler customer
- 2H FY28 (calendar 2027): meaningful revenue contributions begin
- Q4 FY28: $500M annualized run-rate
- Q4 FY29: $1B annualized run-rate (these are CELESTIAL revenue figures, not PENG share)

**Estimated PENG PMA revenue contribution** (per wave-2 analysis): FY27 $0-15M, FY28 $25-75M, FY29 $75-200M.

**What this leg depends on**:
- Marvell publicly naming PENG as the chassis integrator (no such disclosure exists in any Marvell press release, earnings call, 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, or CTO conference talk to date)
- Marvell's chassis-OEM pattern (Teralynx 10 names Celestica + Wistron Neweb; Tomahawk uses Edgecore + Quanta + Wiwynn + Foxconn + Inventec) — multi-vendor by design with no precedent for sole-sourcing
- Hyperscaler buy-vs-build decisions: Google Apollo OCS already deployed (9,216 TPUs interconnected as 1.77 PB HBM pool — Google has solved its own photonic memory problem in-house); AWS Trainium3/4 (late 2026/early 2027) sticking with NeuronLink/NeuronSwitch electrical, no public photonic memory commitment; Meta MTIA uses Broadcom CPO for scale-out networking, no public photonic memory pool strategy
- Western photonic memory pool effective addressable market: Microsoft + Meta + Oracle + tier-2 GPU clouds (CoreWeave, Lambda, Crusoe, Nebius), with Google internal and AWS leaning electrical
- Marvell hitting Celestial revenue ramp targets ($500M Q4 FY28, $1B Q4 FY29)
- Silicon validation of CelestiSim performance numbers
- Lightmatter Passage L200 (the credible US-side photonic competitor, $4.4B post-money, "last private round" CFO hire signals 2026-2027 IPO window) targets a different lane — scale-up XPU-to-XPU interconnect, not memory pool — but the categories may converge by 2029
- Ayar Labs (NVIDIA + AMD + MediaTek + Intel strategic investors, $500M Series E March 2026) is complementary not competitive — in-package optical I/O, not external memory fabric

### Leg 5 — Marvell-Celestial earnout

**What it is**: PENG was an early investor in Celestial AI (Series B June 2023, Series C1 March 2025). When Marvell acquired Celestial (announced Dec 2 2025, closed Feb 2 2026, $3.25B upfront + up to $2.25B contingent earnout), PENG received approximately $32M proceeds ($10.5M cash + 281,834 Marvell shares fair-valued at $22.2M) and recognized a $27.5M GAAP gain in Q2 FY26. PENG retains pro-rata exposure to the contingent earnout.

**Earnout structure**: Up to $2.25B contingent (~27.2M Marvell shares) for selling shareholders if Celestial cumulative revenue exceeds $2B by end Marvell FY29. First third unlocks at $500M cumulative revenue.

**PENG's pro-rata stake size is undisclosed.** Back-of-envelope: $32M from $5.5B headline deal implies low single-digit % stake, suggesting realizable PENG earnout share of $50-150M if fully triggered.

**What this leg depends on**:
- Marvell hitting Celestial revenue trajectory ($500M Q4 FY28, $1B Q4 FY29)
- The $2B cumulative threshold being reached by end Marvell FY29
- ASC 815 scope exception accounting — gains recognized only when realizable
