# Crux Capital pitches Jenoptik (JEN.DE) as ~EUR2B European semi-optics play

## Key Takeaways

- **Dual end-market exposure rare in this name set.** Jenoptik supplies micro-optics into lithography/inspection systems AND microlens arrays into optical transceivers — one foot in WFE recovery, one foot in AI-datacenter optical buildout. Structurally close to the European semi-equipment names that [[Sancet 2026 - Goldman optical cheat sheet omits substrate epi equipment and laser layers (IQE Soitec Tower Aixtron AEHR LPKF SIVE)]] flagged as missing from Goldman's cheat sheet — neighbors [[Aixtron (AIXA.DE)]], [[LPKF Laser (LPK.DE)]], [[Aehr Test Systems (AEHR)]] sit in adjacent equipment niches but none span both lithography optics and transceiver micro-optics the way JEN does.
- **Trough-cycle financials suggest operating leverage is intact.** ~€1B revenue at ~2.3x EV/Sales (~€2.3B EV); semi segment alone runs €114M EBITDA at 25%+ margin; FCF grew ~50% in a weak year. The combination of margin durability and FCF growth at the bottom of the cycle is the setup for a cyclical entering recovery — guidance of "back to profitable growth in 2026" supports it.
- **Dresden fab is a 2026 capex signal, not a delivered catalyst.** Biggest-ever fab just opened — management is positioning ahead of the AI/memory capex ramp rather than waiting. Validates the thesis but also implies near-term FCF gets reinvested, so the trade is on cycle-end EPS rather than imminent cash return.
- **Thesis is genuinely multi-vector — read as diversification, not as conviction.** It needs SOME of {memory capex acceleration, WFE recovery, litho/inspection demand, AI optics scaling} to play out, not all. Cleaner than single-point-of-failure pure-plays on transceivers ([[Lumentum (LITE)]]) or substrates ([[IQE (IQE.L)]]); also less explosive upside if any one of those vectors becomes the singular winner.
