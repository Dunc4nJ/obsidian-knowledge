# Oxford Instruments (OXIG.L)

Oxford Instruments (LSE: OXIG) — back-end plasma processing equipment for InP/GaAs photonic semis. OIPT product family covers ICP-RIE waveguide etching (PlasmaPro 100 Cobra), ICPCVD passivation, ALD dielectrics (OpAL/FlexAL), and IBE laser-facet mirror coating (Ionfab). Ramping into Coherent's Sherman and Järfälla 6-inch InP fabs.
