## Key Takeaways

- Enablence's moat is **owning the fab** — they design AND manufacture Planar Lightwave Circuits (PLCs) on silicon at their own Fremont, CA facility. In an industry where most photonics players outsource wafer processing, in-house capacity is "incredibly expensive and time-consuming to replicate" and gives them prototype-to-production speed near Silicon Valley customers. This is the compressed-floor part of the asymmetric setup.

- The thesis is fundamentally a **Sivers ([[Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE.ST)]]) supply-chain bet**. Sivers makes the laser light source; ENA makes the Star Couplers and splitters that route light inside the package. CPO at scale needs both, so ENA's fortunes track Sivers' design wins. If Sivers ramps, ENA ramps with it — a coupled exposure rather than an independent thesis.

- The setup is a **business-model transition**: ENA is moving from "does projects" (lumpy, custom one-off engineering revenue) to "manufactures products" (repeatable wafer throughput). Revenue is guided to nearly double FY26 ($5.95M → $12M ± $0.5M), and the make-or-break catalyst is whether the cash burn flips before the AI optical cycle peaks.

- **Sancet hasn't entered yet** despite high conviction. The block is "momentum and visibility" — he's waiting for Serenity (@aleabitoreddit) validation as the catalyst that could re-rate the stock. This is a flag that the bull case is real but illiquid/under-followed; the entry is psychological, not analytical.

- **QT context** ([[Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI)]] / [[Lumentum (LITE)]] / [[Coherent (COHR)]] / [[AXT (AXTI)]] / Innolight): Serenity called those names "before the supercycle" and is now flagging this year's basket. ENA is positioned as a higher-beta, smaller-cap derivative of the same AI optical thesis that drove the 2025 winners. Innolight (300308.SZ) is Chinese A-share — not retail-tradeable, no folder.
