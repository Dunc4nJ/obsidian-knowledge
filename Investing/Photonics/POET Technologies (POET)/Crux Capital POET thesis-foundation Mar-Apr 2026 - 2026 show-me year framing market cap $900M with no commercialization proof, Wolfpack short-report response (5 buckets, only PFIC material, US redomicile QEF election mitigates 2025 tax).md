# Crux Capital POET thesis-foundation: Mar-Apr 2026

This note consolidates two Crux Capital Group commentary posts on [[POET Technologies (POET)]] from spring 2026:

- **2026-03-26 — Pre-earnings check-in** ([source](https://cruxcapitalgroup.substack.com/p/poet-tech-update)): valuation/conviction recalibration ~7 months after Crux's initial entry; lays out the "2026 is still the show-me year" framing at a ~$900M market cap.

## Show-me-year framing (per Crux 2026-03-26)

### Valuation arc
- Crux initial entry: ~$400M market cap, ~$6 share price (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- ~7 months later: ~$900M market cap — roughly doubled (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Framework when first bought: "Exciting story. Really cool tech. Solid partnerships. Still needs to prove it can actually commercialize." That framework is essentially unchanged seven months in (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- BUT stakes are higher: dilution-adjusted upside is narrower, downside risk is more real at the higher valuation (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Crux self-discipline note: POET is one of only **2 positions** in Crux's book currently in the red (modestly, due to dilution) despite the run-up, because Crux entered very early (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Framed as a story that still needs to prove itself, not one whose proof has arrived; allocated accordingly; building further only on incremental proof (per Crux 2026-03-26).

### Core question (unchanged across both posts)
- Can [[POET Technologies (POET)]] move from "very interesting photonics company" to a real production business with recurring revenue? "That is still the whole game" (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Restated in the Wolfpack response: "Can POET convert its cash, partnerships, product work, and industry positioning into real commercial traction? That is still the question that matters most" (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Management voice shift
- CRO has become noticeably more public and operator-like in messaging (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Tone now: finishing qualification, getting production lines ready, shipping units, converting engagements into committed development programs, building toward recurring revenue (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- That tone shift is what the market needs from a name labeled a "science project" — management acting like operators, not just engineers (per Crux 2026-03-26).

### 2026 = show-me year, with more structure
- CRO has said POET has a "healthy number of units scheduled to ship over the next 3 to 4 quarters" (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Near-term focus: finishing qualification + getting production lines ready (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Timeline is becoming tangible rather than abstract — but risk is not removed, just sharpened (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- "If we exit 2026 and nothing has changed here, we may need to revisit the thesis" (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Restated in dilution bucket: "2026 is a big line in the sand for me here" (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Cash position
- ~$400M cash balance (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Crux read: prep for the commercial inflection; allocation sized for execution risk (per Crux 2026-03-26).

### Pre-earnings expectations (Mar 26 context)
- POET slated to report earnings soon — some sources said "today after hours," most pointed to March 30 or 31 (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Crux not expecting much signal from numbers themselves; still largely pre-revenue (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Cash balance disclosure would be nice; direct CEO commentary on a call would matter most; no call appeared scheduled as of writing (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Focus is on future possibility, not current EPS (per Crux 2026-03-26).

### Partnership breadth ≠ revenue conversion
- Named partners: Lessengers, QCi, LITEON, ADTRAN, and the older Celestial AI relationship (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- Broader interest is real and signals platform credibility (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- BUT partnership announcements are not the same as conversion into development programs / production orders / recurring shipments / quarter-over-quarter revenue (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- "This company does not have a problem attracting interest. The [thing] I still need to see as an investor is conversion" (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- That conversion is "the bridge POET still has to cross" (per Crux 2026-03-26).

### MRVL-Celestial angle (interesting, not yet confirmed exposure)

- Raises the strategic value of the prior POET-Celestial relationship; key piece of current bull case (per Crux 2026-03-26).

## Wolfpack response by bucket (per Crux 2026-04-14)

Crux divides the Wolfpack Research short report into 5 buckets and ranks each by what's actually material. Short-report URL: <https://www.wolfpackresearch.com/items/we-believe-poet-is-a-obvious-stock-promote%2C-has-created-an-irs-nightmare%3A-us-holders-have-until-april-15th-to-act> — titled "we believe POET is obvious stock promote, has created an IRS nightmare: US holders have until April 15 to act."

Framing context: the Wolfpack author is a generalist, not an industry expert in optics; their shorts cover a vast array of industries (per Crux 2026-04-14). "This is not an industry that is very easy to understand if you do not spend time here often" (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Bucket 1 — PFIC / tax / IRS (the ONLY genuinely material bucket)
- The one bucket Crux actually thinks investors should examine for themselves (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- POET's **2025 20-F** says the company "believed" it was not a PFIC for the year ended Dec 31 2024 and did not expect to be classified as a PFIC for the year ending Dec 31 2025 (per Crux 2026-04-14, quoting 20-F).
- Same 20-F: PFIC status can only be determined at end of each taxable year, depends on composition of income, value/nature of assets, market capitalization; no assurance company will not be classified as a PFIC in current or any future tax year (per Crux 2026-04-14, quoting 20-F).
- **January 2026 prospectus supplement** softened the language: "had not made any determination regarding its PFIC status for the year ended December 31 2025, the current taxable year, or any other year" (per Crux 2026-04-14, quoting supplement).
- That filing: PFIC status can only be determined after year-end; no assurance company will not be a PFIC for 2025 or future years; neither company nor its U.S. counsel expresses any opinion regarding its PFIC status or expectations (per Crux 2026-04-14, quoting supplement).
- Filing also states a U.S. holder of a PFIC is required to file **IRS Form 8621** and should consult a tax advisor (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Crux personally: not a thesis-breaker; long-term holders should still talk to a tax pro (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Bucket 1 — POET's structural response (the EDIT at the top of the post)
- POET will provide the information U.S. shareholders need to make a **QEF election for 2025** (per Crux 2026-04-14, quoting POET announcement).
- If a U.S. holder timely makes that election, it should **mitigate certain adverse U.S. federal income tax consequences** that could otherwise result from POET's PFIC status for 2025 (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- POET reported a **net loss for 2025** — a timely QEF election for that year is **not expected to create current income inclusions for 2025** (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Expected to result in **no negative U.S. federal income tax consequences** for U.S. shareholders who continued to hold their shares (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Crux framing: "turns the narrative from 'IRS nightmare' into 'filing/compliance fix available, with limited near-term tax damage if handled properly'" (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- POET's board has **approved the intention to redomicile to the U.S.**, which would eliminate the possibility of being classified as a PFIC in future years once it happens (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- If shareholder approval is required, the matter is expected to go to the **June 26 2026 meeting** (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Crux: "this is a rare case where a short report appears to have prompted a structural fix" — kills PFIC overhang prospectively once redomicile completes (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Bucket 2 — Stock promotion / paid influencers
- Pre-existing bear case Crux has discussed before (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- "There is a difference between promotion and falsehood" (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- If a company is paying for visibility, trying to get in front of more investors, trying to improve access to capital markets — Crux does not automatically have an issue with that; small companies do this all the time (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- What would matter: whether information being pushed is false, whether capital being raised is being misused, whether the business underneath is not moving forward (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Same for paid research coverage — when coverage is sponsored, it should be read with that in mind; useful but not independent in the same way fully third-party coverage would be (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- "I do not think 'they tried to get visibility' is some devastating bear case on its own" (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Bucket 3 — Dilution / financing / cash raises
- Pre-existing bear case (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Crux would prefer less dilution; every shareholder would (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Real question: what is the money being used for? If used to move business forward — production, development, manufacturing readiness, customer programs, strategic strengthening — Crux can live with dilution (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- If company keeps raising and there is no commercial progress to match it, Crux would start to care a lot more (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- "2026 is a big line in the sand for me here" (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- "The better question is whether management turns that cash into actual commercial progress" (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Bucket 4 — Serial pivots / missed promises / moving goalposts
- "I do not care much about what POET was trying to do ten years ago" (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Cares about what POET is trying to do now, why the opportunity is attractive now, whether it can execute now (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- If a company sees a better application for its technology and pivots toward it, Crux does not view that as bearish — would rather see management move toward the biggest opportunity than stay stuck chasing something clearly less important (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- "I own it because of the current optical interposer and AI connectivity opportunity. If that opportunity changed, I would reassess" (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Bucket is mostly backward-looking for Crux; focused on execution from here (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Bucket 5 — Value chain / hyperscalers / what POET actually is
- Crux finds this "honestly one of the stranger parts of the report" (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- POET is a component and platform company. Not "selling directly to Nvidia" or dropping finished systems straight into hyperscalers (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- "That is obvious to anyone who has spent real time understanding the stock" (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- "That is not some hidden bear case. That is just describing where the company sits in the stack" (per Crux 2026-04-14).

### Wolfpack response — overall takeaway
- "None of these claims are novel except PFIC" (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Rest comes down to questions already known to matter: promotion, dilution, commercialization, execution, where POET fits in the value chain (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Crux methodology useful as a template: separate claims into buckets, rank by materiality, distinguish recycled bear points from genuinely new information (per Crux 2026-04-14).

## Crux's methodology and meta-points (across both posts)

- Read short reports with a clear head, separate the claims out, deal with them one by one (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Author has a financial incentive for stock to go down, just like longs have incentive for stock to go up (per Crux 2026-04-14).
- Discipline applied to long side too: Crux owns POET despite valuation expansion *without* yet a corresponding step-up in execution proof — so he sizes accordingly and waits for confirmation rather than chasing partner-announcement headlines (per Crux 2026-03-26).
- The "show-me year" framing is the bridge between the two posts: 2026 is when the conversion has to happen, and the Wolfpack PFIC fix (April announcement) removed the structural tail risk that could have derailed the year even if execution went well (per Crux 2026-03-26 + 2026-04-14).
