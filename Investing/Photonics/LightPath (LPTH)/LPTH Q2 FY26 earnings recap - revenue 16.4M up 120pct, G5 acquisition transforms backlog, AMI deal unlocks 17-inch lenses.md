# LPTH Q2 FY26 earnings recap - revenue $16.4M up 120%, G5 acquisition transforms backlog, AMI deal unlocks 17-inch lenses

## Key takeaways

- [[LightPath (LPTH)]] Q2 FY26 (quarter ending Dec 31 2025) revenue came in at **$16.4M, +120% YoY**, beating author's $15.1M model. Through 7.5 months of fiscal 2026 the company has booked $31.5M — already 85% of all of fiscal 2025 ($37.2M). Author now models ~$82M for full fiscal 2026 vs analyst consensus implying ~$15.3M Q3 / $16.3M Q4 (i.e. analysts still pricing a slowdown that the print contradicts).

- **Gross margin 36.6%** — CFO Al had guided "mid-30s in a few quarters towards year end"; arrived this quarter. Author expects margins to hover mid-30s in coming quarters. The $9.4M reported loss is a $7.6M G5 acquisition charge stacked on a $1.8M operating loss — the algo readers anchored on the headline number.

- **Europe (incl Israel) overtook US as the top region**. A year ago US was 69% of sales / Europe 15%. Now US is 49% / Europe is the leader. This is the *un-narrated* part of the story — sell-side and most retail focus exclusively on US defense contracts, yet European sales are quietly the biggest line item.

- **G5 acquisition is "the acquisition of the year for 2025"** — backlog has grown >1000% under LPTH ownership ($15M revenue at acquisition → $80M of current/future business under LPTH). The economic ratchet: better G5 performance = higher near-term earn-out costs (2026-2027) → then it's all LPTH upside on 10-year-floor military programs. Two of G5's cameras already redesigned with Black Diamond glass; all of them by fall 2026 post-AMI.

- **AMI acquisition extends lens-size capability from 6 inches (LPTH legacy) → 10 inches (G5) → 17 inches with "certain techniques" (AMI)** — directly enabling space and long-range imaging. Adds Texas as a second production location (vs hurricane-exposed Florida HQ) and locks down specialized talent. CEO Sam Rubin's plan to "make larger Black Diamond lenses for long-range imaging, space, all the golden dome missile detection" is now executed.

- **[[Lockheed Martin (LMT)]] relationship is deeper than the market models** — not a one-program shotgun marriage replacing Stinger, but multiple programs across the relationship. LPTH is "a gnat on an elephant's ass" for LMT — immaterial spend for LMT, transformational for LPTH. Author also reads CEO Sam as biting his tongue on NGSRI; expects both LMT and [[RTX (RTX)]] to share the award. A $50-$100M NGSRI win = literally $10-$20 per share material when full-rate production (FRIP) hits late-2027/early-2028.

- **Backlog grew $86M → $97.8M despite drawing down $16.4M into realized revenue this quarter**. Programs with $10M+ potential went from 7 → 9 in 90 days. Author's framing: even winning ~half of those at LPTH's price-to-sales would add ~$10/share.

- **$60M war chest, not a capacity build-out** — author repeats CFO's prior-quarter language that capacity is cheap to expand internally. The raise (which had >$250M demand) is positioned for **more M&A** in this 3-year window where competitors are locked into germanium-supply constraints LPTH doesn't have. Author calls AMI a "no-brainer" template: bought at 2-3x sales using shares trading at 10x sales (Sam's AI World, in replies).

## Context

[[LightPath (LPTH)]] is an Orlando-based precision-optics manufacturer whose Black Diamond chalcogenide-glass lens technology is displacing germanium in IR thermal imaging — a category-shift unfolding right as the US/EU defense complex ramps surveillance, missile-defense and CUAS programs. The Q2 FY26 print is the second of what author expects to be three consecutive 100%+ YoY revenue quarters as the company exits the long sleepy-component-supplier era CEO Sam Rubin inherited.

The dual-acquisition framing matters because it isolates the business model from optics-as-a-commodity. **G5 Infrared** (acquired 2025) brings camera-system contracts and Lockheed Martin / defense-prime relationships; LPTH retrofits G5's cameras with Black Diamond optics, drives a >1000% backlog expansion under common ownership, and books the resulting program flow. **AMI** (Amorphous Materials Inc, just-closed) brings the 17-inch lens capability needed for long-range surveillance and space-imaging payloads (SDA tracking satellites, LEO constellations), plus a Texas production site that de-risks the Florida-hurricane-corridor concentration. Together the two deals reposition LPTH from a sub-scale component maker into the optics layer of multi-program defense primes.

The Europe surge and [[Lockheed Martin (LMT)]] depth are the parts of the thesis sell-side hasn't priced. Europe (incl Israel, Latvia) overtaking US is unusual for a small-cap US-defense optics name and reflects the breadth of Black Diamond adoption beyond the LPTH-as-Stinger-replacement headline. The LMT relationship is described as multi-program rather than single-contract — meaning LPTH could be embedded in NGSRI (where author expects [[RTX (RTX)]] to share the award) alongside Stinger replacement, CUAS, and several $10M+ programs (count up from 7 → 9 in 90 days). The $60M war chest sits as M&A dry powder, not capacity capex — author's read of management is that "the next FLIR" consolidation play is the actual plan.
