# LightPath (LPTH)

Precision lens and infrared optics manufacturer based in Orlando FL. Black Diamond chalcogenide-glass lens technology displaces germanium in IR thermal imaging. G5 Infrared and Amorphous Materials Inc (AMI) acquisitions extended lens-size capability up to 17 inches for long-range surveillance and space-imaging applications. Customers include Lockheed Martin (NGSRI), Motorola Solutions (top customer), DHS (border surveillance) and various defense and aerospace primes.
