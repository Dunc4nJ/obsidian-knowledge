# LPTH Q3 FY26 earnings recap - backlog 110M up 31M QoQ, G5 acquisition drives organic growth, NGSRI delayed by DHS shutdown

## Key takeaways

- **Backlog hit $110.5M, up $31.8M QoQ in a single quarter** — implies a $120M+ annualized run rate for a company that did under $40M in FY25 revenue. G5-related backlog alone went from $5.6M at acquisition to >$80M a year later. AMI contributed just $1.1M of the $31.8M increase, so the build is overwhelmingly organic plus G5 legacy. Q3 revenue printed $19.1M (109% YoY per @sean_krajewski's reply).
- **G5 is the "acquisition of the year" because the structure compounds upside** — the $3.4M Q3 charge to "Change in fair value of acquisition earnout liabilities" is Sam paying former G5 owners more *because* G5 is outperforming; back that out and the operating loss shrinks from -$4.2M to -$0.8M. A side-letter agreement signed April 16 2026 accelerated and locked in the year-two earnout at $9M ($6.3M cash, $2.7M [[LightPath (LPTH)]] stock) payable between Jan 1-15, 2027, giving sellers tax certainty and ending the cash drag in early 2027.
- **NGSRI is "in the bag" but the timing is later than the bull case** — author thinks the [[Lockheed Martin (LMT)]] / [[RTX (RTX)]] competition is decided in LPTH's favor, but Low Rate Initial Production runs ~3/4 of a year minimum before Full Rate, so even a July 2026 win wouldn't be P&L-material until 2H 2027. He hopes the announcement triggers a [[One Stop Systems (OSS)]]-type stock pop.
- **Share dilution surprised to the upside (negatively)** — count jumped from <58M to 62.8M, already nearing the author's end-of-2027 model (65M). Driver is North Run converting Series G preferred (7,610 preferred shares converted to 3,539,379 commons at a 465.12-to-1 ratio). 2030 target rests on 75M-share assumption that now looks tight.
- **[[Motorola Solutions (MSI)]] revealed as top customer**, a new disclosure for the author. Camera/assembly conversions to Black Diamond complete by fall 2026 — until then the Black Diamond optics story (which is driving the >100% growth) is still in early innings.
- **Two negatives, both manageable**: (1) DHS shutdown delays the border-surveillance contract revenue by a quarter or two — possibly longer given the administration; next quarter's revenue target gets cut from $24.3M down to ~$20M. (2) Less "bam pow" on space vs the prior call — space remains a 2028+ story, not 2026 H2. Covered in the prior quarter recap: see [[LPTH Q2 FY26 earnings recap - revenue 16.4M up 120pct, G5 acquisition transforms backlog, AMI deal unlocks 17-inch lenses]].
- **Author's 2030 PT math**: $300M revenue × 11x P/S ÷ 75M shares ≈ $44 PPS. Forward P/S on a $100M FY27 placeholder is 7.2x — "dirt cheap for a company growing 100%". Even reverting to trailing 11.5x P/S with mild dilution (66M shares) gets a 1-year PPS of ~$17.40 vs $11.50 spot.

## Context

[[LightPath (LPTH)]] is a precision-lens and infrared-optics manufacturer based in Orlando FL whose Black Diamond chalcogenide-glass lens technology is displacing germanium in IR thermal imaging — a defense-driven precision-optics thesis. Two transformative acquisitions reshape the company: **G5 Infrared** (closed 2025) brought long-range surveillance optics IP and a customer book that exploded from $5.6M to $80M+ backlog inside 12 months, and **Amorphous Materials Inc (AMI)** (more recent) unlocks 17-inch lens production for long-range surveillance and space-imaging applications. The earnout structure on G5 — payouts scale with G5's performance — means a fat charge to "Change in fair value of acquisition earnout liabilities" is *bullish*, not bearish, because it reflects G5 overshooting plan.

Customer concentration centers on defense and surveillance primes: [[Lockheed Martin (LMT)]] is the prime on NGSRI (Next Generation Squad Recognition / surveillance optic), with [[RTX (RTX)]] as the likely co-winner if it splits; [[Motorola Solutions (MSI)]] was revealed in this call as the top single customer; and DHS funds the border-surveillance work that just got pushed by the federal shutdown. The Q3 print — $19.1M revenue (+109% YoY), $110.5M backlog (+196% from fiscal year-open) — confirms the defense-optics thesis is compounding faster than the model implied, but the offsetting drag is share-count creep (62.8M vs <58M last Q) from North Run's Series G preferred conversions, which compresses the 2030 PPS math if it continues.
