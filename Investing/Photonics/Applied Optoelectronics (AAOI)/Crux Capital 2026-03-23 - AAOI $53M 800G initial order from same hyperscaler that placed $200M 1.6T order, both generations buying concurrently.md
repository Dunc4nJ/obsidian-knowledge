# AAOI $53M 800G Initial Order — Same Customer As 1.6T

[[Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI)]] announced on 2026-03-23 a new **initial volume order of $53M+ for 800G single-mode datacenter transceivers**. The buyer is the same major hyperscale customer that recently placed the **$200M 1.6T volume order** referenced in prior Crux pieces.

## Why this matters

The dual order resolves a real bear concern: that hyperscalers would skip 800G and force the industry to wait through a 1.6T transition. Crux frames the read as the opposite — **1.6T does not kill 800G demand**. Hyperscalers are buying both generations *concurrently* to support GPU cluster buildouts, manage power-per-bit, and stage cost curves. The same dynamic is consistent with what other transceiver vendors ([[Lumentum (LITE)]], [[Coherent (COHR)]], [[Fabrinet (FN)]]) have been describing.

## Step-function math

- Q4 2025 800G revenue was **<$4M**.
- The new $53M order ships **Q2 2026 → mid-Q3 2026** — roughly 1.5 quarters.
- That alone is a >13x sequential step on 800G base.

CFO Stefan Murry explicitly framed this as an **"initial" order** and **"first of more to come"** as the customer scales AI infrastructure across multiple regions.

## Crux's read

Crux's tone is straightforwardly bullish: more orders is better, and the compressed delivery window signals management's confidence in execution. The order also concretely populates the $217M-mo 800G ladder management framed in Q4 ([[Crux Capital 2026-02-26 - AAOI Q4 call sets $378M-mo transceiver target by mid-2027, tripling Texas laser capacity, 95% AI lasers by EoY]]).

## Original Content

AOI just officially announced (03/23) a new initial volume order totaling more than $53 million for its 800G single-mode data center transceivers.

This $53 million order comes from the exact same major hyperscale customer that recently placed a massive $200 million volume order for AOI's next-generation 1.6T transceivers. This dynamic perfectly validates AOI's technological roadmap, proving that the transition to 1.6T does not kill 800G demand. Instead, hyperscalers are actively buying *both* generations concurrently to support massive GPU cluster buildups and manage their long-term operating costs, power efficiency, and cost per bit.

Also, the delivery timeline for this $53 million order is extremely compressed. Shipments are expected to begin in the second quarter of 2026 and be entirely completed by the middle of the third quarter of 2026. CFO Stefan Murry explicitly categorized this as an "initial" order and the "first of more to come" as the customer scales its AI infrastructure across multiple regions. Considering AOI's 800G revenue was under $4 million in Q4 2025, fulfilling a $53 million order in roughly a one-and-a-half-quarter window represents an explosive step-function increase in their near-term run rate.

We like to see this! The more orders, the better. And the tight time frame really shows the belief in management that they can execute.
