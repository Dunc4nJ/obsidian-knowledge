### The Earnings Print & The Initial Dip

Let's set the scene back on February 4th. Coherent reported Q2 FY2026 revenue of $1.69 billion (up 22% year-over-year on a pro forma basis) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.29. By all conventional measures, it was a strong print. Yet, the stock experienced a steep pullback, dropping roughly 20%.

*[Embedded above as image 002 — post-Feb-4 pullback chart.]*

The market experienced severe sticker shock, I believe drawing an uneven comparison between Coherent's blended growth rate and pure-play optics companies like Lumentum. The day after earnings, I posted exactly what I saw in real-time, pointing out that while the price action might have tested people's conviction, digging under the headlines revealed a highly compelling story:

> "$COHR earnings sure were... exciting? There is a lot to unpack here. That price action definitely could make you question your thesis. But if you dig under the headlines the story is very compelling and I am a buyer. Coming off of a massive $LITE earnings I believe that market had really high expectations for $COHR. But it's not an apples to apples comparison. There is a massive valuation gap. $LITE deserves the premium because it's a cleaner, more concentrated AI optics earnings stream, so the growth shows up more directly in the consolidated numbers. $COHR is still a blended story (datacenter + comms plus a real industrial/legacy base), which smooths the headline growth rate even if the datacenter engine growth is solid... I personally bought at $220 / $207 / $170. But this is my long term hold and my avg. price is way below these levels."

Lumentum commands a premium for its concentrated AI optics earnings stream. Coherent, meanwhile, presents a blended story. The Datacenter and Communications segment accelerated to an impressive 14% sequential growth, reaching $1.2 billion, operating alongside a $478 million legacy Industrial baseline. The business is becoming increasingly AI-weighted every single quarter. By listening to the call, management gave us exactly the roadmap we needed to understand the underlying value.

### Use the ears and not just the eyes

While the stock was selling off the initial print, the actual call revealed massive fundamental catalysts signaling that Coherent was structurally shifting into a higher gear. I highlighted this exact transition a few days later in my ecosystem thread:

> "When companies at this layer post strong quarters at the same time, guide higher, and openly talk about being sold out, it means real infrastructure is being built in a very significant way... Demand visibility is moving outward... Supply is still the bottleneck... The architecture shift is very real... Manufacturing execution is becoming a moat..."

Management validated every single one of those signals. In a constrained market, visibility means everything. CEO Jim Anderson revealed the sheer magnitude of their datacenter demand:

> "In Q2, we experienced another step function increase in our data center bookings, with a book-to-bill ratio that exceeded 4x, as customer demand continues to increase and customers place orders further out in time, which provides us with strong visibility for the coming quarters."

Indium Phosphide (InP) capacity serves as the ultimate bottleneck for the 800G and 1.6T supercycle.

*[Embedded above as image 003 — InP capacity bottleneck visual.]*

Coherent works to solve this with their industry-first 6-inch wafer lines, which produce more than 4x as many chips at less than half the cost of legacy 3-inch wafers. Jim Anderson directly addressed the speed of this manufacturing ramp:

> "...if you look at the number of wafer starts that we're starting this quarter, we're basically at 80% of that target capacity already. So we're starting wafers at 80% of the goal of doubling capacity, which is really strong and frankly ahead of schedule."

Great top-line growth requires capturing the underlying value. CFO Sherri Luther has been ruthlessly optimizing the business. She highlighted the sale of their Munich tools division - a business generating $25 million in quarterly revenue with margins below the corporate average:

> "The sale of this product division is expected to be immediately accretive to both gross margin and EPS."

This portfolio optimization directly supports their broader profitability goals. Sherri Luther detailed the massive structural progress the company is making:

> "...we've actually improved our gross margin by about 470 basis points... I'm, you know, really pleased with the progress. We're still, as I would consider, to be in our early stages as we continue to drive toward that greater than 42% target."

### The Story Widens at OFC

Fast forward to mid-March at the OFC 2026 conference, and the underlying thesis found complete validation. Coherent took the stage and blew the doors off their TAM, shifting the narrative to becoming the foundational "fabric" of the AI data center.

Management unveiled new growth engines layering on over $20 billion in entirely incremental market opportunity by 2030.

---

OCS is a $4B Market: They officially doubled their Optical Circuit Switch (OCS) TAM from $2 billion to $4 billion by 2030.

*[Embedded above as image 004 — OCS $4B TAM slide.]*

They now feature over 10 customer engagements, and use cases are expanding rapidly into scale-up networks and hot-swap failover deployments. Hyperscalers love Coherent's liquid crystal technology for its completely solid-state reliability, and CTO Julie Sheridan Eng perfectly summarized the architectural value:

> "...at a software notice, I can reconnect all my GPUs... being able to almost on a job level optimize the topology for the workflow for the maximum utilization of those expensive GPUs is very, very valuable."

If you want to learn more about OCS, check out this article I wrote: [OCS: A Must-Learn Topic](https://cruxcapitalgroup.substack.com/p/ocs-a-must-learn-topic) (Gaetano, Apr 1).

---

Multi-Rail for Scale-Across: As AI workloads expand across multiple facilities, operators require highly efficient connections between geographically separated datacenters.

*[Embedded above as image 005 — Multi-Rail scale-across diagram.]*

Coherent introduced Multi-Rail, a groundbreaking transport system allowing customers to process four times the traffic within the exact same physical footprint. This system features sub-linear power scaling and will start generating near-term revenue in the first half of 2027.

---

The $15B CPO Revolution: Co-Packaged Optics brings optical connections directly to the scale-up network inside the rack. This network utilizes exclusively copper links today, making the transition to optics a pure TAM expansion. Coherent captures an immense competitive advantage here through its deeply integrated vertical technology stack. They design and manufacture every critical element of the CPO ecosystem in-house, ranging from the Silicon Photonics integrated circuits and continuous-wave lasers to highly specialized passive optics like microlens arrays.

*[Embedded above as image 006 — $15B CPO opportunity visual.]*

During the conference, management demonstrated their innovation by showcasing a massive 6.4 Terabit per second socketed CPO engine fitting entirely on the size of a thumb. The commercial trajectory is already materializing rapidly, as they recently secured an exceptionally large purchase order from a market-leading AI customer, paving the way for initial revenue later this calendar year. Jim Anderson emphasized the sheer scale of this transition:

> "We believe the scale up CPO opportunity will dwarf the opportunity in scale out. It will be orders of magnitude larger. And so we view that as all incremental TAM for the optical industry in general, and certainly for us as well. Since that network is 100% electrical today, all of that optical content and scale up is incremental TAM."

---

Thermal: Coherent officially unveiled "Thermal Solutions" as a new $2 billion TAM by 2030

*[Embedded above as image 007 — Thermal Solutions $2B TAM visual.]*

They are engaging with hyperscalers to use their proprietary Thermadite ceramic material to replace copper cold plates, lowering GPU junction temperatures by 5 to 10 degrees. Also, they are developing thermoelectric generators to harvest waste heat and convert it directly into electricity. Jim Anderson explained the economics clearly:

> "If I can take heat out and recycle it back into power, and that's just like free energy, right? And so that's very compelling to every data center owner, even if it were just, you know, small percentages of recovery of heat."

---

### The Stealth Catalysts (Apple & Semicap)

Beyond the headline datacenter numbers, management confirmed two massive, under-the-radar developments that provide even more fundamental support for the stock.

Apple: Coherent secured a massive multi-year partnership with Apple for 3D sensing components. This agreement utilizes their 6-inch Gallium Arsenide (GaAs) lines in their Sherman, Texas facility, proving the immense value of their U.S.-based manufacturing footprint. Revenue from this partnership kicks in during the second half of calendar 2026, creating a fantastic secondary growth engine.

Semicap Recovery: The Industrial segment is bottoming out and preparing to rebound. Coherent provides highly specialized lasers for semiconductor manufacturing equipment (Semicap). Management explicitly noted a very strong pickup in Semicap orders, which will drive sequential revenue growth through the second half of the calendar year.

---

### The NVIDIA Check

The market struggled to digest Coherent's earnings print back in February, but the biggest player in AI saw the vision clearly.

Right as the story was accelerating, NVIDIA and Coherent announced a massive expansion of their two-decade-long partnership. NVIDIA poured a $2 billion equity investment directly into Coherent alongside a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment for advanced optical solutions.

When asked about allocating that massive cash infusion, CFO Sherri Luther gave the exact answer you want to hear in a constrained market:

> "Definitely, first priority is expanding capacity... You know, this agreement is for CPO solutions, so expanding capacity in our Sherman, Texas facility in particular."

This is the ultimate definition of skin in the game. NVIDIA is directly funding Coherent's 6-inch manufacturing moat to ensure their own AI roadmaps remain resistant to bottlenecks.
