# Crux Capital LITE Q2 FY26 readout - EML 25-30 pct supply gap, all capacity locked under LTAs through CY2027, OCS backlog past $400M, CPO and UHP into 1H 2027

Crux Capital's same-day reaction to Lumentum's Q2 FY26 earnings call (Feb 3, 2026 print). Frames the print as Lumentum proving an InP-based light-source choke point while building OCS and ultra-high-power lasers into second/third legs with real backlog. Companion Coherent ([[Coherent (COHR)]]) note promised next day; downstream reads on [[Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI)]], POET, and ALMU to follow.

## Key takeaways

- **EML supply still 25-30% short of demand even after pulled-forward throughput and yield work** — value continues to concentrate upstream in [[Lumentum (LITE)]]'s light sources, the same constraint [[Sancet 2026 - Goldman optical cheat sheet omits substrate epi equipment and laser layers (IQE Soitec Tower Aixtron AEHR LPKF SIVE)]] argues the sell-side cheat sheets miss by skipping the InP/MOCVD layer. Constraint is structural, not transient.

- **All EML capacity LTA-locked through calendar 2027, with marginal units triggering fresh pricing conversations** — LTAs set the baseline price; any incremental unit becomes a new negotiation in a tight regime. This is the textbook setup for sustained pricing power and reframes how to model FY26-FY27 ASP trajectory.

- **Q2 print $665.5M with guide to ~$805M next quarter, both with margin expansion** — a ~21% sequential step-up alongside mix improvement (200G lane lasers already punching above weight in revenue dollars) means the bottleneck is monetizing as both more units and richer mix per wafer, not one or the other.

- **OCS backlog past $400M across multiple customers, weighted to 2H CY26 and early FY27** — optical circuit switching is no longer a slideware bet for LITE; it is a booked, multi-customer revenue stream with timing visibility. Reads as confirmation for the broader OCS thesis embedded in the [[Celestial Photonic Fabric Platform for AI Accelerators - arXiv 2507.14000 specs 33TB unified memory and 115Tbps all-to-all switching across 16 XPUs (Ding Diep 2025)]] photonic-fabric narrative.

- **CPO + ultra-high-power lasers picked up another multi-hundred-million dollar order shipping 1H 2027, plus a material UHP shipment inflection later this year** — second and third revenue legs are sequencing into hard backlog faster than consensus modeled, and CPO booking timing aligns with the supply-chain map in [[Sancet ENA bull case - Enablence in-house PLC fab in Fremont makes them irreplaceable Sivers partner in AI CPO supply chain]].

- **Mgmt now openly evaluating additional fab capacity including acquisitions, plus faster pivot to contract manufacturing for backend assembly** — capacity-add language signals demand visibility strong enough to justify structural decisions. Backend gating means even if MOCVD wafer fab were uncapped, assembly throughput is now its own constraint (cf. [[Ren Aramb thesis - Q1 2026 EPS up 30x YoY makes Win Semi the III-V foundry bottleneck for the photonics and LEO satellite ramps]] on the III-V-foundry-as-bottleneck theme — same story, one node downstream).

- **ELS (external light source) modules called out as a strategic wildcard** — moving up the stack from chips to packaged ELS modules expands content per deployment and broadens addressable customer set. Direct read-through to POET's thesis; Crux promises a separate ELS-focused post for POET investors.

- **Forward guide implies >85% YoY revenue growth and mgmt frames OCS + CPO as still at "the starting line"** — guidance is a baseline for two opportunities mgmt explicitly says have not yet ramped. Asymmetric upside is in the not-yet-modeled ramp of OCS and CPO on top of the EML pricing capture.
