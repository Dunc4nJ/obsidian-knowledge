# LITE CEO Q2 FY26 - scale-up CPO is largest single growth driver still in infancy, massive supply demand imbalance, $2B quarterly target on track

## Key Takeaways

- LITE CEO frames scale-up CPO as the single largest growth driver and "very much in its infancy" — explicit issuer-side corroboration of the [[Goldman 2026 AI optical cheat sheet maps EPS upside across lasers PCBs and CCL manufacturers|Goldman CPO TAM expansion thesis]] (close-to-nothing → ~$91B by 2028), straight from the Q2 FY26 earnings call.

- CEO sharply distinguishes scale-out from scale-up CPO: the multi-billion dollar orders disclosed on prior calls were "mostly on scale-out", but scale-up revenue opportunity will be "significantly more than that" — implies disclosed backlog is the smaller half of what's coming and that the bigger leg has not yet been quantified to investors.

- Total CPO revenue opportunity sized at "somewhere greater than $5 billion" (transcript screenshot cuts off mid-sentence), and the $2B-quarterly-revenue goal articulated at OFC is reaffirmed as on-track. Bridges near-term guidance and the longer-term TAM the bulls are underwriting.

- Author (@aleabitoreddit) reads the CEO commentary as confirmation of the CPO supercycle thesis and positions via high-beta peers: [[Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE.ST)]], [[Tower Semiconductor (TSEM)]], [[Soitec (SOI.PA)]], plus MSScorps and Shunsin (Taiwan/non-US names not in vault).
