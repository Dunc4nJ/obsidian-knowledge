# CRDO Q4 FY2026 earnings — FY26 tripled to $1.335B, but 200G/lane FY27 "relatively light" (1.6T is a FY28 story)

Credo reported fiscal Q4 2026 (quarter ended **May 2, 2026**, reported **June 1, 2026**): record Q4 revenue $437.0M (+157% YoY) and FY26 revenue of $1,335.1M, more than tripling YoY (+206%). FY27 is guided to +80%+ growth with optical revenue exceeding $600M. But the load-bearing peer-benchmark item is CEO Bill Brennan's call statement that **FY27 (May 2026–Apr 2027) 200G/lane revenue will be "relatively light"** — meaning Credo's 1.6T volume, like much of the optical-DSP cohort, is **effectively a FY28 story**. That places Credo's 1.6T ramp in the same late-window timeframe as [[MaxLinear (MXL)]]'s Rushmore late-2026 ramp, well behind [[Marvell Technology (MRVL)]] and [[Broadcom (AVGO)]], which are already shipping 1.6T in volume.

Companion peer reads: [[MRVL Q1 FY2027 earnings - record $2.418B rev +28pct, data center $1.833B +27pct (76pct of total), 1.6T 200G-lane ramping after H2 FY26 production launch, interconnect guided +70pct YoY]] (1.6T in production since H2 FY26) and [[AVGO Q2 FY2026 earnings - record $22.187B rev +48pct, AI semi $10.8B +143pct, Hock Tan calls AVGO de facto standard in CPO and 1.6T DSPs, Q3 AI guide $16B]] ("de facto standard" in CPO/1.6T). Credo is the 4th named 1.6T DSP supplier (see [[Credo Bluebird 1.6T optical DSP under-20W full-DSP and LRO variants makes CRDO a 4th 1.6T DSP supplier vs MRVL AVGO MXL]]).

## Key Takeaways

- **Record Q4 revenue: $437.0M, +157.0% YoY** (+7.4% QoQ). GAAP gross margin 68.2%; non-GAAP 68.3%. GAAP net income $169.1M; non-GAAP $226.7M. GAAP diluted EPS $0.88 (up from $0.20 prior-year quarter); non-GAAP $1.16.
- **FY26 revenue: $1,335.1M, more than tripled YoY (+206%)** ($1,335.116M). Non-GAAP net income $661.5M (>5x prior year). FY26 non-GAAP operating margin 47.8%. Ended FY26 with $1.4B cash and short-term investments.
- **FY27 guided to +80%+ total revenue growth.** Verbatim (CFO Daniel Fleming): *"Driving more than 80% year over year total revenue growth for the full year."* Q1 FY27 guidance: revenue $465.0M–$475.0M; non-GAAP gross margin 67.0%–69.0%.
- **FY27 optical revenue >$600M, with optical DSPs, SiPho PICs, and ZeroFlap optics each >$100M.** Verbatim (CEO Bill Brennan): *"In fiscal 27, we expect our optical DSPs, SiPho PICs, and ZeroFlap optics will each contribute more than $100 million of revenue. And in total, more than $600 million of revenue."*
- **CRITICAL — 200G/lane FY27 will be "relatively light"; 1.6T is effectively a FY28 story.** Verbatim (CEO Bill Brennan): *"I think our fiscal 2027 will have relatively light revenue as it relates to 200 gig per lane."* Management framed 200G/lane adoption as limited in FY27 with acceleration expected thereafter (i.e., FY28). This is the timing read that matters versus the peer set.
- **CEO outlook quote** (Brennan, press release): *"Fiscal 2026 marked another defining year for Credo. For the year, revenue more than tripled to $1.3 billion, and non-GAAP net income increased more than five times to $662 million."*
- **CEO compensation context:** the board granted CEO William Brennan a one-time 100%-performance-based PSU award of up to 1,437,000 shares (six tranches through June 30, 2031), tied to revenue hurdles from $2.5B to $7.5B and stock-price hurdles from $244.70 to $489.40 — the tranche-1 revenue hurdle of $2.5B is ~87% above FY26 revenue of ~$1.335B. Signals an internal strategic plan targeting ~$5B trailing-four-quarter revenue by end of FY31.

## Why it matters (vs MaxLinear)

Credo's FY26 is a blowout on AECs and SerDes/optical mix, but on the specific 1.6T optical-DSP timing question, Credo lands closer to [[MaxLinear (MXL)]] than to the incumbents: 200G/lane revenue is "relatively light" in FY27, so 1.6T volume is effectively a FY28 story. That maps to MXL's Rushmore late-2026 ramp (see [[MaxLinear OFC 2026 Rushmore live showcase - first major high-speed DSP built entirely in Samsung technology as foundry second source, demos and interop only, no ramp language]]) — both trail [[Marvell Technology (MRVL)]] (1.6T in production since H2 FY26) and [[Broadcom (AVGO)]] (self-described 1.6T/CPO "de facto standard"). The differentiator: Credo is building a vertically integrated optical stack (DustPhotonics SiPho PICs, ZeroFlap, Bluebird DSP — see [[Crux Capital 2026-04-14 - CRDO Big Optics Acquisition - DustPhotonics SiPho PIC deal adds 400G-800G-1.6T-3.2T PIC portfolio plus Hyperlume microLED plus CoMira IP make vertically integrated AI connectivity stack]]) with optical guided >$600M in FY27, whereas MaxLinear competes mainly at the DSP + TIA component layer.

### Press release — financial highlights (verbatim)

San Jose, Calif. (June 1, 2026) - Credo Technology Group Holding Ltd (Nasdaq: CRDO) ... today reported financial results for the fourth quarter and full fiscal year 2026, ended May 2, 2026.

**Fourth Quarter of Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Highlights**

- Revenue of $437.0 million grew by 7.4% quarter over quarter and 157.0% year over year
- GAAP gross margin of 68.2% and non-GAAP gross margin of 68.3%
- GAAP operating expenses of $142.2 million and non-GAAP operating expenses of $81.7 million
- GAAP net income of $169.1 million and non-GAAP net income of $226.7 million
- GAAP diluted net income per share of $0.88 and non-GAAP diluted net income per share of $1.16

For fiscal 2026, revenue reached $1.34 billion ($1,335.116 million), more than tripling versus the prior year, while non-GAAP net income rose to $661.5 million ($661.543 million), more than five times the prior year. The company ended the year with $1.4 billion in cash and short-term investments as of May 2, 2026.

> Bill Brennan, Credo's President and Chief Executive Officer, stated, "Fiscal 2026 marked another defining year for Credo. For the year, revenue more than tripled to $1.3 billion, and non-GAAP net income increased more than five times to $662 million. As we enter into fiscal 2027, Credo expects to achieve continued strong financial performance with our innovative and vertically integrated approach that enables customers to accelerate cluster time-to-stability, maximize GPU utilization, improve network reliability, and reduce overall infrastructure power and operating costs."

**First Quarter of Fiscal Year 2027 Financial Outlook**

- Revenue is expected to be between $465.0 million and $475.0 million
- GAAP gross margin is expected to be between 66.9% and 68.9%, and non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be between 67.0% and 69.0%
- GAAP operating expenses are expected to be between $167.6 million and $171.6 million, and non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be between $86.0 million and $90.0 million

**CEO special PSU award (from the 8-K):** one-time, 100% performance-based grant to William Brennan of up to 1,437,000 ordinary shares, vesting in six substantially equal tranches (239,500 each) over a performance period ending June 30, 2031, tied to both a Revenue Goal and a Stock Price Goal per tranche. Tranche-1 hurdles: $2,500,000,000 revenue (≈87% above FY26 revenue of ≈$1,335,116,000) and $244.70 stock price (≈63% above the April 2026 30-day average of $150.02). Final tranches envision trailing-four-quarter revenue of $5,000,000,000 (the strategic-plan goal) and up to $7.5B / $489.40.
