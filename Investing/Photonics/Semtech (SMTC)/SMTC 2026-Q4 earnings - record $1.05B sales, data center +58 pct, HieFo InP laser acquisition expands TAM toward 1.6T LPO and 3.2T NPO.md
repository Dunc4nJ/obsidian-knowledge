### Semtech (SMTC) Earnings

_A quick introduction!_

Today we are taking a look into Semtech Corporation (SMTC). If you follow the massive infrastructure build-out powering the AI revolution, you need to be paying attention to what this company is doing right now especially in the world of high-speed optics.

Let's get into it.

---

#### What is Semtech?

Historically, Semtech has been best known as a high-performance analog and mixed-signal semiconductor company, with a massive footprint in IoT connectivity (specifically as the pioneer of LoRa technology) and consumer circuit protection.

However, over the last few years, SMTC has positioned itself as a critical enabler of the AI data center. They specialize in the physical layer of networking connectivity, specifically the Transimpedance Amplifiers (TIAs), laser drivers, and active copper cables (ACCs) that allow AI clusters to move mind-boggling amounts of data without melting down the power grid.

---

#### Data Center is the Engine

Semtech just closed out a monster fiscal year, reporting a record $1.05 billion in net sales (up 15% year-over-year). Adjusted diluted earnings per share (EPS) surged 94% to $1.71 for the year.

But I think the real story here is the data center segment. Driven by hyperscaler demand, data center revenues hit a record $223 million for the year, representing 58% annual growth. And they are just getting started. Management guided that they expect data center year-over-year revenue growth to exceed 50% this coming fiscal year.

Why the explosive growth? It comes down to power constraints:

> "As hyperscalers measure data center capacity in megawatts, the ability to move data faster while consuming less power at the networking layer is no longer just a differentiator, it's an enabler."

---

#### LPO, NPO, and the HieFo Acquisition

I know most of my readers care a lot about optics, and that is exactly where Semtech's story gets the most exciting.

As data center architectures evolve from 800G to 1.6T and eventually 3.2T, the complexity and power draw of optical interconnects skyrocket. Semtech is attacking this optical bottleneck from multiple angles:

1 - Linear Pluggable Optics (LPO). Semtech is already securing design wins with several leading U.S. hyperscalers for their TIAs and drivers used in 800G LPO transceivers. The shift to LPO is a massive financial tailwind for Semtech. Because LPO architectures allow Semtech to sell both the modulator drivers and the TIAs, it effectively expands their Total Addressable Market (TAM) by 150%.

> "We expect a meaningful revenue contribution from TIAs for LPOs starting in Q4 and a momentum to build into calendar 2026," Hou noted.

2 - The HieFo Acquisition. Semtech just acquired HieFo Corporation for ~$34 million. HieFo makes high-efficiency Indium Phosphide (InP) optoelectronic devices, specifically gain chips and Continuous Wave (CW) lasers.

> "Put simply, HieFo makes the light-emitting building blocks that sit at the heart of high-speed optical interconnects."

Previously, Semtech made the electronic components (TIAs, drivers) that talked to the optical lasers. By bringing HieFo's InP laser technology in-house, Semtech can now co-develop and co-optimize the entire electronic-optoelectronic-photonic chipset. This allows them to squeeze out maximum power efficiency for hyperscalers navigating the jump to 1.6T and 3.2T optical modules.

This combination will "increase Semtech's content opportunity from high single-digit dollars in an 800G module to about $80 in a 3.2T module."

3 - Near-Packaged Optics (NPO). Looking slightly further out, Semtech is actively helping define the NPO (Near-Packaged Optics) standard alongside other industry leaders. As optics move closer to the compute ASIC to save power, NPO allows the ecosystem to standardize form factors. Unlike Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) where Semtech might only provide the laser, NPO ensures Semtech will be "all over the place with the laser drivers and TIAs and lasers and even silicon photonics modulator," ensuring they capture maximum value inside the rack.

---

#### TLDR;

Semtech used to be a secondary player in the data center, but by dominating the low-power analog connectivity space and aggressively expanding their optical component stack via HieFo, they have made themselves indispensable. With live 1.6T optical demonstrations alongside NVIDIA and Broadcom at this year's OFC conference, and >50% data center growth projected for the coming year, SMTC is cementing itself as a premier pure-play on AI optical infrastructure.
