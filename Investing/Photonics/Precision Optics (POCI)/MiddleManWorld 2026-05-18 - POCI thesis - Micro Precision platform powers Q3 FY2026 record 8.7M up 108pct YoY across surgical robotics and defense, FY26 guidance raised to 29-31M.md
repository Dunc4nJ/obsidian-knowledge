# MiddleManWorld 2026-05-18 - POCI thesis - Micro Precision platform powers Q3 FY2026 record 8.7M up 108pct YoY across surgical robotics and defense, FY26 guidance raised to 29-31M

## Key Takeaways

- [[Precision Optics (POCI)]] is a 1982-vintage Massachusetts micro-optics shop pitched as a vertically integrated U.S. supplier of sub-millimeter lenses, prisms, and CMOS endoscopes for robotic surgery platforms and defense/aerospace — meaningfully different from the AI-datacenter optics names that dominate the [[moc - Photonics]] roster.
- The Micro Precision technology platform — custom lenses down to 0.37 mm, prisms down to 0.10 mm, CMOS endoscopes/cameras as small as 0.6 mm, plus 3D imaging — is the differentiated SWaP-optimized capability stack; the Unity Imaging Platform productizes it as a modular system for accelerating prototyping to production with robotic surgery customers.
- Q3 FY2026 revenue of $8.7M (+108% YoY) is the headline data point, driven by surgical robotics and aerospace; supporting bookings include a $3.5M follow-on for single-use ophthalmic endoscopes, multi-million-dollar ramps on single-use cystoscopes with a leading surgical robotics company, and new defense orders for high-resolution borescopes and AR pilot-training subsystems.
- FY2026 revenue guidance was raised to $29-31M, with management framing the next leg as scaling 3D imaging into next-gen robotic surgery systems and expanding high-reliability defense programs — explicitly leaning on robotic-assisted minimally invasive surgery growth and U.S. defense optics priorities as the demand backdrop.
- Closest precedent in the existing photonics universe is [[LightPath (LPTH)]] — also a vertically integrated U.S. precision-lens shop selling into surgical visualization and defense (NGSRI, DHS, AR). POCI's edge is in the sub-millimeter regime (endoscope/cystoscope optics) versus LPTH's IR/long-range Black Diamond chalcogenide stack, so they're complements rather than direct competitors in any single program.

> Pioneering Micro-Precision Optics for Robotic Surgery and Defense Applications
>
> Precision Optics Corporation $POCI founded in 1982 and headquartered in Littleton (Gardner), Massachusetts, is a vertically integrated U.S. manufacturer specializing in advanced micro-optics, 3D imaging systems, and illumination solutions. The company delivers high-performance custom components and complete devices optimized for robotic surgery platforms and high-reliability defense/aerospace programs.
>
> At the core of $POCI expertise is its Micro Precision™ technology platform: ultra-small custom lenses (down to 0.37 mm), prisms (down to 0.10 mm), CMOS endoscopes and cameras (as small as 0.6 mm), and 3D imaging systems. Their fully in-house capabilities span design, prototyping, regulatory support, and volume production — enabling SWaP-optimized (Size, Weight, and Power) solutions with superior clarity and integration. Flagship offerings include the Unity™ Imaging Platform (modular system accelerating prototyping to production for robotic applications), micro medical cameras, 3D endoscopes for robotic surgery, single-use cystoscopes, and complex optical assemblies for augmented reality pilot training and jet engine borescopes.
>
> Strategically, $POCI is capitalizing on the rapid growth of robotic-assisted minimally invasive surgery and U.S. defense priorities for advanced optics. Recent momentum includes record Q3 FY2026 revenue of $8.7M (+108% YoY), driven by surgical robotics and aerospace programs, a $3.5M follow-on production order for single-use ophthalmic endoscopes, multi-million dollar ramps with a leading surgical robotics company for single-use cystoscopes, increased production for top-tier aerospace customers, and new defense development orders for high-resolution borescopes and AR subsystems.
>
> Raised FY2026 revenue guidance to $29–$31M, with future focus on scaling 3D imaging in next-gen robotic surgery systems and expanding high-reliability defense programs.
