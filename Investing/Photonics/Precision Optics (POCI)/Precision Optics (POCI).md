# Precision Optics (POCI)

Vertically integrated U.S. micro-optics manufacturer based in Littleton (Gardner), Massachusetts, founded 1982. Micro Precision technology platform spans ultra-small custom lenses (down to 0.37 mm), prisms (down to 0.10 mm), CMOS endoscopes and cameras (as small as 0.6 mm), and 3D imaging systems. Flagship Unity Imaging Platform serves robotic surgery customers; defense/aerospace programs include single-use ophthalmic endoscopes, multi-million dollar single-use cystoscope ramps, AR pilot training optics, and jet engine borescopes. Q3 FY2026 revenue of $8.7M (+108% YoY); FY2026 guidance raised to $29-31M.
