# MRVL Q1 FY2027 earnings — record $2.418B revenue, 1.6T 200G/lane ramping a year ahead of [[MaxLinear (MXL)]]

Marvell reported its fiscal Q1 2027 (quarter ended **May 2, 2026**, reported **May 27, 2026**) and used the call to confirm that its **200G/lane 1.6T PAM DSPs went into production in the second half of fiscal 2026 and are "ramping quickly" in FY27** — putting Marvell into 1.6T volume roughly a year before [[MaxLinear (MXL)]]'s Rushmore late-2026 ramp (see [[MaxLinear OFC 2026 Rushmore live showcase - first major high-speed DSP built entirely in Samsung technology as foundry second source, demos and interop only, no ramp language]]). Marvell raised both FY27 and FY28 outlooks and now guides the **interconnect business to grow >70% YoY in FY27**, a sharp step up from the prior 50% expectation.

This is the benchmark quarter for the optical-DSP peer set: Marvell shipping 1.6T in volume now, with revenue stepping up again in FY28, vs. MaxLinear's Rushmore still pre-ramp. Companion peer reads: [[AVGO Q2 FY2026 earnings - record $22.187B rev +48pct, AI semi $10.8B +143pct, Hock Tan calls AVGO de facto standard in CPO and 1.6T DSPs, Q3 AI guide $16B]] and [[CRDO Q4 FY2026 earnings - record $437M rev +157pct, FY26 $1.335B tripled, FY27 guided +80pct with optical >$600M but 200G-lane FY27 relatively light (a FY28 story)]].

## Key Takeaways

- **Record revenue: $2.418B, +28% YoY** ($2,417.8M; up from $1,895.3M in Q1 FY26, +9% QoQ). $18.0M above the mid-point of prior guidance. GAAP gross margin 52.1%; non-GAAP 58.9%. GAAP diluted EPS $0.04; non-GAAP $0.80. Record operating cash flow $638.8M.
- **Data center end market $1,832.7M, +27% YoY** (+11% QoQ), **76% of total revenue** (vs 74% in Q4 FY26, 76% in Q1 FY26). Communications and other was $585.1M (+29% YoY), 24% of total. CEO on the call (line ref below): *"In our data center end market, we delivered record first quarter revenue of $1.8 billion representing 11% sequential growth and 27% year over year growth."*
- **1.6T (200G/lane) PAM DSPs launched production in H2 FY26 and are ramping quickly — the headline peer-benchmark statement.** Verbatim from CEO Matt Murphy on the May 27, 2026 call:
  > "Within our scale out PAM franchise, demand continues to strengthen for our 800 gig products, while our **200-gig-per-lane 1.6T solutions are ramping quickly this fiscal year following their production launch in the second half of fiscal 26**. We expect 1.6T revenue to take another substantial step up in fiscal 28. We continue to benefit from the first to market cadence we have maintained across successive PAM 4 generations."
- **Interconnect guided to grow >70% YoY in FY27** (up from prior ~50%), and is the single largest portion of the data center business. Verbatim (Murphy):
  > "Demand for our interconnect products continues to accelerate and as a result, we have increased our fiscal 27 revenue growth expectations for this business to more than 70% year over year. Interconnect is also a major driver of the higher fiscal 28 company revenue outlook we provided today."
  > "We now expect overall Marvell revenue fiscal 27 to grow approximately 40% year over year to nearly $11.5 billion ... Notably, we expect our interconnect business to grow more than 70% year over year, well above our prior expectation of 50% growth."
- **Q2 FY27 guidance: $2.700B +/- 5%** (~35% YoY growth at the mid-point); non-GAAP gross margin 58.25%–59.25%; non-GAAP EPS $0.93 +/- $0.05. CEO: revenue growth expected to "continue accelerating each quarter throughout fiscal 2027."
- **CoherentLight 200G/lane 1.6T already shipping; DCI 1.6T (2nm coherent DSP) sampling this year.** Verbatim (Murphy): *"We have already begun shipping the first generation of our CoherentLight 200-gig-per-lane 1.6T products"*; on DCI, the *"industry's first secure 1.6T ZR and ZR+ DCI modules ... are expected to begin sampling this year."*
- **Scale-up optics ramps next fiscal year, >2x prior outlook.** Marvell now expects scale-up optics revenue to "more than double our prior outlook of approximately a $150 million" (the prior figure was based solely on Celestial AI). 6.4T 3rd-gen Light Engine engaged with multiple tier-1 customers for NPO/CPO; Celestial AI photonic-fabric chiplet selected by a tier-1 hyperscaler for next-gen XPU scale-up.
- **Expanded NVIDIA partnership** spanning optics (DSPs/TIAs/drivers extending into silicon photonics collaboration), NVLink Fusion, and AI RAN; Marvell received an investment from NVIDIA.

## Why it matters (vs MaxLinear)

Marvell is the timing yardstick for the 1.6T optical-DSP race. With 200G/lane in production since H2 FY26 and "ramping quickly" now — plus a "substantial step up" guided for FY28 — Marvell is roughly a year ahead of [[MaxLinear (MXL)]]'s Rushmore, which at OFC 2026 was still demos/interop with "no ramp language." Among the four 1.6T DSP suppliers (MRVL, [[Broadcom (AVGO)]], [[Credo Technology (CRDO)]], MXL — see [[Credo Bluebird 1.6T optical DSP under-20W full-DSP and LRO variants makes CRDO a 4th 1.6T DSP supplier vs MRVL AVGO MXL]]), Marvell pairs first-to-market DSP cadence with TIAs/drivers (heading to a >$1B annualized run rate), where MaxLinear competes at the component layer (see [[MXL Washington 200G TIA available now - four-lane SiGe TIA for 1.6T AI optics at 750mW, interops with all major PAM4 DSP vendors, mass production H2 2026]]).

### Press release — financial highlights (verbatim)

May 27, 2026

- Q1 Net Revenue: $2.418 billion, a new record, grew by 28% year-on-year
- Q1 Gross Margin: 52.1% GAAP gross margin; 58.9% non-GAAP gross margin
- Q1 Diluted income per share: $0.04 GAAP diluted income per share; $0.80 non-GAAP diluted income per share

SANTA CLARA, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Marvell Technology, Inc. (NASDAQ: MRVL), a leader in data infrastructure semiconductor solutions, today reported financial results for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027.

Net revenue for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 was $2.418 billion, $18.0 million above the mid-point of the Company's guidance provided on March 5, 2026.

GAAP net income for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 was $34.5 million, or $0.04 per diluted share. Non-GAAP net income for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 was $718.0 million, or $0.80 per diluted share. Cash flow from operations for the first quarter was $638.8 million, a record high.

> "Marvell delivered record first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $2.418 billion, up 28% year-over-year, and guided second-quarter revenue to $2.7 billion at the mid-point, representing 35% year-over-year growth. We expect revenue growth to continue accelerating each quarter throughout fiscal 2027, driven by continued strength in our data center business," said Matt Murphy, Marvell's Chairman and CEO. "We are seeing exceptional AI-related bookings, and as a result, we are significantly raising Marvell's revenue outlook for both fiscal 2027 and fiscal 2028 compared with the guidance we provided last quarter. This improved outlook is being driven by strong demand across a broad set of Marvell solutions, including 800G and 1.6T scale-out optics, 51.2T Ethernet scale-out switches, scale-up optical solutions for NPO and CPO applications, scale-across datacenter interconnect modules, and custom XPU and XPU-attach solutions."

**Second Quarter of Fiscal 2027 Financial Outlook**

- Net revenue is expected to be $2.700 billion +/- 5%.
- GAAP gross margin is expected to be 52.1% to 53.1%.
- Non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be 58.25% to 59.25%.
- GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $960 million.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be approximately $600 million.
- GAAP diluted net income per share is expected to be $0.37 +/- $0.05 per share.
- Non-GAAP diluted net income per share is expected to be $0.93 +/- $0.05 per share.
