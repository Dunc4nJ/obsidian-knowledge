Companies and research covering optical modules and engines, lasers (CW, EML, VCSEL), passive optical components (couplers, filters, attenuators, WDM), silicon photonics, co-packaged optics (CPO), and PCB/CCL inputs to the optical stack. Primary demand driver tracked here is AI-datacenter buildout (400G → 800G → 1.6T transitions) and adjacent telecom.
