# Win Semi (3105.TWO)

Win Semi (3105.TWO) — Taipei Exchange (TPEx); largest pure-play compound semiconductor foundry globally (~65% share). Fabricates GaAs, InP, GaN ICs for RF (5G, WiFi 7), AI-datacenter optics (EML, optical PD), LEO satellites, defense radar; outsourced foundry partner for Sivers' DFB lasers.
