# Ren Aramb thesis - Q1 2026 EPS up 30x YoY makes Win Semi the III-V foundry bottleneck for the photonics and LEO satellite ramps

## Key Takeaways

- Q1 2026 prints flagged as the strongest in years — EPS +30x YoY, revenue +28% YoY, Q2 guidance +14-16% QoQ — and crucially delivered with capacity utilization still only at ~60%, implying significant operating leverage if utilization climbs into the optical/LEO ramps the company is calling out.
- Author frames Win Semi as a structural ~65% global share pure-play III-V foundry — the GaAs/InP/GaN fab layer that sits beneath every compound-semi roadmap. This is the same logic that places it under Photonics → Foundries alongside [[Tower Semiconductor (TSEM)]]: not a substrate maker (that's [[AXT (AXTI)]], [[IQE (IQE.L)]], [[Soitec (SOI.PA)]]), but the pure-play wafer fab that turns those substrates into laser/PD/PA dies.
- Optical PD products enter mass production in Q2 with significant H2 ramp, and 4-inch InP line capacity is expanding by multiples this year — concrete supply-side capex matching the demand narrative around 800G/1.6T transceivers and CPO. The "AI data center revenue more than doubles in 2026" claim is the headline number for the photonics-exposed slice of the mix.
- Win Semi is named as the confirmed outsourced manufacturing partner for [[Sivers Semiconductors (SIVE.ST)]]'s DFB lasers and laser arrays for CWDM/DWDM, CPO, and next-gen optical interconnects — making the SIVE volume ramp a direct foundry-revenue tailwind. Author's framing: "When SIVE scales, Win Semi scales with it."
- Customer breadth thesis: not just Sivers — WiFi 7 chips for [[Qualcomm (QCOM)]], RF front-end modules for [[Broadcom (AVGO)]], EML lasers for hyperscaler transceivers, power amplifiers for LEO satellite constellations. Frames Win Semi as a non-substitutable toll on the optical-interconnect TAM expansion ($15B → $154B cited) — i.e. a derivative bet on every photonics-ramp thesis in the [[moc - Photonics]] sector at once.
