# Solaris Energy Infrastructure (SEI)

NYSE-listed modular natural-gas power solutions provider. ~3.1 GW pipeline by 2029 across three signed long-term hyperscaler contracts (Q1 2026), with a 900 MW expansion via the Genco acquisition plus 30 incremental turbine slots. Trades at ~6.2x EV/FY28E Sales — the closest direct peer comp for behind-the-meter gas-turbine deployments in AI data centers, distinct from [[Bloom Energy (BE)]] (SOFC fuel cells) and [[Capstone Power+ (CGEH)]] (sub-MW microturbine).
