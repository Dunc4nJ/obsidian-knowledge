# Energy

Companies and research covering electricity generation and behind-the-meter power equipment for AI data centers and the broader grid: gas turbines (combined cycle, aeroderivative, reciprocating), fuel cells, small modular reactors, utility-scale solar + storage, transmission, and the grid-interconnection bottleneck. Primary demand driver tracked here is AI-datacenter buildout shifting from a capital-scarce / permission-abundant regime (2024–25) to capital-abundant / permission-scarce (2026+), favoring vendors that compress time-to-power and dodge regulatory friction.
