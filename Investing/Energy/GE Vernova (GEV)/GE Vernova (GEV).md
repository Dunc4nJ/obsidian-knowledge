# GE Vernova (GEV)

GE Vernova (GEV, NYSE) — utility-scale power equipment incumbent. 7HA heavy-duty (290–430 MW per machine, ~64% combined-cycle efficiency) and LM6000 aeroderivative (~53.8 MW, ~42% simple-cycle) gas turbines anchor 500 MW+ baseload deployments. >$150B backlog. 4–7 year lead times for new 7HA slots are the key bottleneck shaping the BTM-vs-utility-scale split for AI data center power.
