# Independent stress test of CGEH bull theses: SEC filings, BTM leaderboard absence, and pseudonymous-promoter signals make $11.50 the floor, not the entry

Synthesis of three research waves (May 4, 2026) cross-checking the bull case for [[Capstone Power+ (CGEH)]] against SEC primary sources, independent BTM-deployment data, the named-analyst credibility, and post-publication price action. Companion to [[Longview thesis - Capstone Power+ (CGEH) is a 14x like Bloom Energy with microturbine plus ESP for AI data center BTM power]] (the bull) and [[Everyone Hates Poetry exits CGEH at 11.50 in Dec 2025 warning of retail pumps mis-stating cap structure and hyperscaler vs tier-2-3 framing]] (the credible peer's exit). Disposition: **NOT a buy at $11.60. Watchlist with explicit triggers** (real 8-K customer contract; NASDAQ S-1 filed; insider open-market buying; pullback to $7-9).

## Key Takeaways

- **The Longview thesis has at least eight verifiable factual gaps the SEC filings expose.** Legal name is *Capstone Energy+, Inc.* not "Capstone Power+" (rebrand effective 4/30/26 8-K). Basic shares outstanding are 30.55M per the S-3 dated 4/15/26 — thesis claims 35M, materially overstating it. Common to Monarch was at $4.50, not $5.00. The S-3 reserves 24M Series A conversion shares (vs 16M initial) → potential 58.4M FD if PIK runs full course. Listing covenant penalty triggers at month 18 not month 12 (+200bps step-up, max 13% PIK). $25.3M Exit Roll Up Notes mature December 2026 at 11.31% — refi risk thesis omits. ~$14M extinguishment loss on Goldman OpCo redemption hits Q4 FY26 reported earnings. Equity plan pool doubled from 4M to 7M shares on 4/23/26. Stockholders' deficit is **−$63.3M** at 12/31/25. None of these are deal-breakers individually; together they signal the thesis's capital-structure modeling is loose. [[Everyone Hates Poetry exits CGEH at 11.50 in Dec 2025 warning of retail pumps mis-stating cap structure and hyperscaler vs tier-2-3 framing]] called out exactly this category of error in December 2025 before exiting.

- **Zero data-center customer contract has been disclosed in any 8-K.** The bull case's binary catalyst (Base $34.82 / Bull $137.95) is conditional on a 100 MW DC deal closing. CEO Canino's actual words on the Q3 FY26 call were *"if we get this 100-megawatt order, what does this look like, and how fast can we ramp up?"* — scenario planning, not a deal in negotiation. No customer named. The "magnitudes, magnitudes above C&I pipeline" claim is real but undisclosed in MW. Captured in [[CGEH Q3 FY2026 earnings transcript - Canino says if we get this 100 MW order on conditional DC pipeline, $26.8M revenue +33pct YoY, 38.9pct gross margin]].

- **Independent BTM-deployment data has CGEH conspicuously absent from the leaderboard.** Real wins shipping or contracted today: [[Solaris Energy Infrastructure (SEI)]] 3.1 GW pipeline by 2029 (three signed long-term hyperscaler contracts); VoltaGrid 2.3 GW Oracle Texas modular gas + 1.5 GW INNIO order; ProEnergy 21 × 48-MW aero turbines for 2 NA DC projects; xAI Memphis Colossus 422 MW (VoltaGrid mobile gas); Crusoe Abilene Stargate Phase 1 360 MW (GEV LM2500); Meta New Albany 200 MW (Williams gas); and the canonical 2026 BTM proof point — [[Project Jupiter - Oracle picks Bloom Energy SOFC fuel cells over gas turbines for 2.8 GW MSA with 1.2 GW initial firm at Dona Ana NM AI data center campus]] for 2.45 GW [[Bloom Energy (BE)]] SOFC. CGEH has the Microgrids 4 AI MOU (Oct 2025, no orders) and a Utah hospitality C800. [[Bloom Energy (BE)]] is shipping in 55 days vs 90-day target; CGEH is pitching. The most expensive datapoint for the bull case is that **Oracle SWAPPED OUT previously-planned gas turbines and diesel** at Project Jupiter — this is a precedent any combustion-based BTM vendor (CGEH included) has to overcome on emissions and water, not just price.

- **The "Longview Research" attribution is almost certainly a promoter, not a credentialed analyst.** @Longviewres was created **2026-04-22**, twelve days before the thesis published. 748 followers, generic AI-generated avatar, paid-blue-check (cheap legitimacy theater, NOT verified/affiliated), no linked Substack/website/contact, no pinned tweet, zero Wayback archive history. Bio reads "Technology researcher with a focus on AI power and infrastructure." This pattern-matches the canonical OTC pump template: pseudonymous account, multibagger framing tied to a hot adjacent comp ([[Bloom Energy (BE)]]), disclosed long, name already up 17-bagger. The most credible established CGEH bull — [[Everyone Hates Poetry exits CGEH at 11.50 in Dec 2025 warning of retail pumps mis-stating cap structure and hyperscaler vs tier-2-3 framing|Everyone Hates Poetry Research]] (5,341 X followers, 5-yr track record) — exited at $11.50 in December 2025 with an explicit warning about exactly this kind of retail pump mis-stating cap structure. That exit price is roughly today's price ($11.60).

- **Floor-case math at $11.60 is approximately at fair value, not below it.** With 30.55M basic / 50M FD-initial / 58.4M FD-fully-reserved, and ~$11.7M net cash post-Monarch, EV at $11.60 is ~$354-680M depending on share-count basis. EV/FY28E sell-side $156.9M revenue: 2.3x basic / 3.7x FD-initial / 4.3x FD-reserved. The realistic no-DC peer set ([[FuelCell Energy (FCEL)]] 3.5x, [[Bloom Energy (BE)]] 13x with hyperscaler optionality, [[Solaris Energy Infrastructure (SEI)]] 6.2x with 3.1 GW backlog) brackets CGEH at 3-4x without a DC contract — implying $10-14 fair value. **The asymmetry the thesis claimed at $9.70 has substantially compressed by $11.60.** Bear case is here; Base case requires a 100 MW DC contract that is not in 8-K disclosure or hiring footprint or supplier capacity-expansion or insider buying behavior; Bull case requires hyperscaler win that the BTM leaderboard suggests is going to fuel cells or larger turbines, not microturbines.

- **The investible thesis is still "wait for a real catalyst" not "buy the dip on retail FOMO."** Triggers worth waiting for: (a) 8-K disclosing a named DC customer contract with revenue commitment >$50M; (b) NASDAQ/NYSE S-1 or initial-listing application filed on EDGAR (covenant deadline March 2027); (c) CEO or director Form 4 open-market purchase (would signal information asymmetry); (d) pullback to $7-9, restoring Bear-case downside protection. Q4 FY2026 earnings (likely May-June 2026) will absorb the ~$14M Goldman extinguishment loss — a one-time hit that may compress the multiple temporarily; that's a structurally cleaner entry than chasing $11.60. Alternative path: pair-trade long [[Bloom Energy (BE)]] (proven hyperscaler exposure) + short or avoid CGEH (unproven hyperscaler exposure) — captures the BTM theme without the binary contract risk.

### BTM market and competitive position

Independent market sizing supports the BTM thesis structurally but disconfirms CGEH's specific positioning:

- **Wood Mackenzie** (April 2026): 90 GW total US power demand growth by 2030 of which 59 GW from data centers — 21% upward revision from April 2025
- **EPRI** (Feb 2026): DC load reaches 9-17% of US generation by 2030 (~380-790 TWh range); ~one-third of currently-planned DCs are behind-the-meter, gas dominant
- **BloombergNEF**: AI DC demand 40 GW (2025) → 106 GW (2035), revised up 36% from April 2025
- **McKinsey/Goldman**: 25-50 GW of BTM by 2030, 30% share of incremental DC load — supports thesis range

But the **vendor mix favors fuel cells and larger turbines, not microturbines**. From the captured [[SemiAnalysis maps the AI behind-the-meter power solution stack - VoltaGrid leads with 2.3 GW Oracle Texas, Bloom wins Project Jupiter, Crusoe Abilene proves GEV LM2500]] piece and Wave 1 sourcing:

- Frame turbines ([[GE Vernova (GEV)]], Siemens Energy, MHI) booked through 2028
- Aeroderivatives (LM2500/LM6000) winning Crusoe-class deals — GEV LM2500XPRESS at Crusoe Abilene (~360 MW)
- Reciprocating engines ([[Cummins (CMI)]], [[Caterpillar (CAT)]] Solar Turbines, INNIO/Jenbacher, Wärtsilä) have available uncommitted fleet capacity — fastest-to-power competitor
- Fuel cells ([[Bloom Energy (BE)]]): largest single BTM award disclosed (Project Jupiter 2.8 GW MSA), 24-month time-to-power, 55-day delivery on first units; FuelCell Energy ([[FuelCell Energy (FCEL)]]) sympathy-rallied +32% on BE's Q1 print
- Microturbines (Capstone): positioning is CHP / 65 kW–1 MW range, 800VDC AI Power Block on slideware. **Notably absent from any disclosed gigawatt-scale RFP win.** Public deployments cited are colo/CHP and a Utah hospitality property.

Microgrids 4 AI (the announced 800VDC partner): founded 2025, 6 LinkedIn-discoverable employees, 295 followers, zero commercial wins, zero shipping product disclosed. Two startups announcing each other.
