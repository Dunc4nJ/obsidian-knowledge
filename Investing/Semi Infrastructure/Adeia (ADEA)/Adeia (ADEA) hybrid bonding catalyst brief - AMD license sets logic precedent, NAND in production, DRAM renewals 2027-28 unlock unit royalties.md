# Adeia (ADEA) hybrid bonding catalyst brief — AMD license sets logic precedent, NAND in production, DRAM renewals 2027–28 unlock unit royalties

Independent catalyst brief from Pennycheck dated 2026-05-11, synthesizing Roth Capital's April 14, 2026 ADEA note (Scott Searle), the Q1 2026 earnings call (May 4), and CEO Paul Davis's commentary at the Roth 30th Annual Conference (March 2026). Walks through what hybrid bonding is, the IP [[Adeia (ADEA)]] controls, the AMD license precedent, and the named pipeline of next licensees across logic, NAND, and DRAM.

## Key Takeaways

- **The AMD license is "a seminal event," not a one-off.** Roth's framing: AMD's March 2026 multi-year license (signed four months after [[Adeia (ADEA)]] sued in W.D. Texas + ITC over ten patents) "underscored the technical validation, adoption trends, and monetization aspects of HB for deployments of logic… in data centers and AI applications." Roth quantifies AMD royalties at ~$2M/Q low-end ramping to **$5–10M+/Q** as 3D V-Cache spreads across the [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]] portfolio (Ryzen X3D + EPYC X-variants on Microsoft Azure / Cloudflare). Implication: the lawsuit-then-license playbook is now a working monetization pattern for the rest of the logic pipeline.

- **The next logic license is named — Roth calls Broadcom for 2026.** "Products commercialize in 2026 led by [[Broadcom (AVGO)]] (3.5D XDSiP packaging), as well as [[Intel (INTC)]] (Clearwater Forest server CPU on Foveros Direct), [[Marvell Technology (MRVL)]], and others." This is the most specific timing call in the sell-side coverage and would pull validation forward of the SK Hynix HBM ramp window. Davis adds [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] is "a matter of when, not if." Roth flags hyperscaler engagement (Amazon, Alphabet, Meta) as a parallel disclosed pipeline — unusual because hyperscalers don't typically license patents directly, suggesting Adeia may be pursuing direct licenses on internally-designed silicon rather than only routing through their ASIC partners.

- **NAND is already in production today** ([[Kioxia (285A.T)]] + [[SanDisk (SNDK)]], ~25%+ combined share, ~30% power reduction driving share gains), but the bigger NAND catalyst is **renewal economics**: [[Samsung Electronics (005930.KS)]], [[SK Hynix (000660.KS)]], and [[Micron (MU)]] together hold ~65% NAND share already licensed under pre-existing deals **with no per-unit royalties** — pre-2027 renewals "should further accelerate NAND-related revenue."

- **The DRAM renewal cycle in 2027–2028 is the structural step-change in the licensing model.** Roth: "over 90% of the DRAM is currently licensed for existing ADEA solutions… this predominantly entailed one-time fixed fees (paid in prior years) with the existing licenses expiring in 2027/28… renegotiated licenses will have meaningfully better economics tied to unit volumes/memory capacity." Davis confirms "key memory renewals around 2028." Translation: ~95% of the DRAM market (SK Hynix + Samsung + Micron) flips from fixed-fee to unit-based pricing into the HBM4e/5 ramp — the long-term Adeia target is **$500M annual licensing revenue** vs 2026E semis revenue of $40M, scaling to Roth's $77.5M (2027E, +94% y/y) and a TAM Roth pegs at $200M+ by 2030.

- **JEDEC HBM-thickness chatter is a "red herring."** Roth pushes back hard on investor concerns that relaxed HBM thickness specs threaten HB adoption: "this completely misses the point… HB delivers increased throughput and lower latency, as well as the paramount lower power and better thermal characteristics, thereby reducing cooling costs… HB adoption timelines in HBM DRAM remain unchanged." Power efficiency (30%+ reduction) is the real driver — meaningful when datacenters are projected from 4% of US electricity (today) to 12%+ by 2028. Risk to track: AMD deal settled before Markman, so the asserted patents have not been judicially construed; Davis is also stepping down for health reasons with a successor search through Q4 2026.

> **SECTION 1**
> ## **What Hybrid Bonding Does (Plain Terms)**
>
> Modern chips are no longer just one slab of silicon. To get more performance without making the chip larger or hotter, manufacturers stack multiple chips vertically — memory on top of logic, cache on top of CPU cores. The way those stacked chips physically connect determines how fast data can move between them, how much power the system uses, and how much heat builds up.
>
> **The old way vs. hybrid bonding**
>
> Traditional packaging uses tiny solder bumps spaced 25–40 microns apart — microscopic stilts that take up vertical space, hold heat in, and limit interconnect density. Hybrid bonding replaces this with direct copper-to-copper fusion at sub-1-micron pitch. The result: roughly **200x more electrical connections per square millimeter**, much shorter signal paths, far better heat transfer, and chip stacks that are physically thinner.
>
> **Roth's technical framing of the value proposition**
>
> _Hybrid bonding permits wafers and dies to be bonded with fine pitch (process geometries down to 1 µm) for 3D electrical interconnects at room temperature. Consequently, this is a highly repeatable (a high yield) process that enables increasing device performance (bandwidth ior throughput) with lower power (30%+ less power consumption) and lower costs. This is key to enabling vertically stacked, next-gen high-density memory structures (2.5D/3D NAND and HBM4e/5 DRAM) and chiplet architectures (logic with increasing on-board memory components)._
>
> — **ROTH Capital Partners**, _ADEA: Tech Talk Takeaways... All Roads Still Lead to Hybrid Bonding; Buy_, Scott W. Searle, CFA (April 14, 2026, p.3).
>
> **Why the power efficiency matters at AI scale**
>
> _We reiterate power consumption savings in excess of 30%, which are meaningful when considering Data Centers were estimated to consume 4%+ of electricity in the US and could ramp to 12%+ by 2028. … power efficiency, particularly in the data center, should propel HB adoption._
>
> — **ROTH Capital Partners**, _ADEA: Tech Talk Takeaways... All Roads Still Lead to Hybrid Bonding; Buy_, Scott W. Searle, CFA (April 14, 2026, p.1, 5).

> **SECTION 2**
> ## **The IP Adeia Owns**
>
> Adeia's hybrid bonding patent portfolio traces back to **Ziptronix**, the company that invented direct bond interconnect. Ziptronix was acquired in 2015 (~$39M, by Tessera/Invensas), which became part of Xperi Corporation. On October 1, 2022, Xperi separated its product business; the IP licensing entity was renamed **Adeia Inc.** (NASDAQ: ADEA) and retained ownership of the full semiconductor patent portfolio.
>
> **Three core patent families**
>
> - **DBI** — wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding, in volume production in CMOS image sensors and 3D NAND.
> - **DBI Ultra** — die-to-wafer hybrid bonding for larger dies (DRAM, CPUs, GPUs, SoCs). Enables 8-, 12-, and 16-high chip stacks.
> - **ZiBond** — wafer-to-wafer oxide-only direct bonding, the process foundation.
>
> **Portfolio scale**
>
> Adeia disclosed **~13,750 total patent assets** as of Q1 2026, up from approximately 9,500 at separation in 2022. Of those, **over 1,100 active patents** sit in hybrid bonding specifically — spanning methods, materials, processes, chemistries, and integrated circuit structures.
>
> **Roth on the depth of the bonding portfolio**
>
> _HB dates back to the 2015 acquisition of Ziptronics ($39M by Tessera), and today stands at a portfolio of over 1,100+ patents around methods, process, chemistries, etc. that deliver improved performance (speed, latency), power, thermal, cost and form factor benefits over existing legacy interconnect and packaging solutions._
>
> — **ROTH Capital Partners**, _ADEA: Tech Talk Takeaways... All Roads Still Lead to Hybrid Bonding; Buy_, Scott W. Searle, CFA (April 14, 2026, p.1).
>
> **Adeia's own framing of the moat**
>
> _Adeia continues to be the technology pioneer and IP leader in hybrid bonding that offers technology transfer to their customers who want an accelerated path to manufacturing. … The company's portfolio — now over 1,100 active patent assets in bonding — spans materials, processes, chemistries, integrated circuit structures and system architectures, providing the foundational building blocks for the industry's hybrid bonding ecosystem._
>
> — Adeia Tech Talk Recap, _Hybrid Bonding & Direct-to-Chip Cooling with Roth Capital Partners_ (Nov 5, 2025).

> **SECTION 3**
> ## **The AMD Deal — Connected Directly to Their CPUs**
>
> AMD's flagship CPU products use hybrid bonding to physically stack cache memory on top of compute cores. The technology is the engineering basis for **3D V-Cache**, which powers Ryzen X3D consumer chips (5800X3D, 7800X3D, 7950X3D, 9800X3D, 9950X3D) and EPYC X-variant server chips (Milan-X, Genoa-X, Turin-X) deployed by hyperscalers like Microsoft Azure and Cloudflare.
>
> **Timeline**
>
> - **Nov 3, 2025:** Adeia files two patent infringement lawsuits in W.D. Texas (Midland) plus parallel ITC complaint. Ten patents asserted — seven hybrid bonding, three advanced process node. (Tom's Hardware)
> - **Mar 9, 2026:** AMD signs multi-year license. All Texas litigation dismissed. ITC case dropped. Financial terms confidential. (Investing.com)
>
> **Roth on the strategic significance — "a seminal event"**
>
> _The Advanced Micro Devices (AMD-Buy; Desilva) deal was a seminal event as it underscored the technical validation, adoption trends, and monetization aspects of HB for deployments of logic (processors) in data centers and AI applications. We believe this is a precursor to other logic deals as products commercialize in 2026 led by Broadcom (AVGO-NC), as well as Intel (INTC–Neutral, DeSilva), Marvel (MRVL–Buy, DeSilva) and others._
>
> — **ROTH Capital Partners**, _ADEA: Tech Talk Takeaways... All Roads Still Lead to Hybrid Bonding; Buy_, Scott W. Searle, CFA (April 14, 2026, p.1).
>
> **Roth's AMD royalty estimate — quantifies the ramp**
>
> _ADEA recently announced that it entered into a multi-year licensing agreement with AMD … we believe that the economics are favorable to ADEA (in-line with expectations before litigation) and provide solid comfort to the 2026 outlook._ _**We estimate that at the low end of the range quarterly royalty payments could be ~$2M, before ramping to $5–10M+/Q over the next several years as hybrid bonding is adopted more widely across the AMD product portfolio.**_
>
> — **ROTH Capital Partners**, _ADEA: Tech Talk Takeaways... All Roads Still Lead to Hybrid Bonding; Buy_, Scott W. Searle, CFA (April 14, 2026, p.5).
>
> **Read:** Roth's low-end of $2M/Q implies $8M annualized; the ramped $5–10M+/Q range implies $20–40M+ annualized just from AMD. AMD was a >10% customer in Q1 2026 partly on retroactive royalties, normalizing toward this range going forward.

> **Roth on NAND adoption and performance gains**
>
> _Within high density memory, HB is finding adoption in NAND (at 200+ [layers]) and HBM DRAM (HBM4e/5 at 16/20+ layers). Today, within 3D NAND,_ _**Kioxia and Sandisk (SNDK-NC) utilize HB, which appears to be driving better performance (including 30%+ power reduction) and market share gains.**_

> — **ROTH Capital Partners**, _ADEA: Tech Talk Takeaways... All Roads Still Lead to Hybrid Bonding; Buy_, Scott W. Searle, CFA (April 14, 2026, p.1).

> **SECTION 7**
> ## **SK Hynix & the DRAM Renewal Cycle (2027–2028)**
>
> SK Hynix licensed Adeia's DBI Ultra in **February 2020** including a technology transfer for next-generation memory. SK Hynix, Samsung, and Micron together control over 95% of the DRAM market. Per Roth, all three are **already licensed** on existing fixed-fee terms expiring in **2027–2028** — and renewal at unit-based economics is the structural step-change in Adeia's licensing model.
>
> **Roth on the DRAM renewal economics**
>
> _HBM4e/5 will be the key driver to hybrid bonding adoption in DRAM. … Importantly,_ _**over 90% of the DRAM is currently licensed for existing ADEA solutions, i.e., WLP (wafer-level packaging). However, this predominantly entailed one-time fixed fees (paid in prior years) with the existing licenses expiring in 2027/28.**_ _We believe renegotiated licenses will have_ _**meaningfully better economics tied to unit volumes/memory capacity.**_ _We expect this will translate to immediately higher licensing fees when these renewal agreements are consummated._
>
> — **ROTH Capital Partners**, _ADEA: Tech Talk Takeaways... All Roads Still Lead to Hybrid Bonding; Buy_, Scott W. Searle, CFA (April 14, 2026, p.4).
>
> **Roth on JEDEC noise as a "red herring"**
>
> _Investor concerns surfaced surrounding the adoption of HB in next gen HBM memory. Specifically, the article referenced relaxing of the spec for HBM thickness…_ _**This completely misses the point of why HB adoption is occurring; performance, not height.**_ _More specifically, HB delivers increased throughput and lower latency, as well as the paramount lower power and better thermal characteristics, thereby reducing cooling costs. …_ _**The bottom line is that HB adoption timelines in HBM DRAM remain unchanged.**_

> — **ROTH Capital Partners**, _ADEA: Tech Talk Takeaways... All Roads Still Lead to Hybrid Bonding; Buy_, Scott W. Searle, CFA (April 14, 2026, p.4–5).

> **Davis on the HBM ramp timing — Roth Conference (March 2026)**

>
> — Markets Daily, Roth 30th Annual Conference coverage (March 23, 2026).

> Next logic licensees on the AMD precedent — **Intel (Clearwater Forest / Foveros Direct), Marvell, NVIDIA**. Roth flags **Amazon, Alphabet, Meta** as active hyperscaler engagements. Consumer electronics / mobile silicon adoption per Davis: "expanding beyond data centers into consumer devices." Roth Semis revenue forecast: **$40M (2026E)** → **$77.5M (2027E)**, +94% y/y.

> **STRUCTURAL · HBM4E / 2027–2028**
>
> **The DRAM renewal cycle** — SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron existing licenses expire 2027/28. Per Roth: "over 90% of the DRAM is currently licensed … this predominantly entailed one-time fixed fees … renegotiated licenses will have meaningfully better economics tied to unit volumes/memory capacity." Davis: "key memory renewals around 2028." Roth TAM: **>$200M+ by 2030**. Long-term Adeia target: **$500M annual licensing revenue.**

> **OPTIONAL UPSIDE · RapidCool (DTC Cooling)**
>
> Roth: "RapidCool's DTC adoption appears to be 'when, not if' given its performance benefits." 70% thermal resistance reduction, 2–3x power density. Cooling is ~40% of DC power consumption. **Commercialization 2028+. Not in Roth or Pennycheck base case** — pure optionality.
