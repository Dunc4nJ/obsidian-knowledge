# ATOM imminent DOE Power America award per Joe Dab - 15-month Sandia GaN-on-Si testing validates MST at $350M mcap

## Key Takeaways

- **DOE/Sandia validation is the catalyst, not the dollar amount.** Joe expects [[Atomera (ATOM)]] to win a Power America award within days; the actual funding is small but the imprint of a Honeywell-operated, DOE-funded national lab "encouraging" Atomera to apply is what derisks the stock at a ~$350M market cap with two years of cash on hand.
- **Three independent third parties have stress-tested MST for GaN before this award.** 15+ months at Sandia/CINT for GaN-on-silicon; Belgian RF house Incize for GaN-on-Si RF characterization; and inclusion in Synopsys ([[Synopsys (SNPS)]]) simulation software for both GaN Power and GaN RF — Joe reads this stack of validations as "all testing went extremely well."
- **GaN may be the fastest mover, but the MST platform is wider than GaN.** Joe still flags definitive Gate-All-Around data and engagements with two of the three large memory makers as parallel catalysts — the GaN award is just the first one likely to print in print-by-print fashion.
- **Position framing matters: this is sized alongside [[Vicor (VICR)]], [[Micron (MU)]], and DRAM.** Joe puts ATOM in his "large positions" bucket with $40+ long-run upside and $12-15 in the next few months, anchoring the swing in DOE validation rather than guidance.
- **Background context surfaced via attached screenshots:** Atomera's own Q4 2025 transcript confirms their first commercial GaN-on-Si MST customer is running wafers (moving ahead of in-house Sandia work); the Oct 2024 Sandia/CINT press release establishes the lab relationship; the July 2025 Incize press release covers RF and Power; the Nov 24 2025 Semiconductor Today article fixes the **proposal selection notification date at on or about 18 May 2026** for 24-month projects.

#### Image 2 — Atomera press release, October 8, 2024 (Sandia/CINT collaboration)

[Image — screenshot of atomera.com press release page; transcribed verbatim below. Author emphasis: "Published: October 8, 2024" appears highlighted.]

> **Atomera Collaborates With the Center for Integrated Nanotechnologies (CINT) at Sandia National Laboratories to Validate MST's Ability to Address GaN Manufacturing Challenges**
>
> **Published: October 8, 2024**
>
> *CINT's state-of-the-art facilities will enable Atomera to quickly evaluate the effectiveness of GaN transistors utilizing MST*
>
> LOS GATOS, Calif.–(BUSINESS WIRE)– Atomera Incorporated (NASDAQ: ATOM), a semiconductor materials and technology licensing company, today announced a user project at the Center for Integrated Nanotechnologies (CINT) at Sandia National Laboratories, a Department of Energy, Office of Science, Nanoscale Science Research Center, to address the challenges of growing Gallium Nitride (GaN) films on Silicon (Si). This project aims to create the world's first GaN transistors and test data from wafers employing Atomera's Mears Silicon Technology™ (MST®). The effort will build upon improvements already observed at the materials level in GaN/MST on Silicon wafers.
>
> "Atomera's MST represents a tremendous opportunity to improve GaN on Si manufacturing and provide speed, efficiency and cost-saving benefits to a wide range of industries including electronics, RF/microwave electronics and even MicroLEDs. This user project will test the effectiveness of the MST solution quickly using CINT's highly specialized tools and technology and give Atomera access to our team of scientists and researchers," said Jeffrey Nelson, director of CINT.

#### Image 3 — Incize × Atomera press release, July 9, 2025 (GaN-on-Si for RF + Power)

[Image — screenshot of atomera.com investor news page; transcribed verbatim below.]

> **Incize and Atomera Announce Strategic Collaboration to Advance GaN-on-Si Technology for Next-Gen RF and Power Devices**
>
> July 9, 2025
>
> LOUVAIN-LA-NEUVE, Belgium & LOS GATOS, Calif.–(BUSINESS WIRE)– Incize, a Belgian cutting-edge semiconductor characterization and modeling company, and Atomera (Nasdaq: ATOM), a pioneer in advanced semiconductor materials, today announced a strategic collaboration to enhance Gallium Nitride on Silicon (GaN-on-Si) technologies.
>
> This press release features multimedia. View the full release here: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250709351477/en/
>
> In certain applications, traditional silicon is hitting its performance ceiling. GaN-on-Si offers a scalable, cost-effective alternative to taking advantage of GaN's performance with silicon's manufacturability. Combining Atomera's Mears Silicon Technology™ (MST®) with Incize's advanced characterization should accelerate the path to volume production of next-generation RF and power devices.
>
> This partnership brings together Atomera's proprietary MST® — a quantum-engineered, ultra-thin silicon film that improves transistor performance, energy efficiency and reliability — with Incize's state-of-the-art characterization platforms, covering substrate trap analysis, noise, linearity, thermal effects and RF performance from DC to mmWave.
>
> The joint program will concentrate on positioning GaN-on-Si device performance for high-frequency and high-power applications, with target markets in wireless infrastructure (5G/6G), satellite communications and advanced power electronics.
>
> "We are thrilled to collaborate with Atomera, whose MST® technology has already demonstrated promising advantages in silicon-based devices," said Dr. Mostafa Emam, CEO of Incize. "By leveraging our advanced RF technology enablement capabilities and applying them to Atomera's innovation, we aim to unlock new frontiers of performance, efficiency and reliability in GaN-on-Si platforms."
>
> "Incize's track record with GaN-on-Si and their superb measurement and modeling capabilities make them an ideal partner," said Scott Bibaud, CEO of Atomera. "Together, we are exploring how MST® can be harnessed to propel compound semiconductor devices forward and deliver truly groundbreaking solutions to our customers."

#### Image 4 — Semiconductor Today, November 24, 2025 (PowerAmerica RFP timing)

[Image — screenshot of semiconductor-today.com article page; transcribed verbatim below. Author emphasis: "The expected date for notifying proposed projects of selection is **18 May 2026**, prior to project negotiations." is highlighted in the source.]

> **PowerAmerica issues request for proposals for 24-month projects**
>
> 24 November 2025
>
> PowerAmerica — a public-private research initiative established in 2014 as the US Department of Energy (DOE)'s first Clean Energy Manufacturing Innovation Institute — has issued a request for proposals for projects lasting up to 24 months, focused on the development of advanced wide-bandgap power semiconductor technologies, power electronics assemblies, and packaging and manufacturing processes with the potential to improve performance and lower cost. Demonstration of wide-bandgap (WBG) manufacturing processes and/or devices in high-volume, commercially viable power electronic applications is also desired. Technology Readiness Levels should be in the range TRL 4–6.
>
> The submission deadlines are 12 December for concept papers and 13 February for full applications. **The expected date for notifying proposed projects of selection is 18 May 2026, prior to project negotiations.**
>
> Funding comprises $9m from PowerAmerica in industrial projects plus $9m in performer cost-match; $3.2m in performer cost-match; and $900,000 in member-initiated projects plus $450,000 in member cost-match — an anticipated total of $26m in WBG projects to accelerate commercialization of silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN) and power electronics.
>
> Where applicants are making multiple proposals, industry and National Lab recipients may receive up to $1m total, and university recipients may receive up to $230,000 total.
