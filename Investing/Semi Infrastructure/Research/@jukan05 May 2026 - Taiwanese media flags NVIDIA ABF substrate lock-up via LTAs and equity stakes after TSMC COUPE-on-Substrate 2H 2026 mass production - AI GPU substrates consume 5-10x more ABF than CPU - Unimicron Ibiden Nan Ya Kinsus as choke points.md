# @jukan05 May 2026 — Taiwanese media flags NVIDIA ABF substrate lock-up via LTAs and equity stakes after TSMC COUPE-on-Substrate 2H 2026 mass production — AI GPU substrates consume 5–10x more ABF than CPU — Unimicron Ibiden Nan Ya Kinsus as choke points

Short point-in-time signal tweet from Jukan (Korean memory-cycle commentator) relaying — and quote-tweeting his own earlier same-day translation of — Taiwanese trade-press analysis that the next supply chain choke point [[Nvidia (NVDA)|NVIDIA]] is poised to lock up is **ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) substrates**. The mechanism mirrors the past playbook for CoWoS and HBM: long-term agreements, prepayments, strategic collaborations, equity investments. Jukan flags he is positioned (long ABF makers — implicit "great for me, though lol"), and his prior captures in the vault establish him as a credible Korean-memory-cycle voice with edge on Asian supply chain.

## Key takeaways

- **TSMC's "COUPE on Substrate" enters 2H 2026 mass production** — TSMC has confirmed the Compact Universal Photonic Engine (COUPE) integration solution targets 2H 2026 mass production. The industry read is that this is not merely an optical-comms upgrade but a signal that **AI supply-chain competition is expanding beyond advanced process and advanced packaging into the ABF substrate + CPO integration domain.** [[TSMC (TSM)|TSMC]]'s photonics packaging push pulls ABF demand forward.

- **Vera Rubin raises the substrate bar** — As [[Nvidia (NVDA)|NVIDIA]]'s next-generation Vera Rubin platform continues raising integration level across AI GPUs, HBM, and ultra-high-speed networking interconnects, **the importance of high-end substrates is set to grow rapidly**. The Taiwanese-media framing is that Vera Rubin is the gating product behind the substrate lock-up calculus.

- **The lock-up mechanism is explicit and pre-emptive** — Per industry participants cited: "if CPO establishes itself as the mainstream AI server architecture going forward, NVIDIA may move to preemptively secure high-end ABF substrate capacity through long-term agreements (LTAs), prepayments, and strategic partnerships." Framed explicitly as **a strategic move aimed at preventing a repeat of past CoWoS and HBM supply shortages** — the same playbook NVDA ran with [[Unimicron (3037.TW)|Unimicron]] / [[Ibiden (4062.T)|Ibiden]]-grade carriers historically and with HBM at SK Hynix more recently.

- **The 5–10x consumption inflection is the most concrete data point** — "Compared with conventional CPU substrates, substrates for AI GPUs and ASICs feature significantly larger area and substantially higher layer counts, **expanding ABF material consumption by 5–10x**." This is the per-unit demand multiplier that makes high-end ABF supply structurally tight independent of unit growth; layered on top of AI GPU/ASIC/networking-chip unit growth, the supply/demand structure for high-end ABF substrates is expected to "remain tight for an extended period."

- **Choke-point names**: Taiwanese leaders [[Unimicron (3037.TW)|Unimicron (3037.TW)]] and [[Nan Ya PCB (8046.TW)|Nan Ya PCB (8046.TW)]] and [[Kinsus Interconnect (3189.TW)|Kinsus Interconnect (3189.TW)]] (the IC-substrate arm of Pegatron, with ~NT$23.5B 2025–2027 capex plan adding ~10M ABF substrates/month to ~50M units total); Japanese leader [[Ibiden (4062.T)|Ibiden (4062.T)]]. Note: **Shinko Electric Industries (6967.T) was delisted from the TSE Prime Market on 2025-06-06** following the JIP-led take-private — no longer publicly tradeable as a pure-play ABF vehicle. The peer commentary in-thread widens the choke point: @tailrisked: "PCBs substrates interposers. What's left? CCL, copper, the drilling machines!" — implying the next leg of the supply-chain lock-up is the upstream CCL (copper clad laminate), copper, and PCB drilling equipment layers.

- **Jukan's positioning tell** — Jukan's "If that happens, it would be great for me, though lol" is the operative signal. He has not previously been a vocal ABF analyst in the vault; he flagging it now while declaring an existing long is the kind of point-in-time positioning data that motivates capture. His prior captures (Korean memory cycle, [[GSR via jukan05 - InP bottleneck on photonics Q1 2026 conviction list - 4 layer reasoning chain and direct names AAOI POET vs LITE.md|GSR InP bottleneck note]] in Photonics, the [[KIS memory report reframes memory as the GPU utilization lever with Hynix peers Samsung Micron and equipment names DISCO Hanmi TOK Soulbrain.md|KIS memory report]] in Memory) establish his pattern: he surfaces supply-chain choke points before they are consensus.

- **Cross-link to glass substrates** — This commentary lands adjacent to the existing [[PhotonCap May 2026 - 15-company glass substrate cycle map frames 2026 as pilot-qualification phase with 2027-2030 volume ramp across AI accelerators HBM4 interposer and CPO|PhotonCap glass-substrate cycle map]] which frames 2026 as pilot-qualification with 2027–2030 volume ramp. Glass substrates are a parallel/competing substrate technology — if ABF gets locked up via NVDA LTAs, glass becomes the relief valve at the high end, but the qualification timing means ABF tightness is the binding constraint for 2026–2027 specifically.
