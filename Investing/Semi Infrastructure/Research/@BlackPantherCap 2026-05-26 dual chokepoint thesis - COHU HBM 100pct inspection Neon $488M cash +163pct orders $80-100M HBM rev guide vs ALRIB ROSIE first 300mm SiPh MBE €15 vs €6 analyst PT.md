# @BlackPantherCap 2026-05-26 — dual chokepoint thesis — COHU HBM 100% inspection (Neon, $488M cash, +163% YoY orders, $80-100M HBM rev guide) vs ALRIB ROSIE first 300mm SiPh MBE (€15 vs €6 analyst PT, Year-0 ramp 2026)

Black Panther Capital pitches **two AI-supply-chain equipment chokepoints** the market hasn't priced — both pattern-matched on the $AEHR / $AXTI / $SIVE chokepoint-trade archetype. The unifying frame: equipment moats that the sell-side is modeling under the wrong multiple bucket. Author has **not yet taken positions** in either — both on watchlist, considering adding one to short-term portfolio.

## Key Takeaways

### Chokepoint 1 — [[Cohu (COHU)]]: HBM 100% inspection monopoly via Neon platform

- **The physical stakes**: modern NVDA GPU = logic die at the bottom + 4-8 HBM memory dies bonded on top, thousands of through-silicon vias + copper micro-pillars (smaller than a human hair). **One defective pillar kills a $40,000 AI GPU package. No buffer. No workaround. The whole unit is scrap.**
- **The supply constraint that forces 100% inspection (not sample)**: **HBM sold out through at least 2027; no significant new capacity until late 2027.** Every die that gets made must reach a GPU. HBM3e → HBM4 makes dies larger + micro-pillar density higher → inspection complexity rises, not falls.
- **The moat**: **Cohu's Neon platform performs full 6-sided optical inspection with proprietary AI-trained defect-recognition trained specifically on each customer's device architecture.** Customer-specific training is the lock-in: "You can't buy a competitor's system and retrain it in a quarter. The switching cost is measured in years." Currently **qualified at a leading US memory manufacturer**.
- **The numbers** (per author): **$488M cash (no dilution risk); Q1 2026 orders +163% YoY; $750M pipeline + 5 customers in active qualification; HBM revenue guidance raised from $15M → $80–100M in a single year.** *Note: see @SlayherVag51673 pushback below — the $750M pipeline figure may be Eclipse test-handler-platform pipeline rather than Neon inspection; verify before sizing.*
- **The re-rate frame**: market prices COHU as a **test equipment cycle recovery (3-4× EV/Revenue)**; author's correct frame is **AI infra bottleneck (7-10× EV/Revenue)**. Author's analogy: "potential **[[Ouster (OUST)]]-look-alike setup**" — 3-5× from here as the recategorization plays out.

### Chokepoint 2 — [[Riber (ALRIB.PA)]]: ROSIE — first MBE platform for 300mm SiPh production

- **The physical stakes**: copper wires hit physical limits inside AI data centers — heat, signal loss, power draw. **Silicon photonics** is the industry answer (lasers built directly onto chips, transmitting data as light). **Co-packaged optics (CPO)** embeds those lasers into AI switches. **Forecast CPO penetration: near-zero today → 35% of all optical networking by 2030.**
- **The moat that nobody's pricing**: every laser is grown via **molecular beam epitaxy (MBE)** — atomic-layer-precision under ultra-high vacuum. **The entire industry's MBE infrastructure was built for 150mm and 200mm wafers. Silicon photonics runs on 300mm production lines.** Until Riber built **ROSIE — Riber Oxide on Silicon Epitaxy — the first MBE platform engineered specifically for 300mm SiPh production lines. No other equipment company makes this.** *Note: see @produdee9 pushback below — Veeco + imec announced a 300mm-HVM BTO process in January 2026; the "no other equipment company makes this" line may already be dated.*
- **The Year-0 timing signal**: first two ROSIE systems ordered in 2025. **ROSIE 2 — the dual-chamber production version — goes into manufacturing in 2026. This is Year 0 of the ramp.**
- **The mispricing gap**: **analyst consensus PT €6 vs current price €15+**. The gap exists because **analysts model Riber as a €40M scientific instruments company**. **Not one single sell-side model contains ROSIE as a separate revenue line.** Author's market frame: SiPh = **$17B market by 2035** vs Riber rev €40M, market cap €320M (~$340M USD). **If ROSIE becomes the production standard for 300mm SiPh the way MOCVD became the standard for LED manufacturing, both the revenue trajectory and the multiple re-rate from here.**
- **Asymmetric sizing**: author frames ALRIB as **the more asymmetric setup — potential ten-bagger**. Compare to COHU's safer 3-5× framing.
