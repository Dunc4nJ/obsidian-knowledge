# Compute

Companies and research covering compute capacity providers — companies that own racks of finished chips and rent compute time downstream of the chip designers in `Chips/`. Spans pure-play AI cloud / neocloud (e.g., CoreWeave, Nebius), Bitcoin miners pivoting to AI (e.g., Iris Energy), hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Amazon when relevant as compute capacity), datacenter REITs (Digital Realty, Equinix), and the power/cooling infrastructure that scales with AI buildout (Vertiv, Eaton). Distinguished from `Chips/` (the silicon designers whose products fill these datacenters) and `Semi Infrastructure/` (the foundries and equipment that manufacture those chips).
