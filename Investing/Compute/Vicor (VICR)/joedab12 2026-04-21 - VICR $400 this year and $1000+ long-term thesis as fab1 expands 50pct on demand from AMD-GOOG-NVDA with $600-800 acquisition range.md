# VICR $400 this year and $1000+ long-term thesis as fab1 expands 50pct on demand from AMD-GOOG-NVDA with $600-800 acquisition range

## Key Takeaways

- **Fab1 capacity expanded 50pct via a second building, not fab2.** [[Vicor (VICR)]] last quarter said fab1 was capable of $1b in revenue; the company has now moved the easiest manufacturing steps to a second building so total fab1 capacity is now $1.5b. Critically, this building is NOT fab2 — it is essentially an extension of fab1, meaning the $500M of incremental capacity is available *now*, not in 2028 when fab2 ships.

- **Fab2 plus a third-party licensing deal sit on top of fab1's $1.5b.** Fab2 is still in the works, and so is a 3rd-party licensing arrangement that would let Vicor expand capacity much faster and collect royalties without bearing capex itself. The licensing optionality matters because it converts a fab-capex business into a higher-margin IP-monetization business at the margin.

- **Customer math points to [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]] and [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]] as the two named ramps beyond Cerebras.** Vicor's CEO confirmed the new $1.5b capacity is enough to service a major OEM and hyperscaler on top of Cerebras (who they can name). Joe's process-of-elimination: it isn't [[Nvidia (NVDA)]] (NVDA is known to be able to "fill 2 fabs" when 1 fab = $1b revenue), and Amazon doesn't have GOOG's power-density requirements — leaving AMD (Helios) and Google (TPU) as the only candidates large enough to absorb the capacity.

- **NVIDIA is still to come, and is sized to fill 2 fabs by itself.** NVDA being able to "fill 2 fabs" of $1b each implies a $2b TAM from NVIDIA alone once Vicor's tech qualifies. Google is roughly half of NVIDIA's volume (filling the original $1b), and AMD Helios is roughly half of Google's TPU next year (the incremental 50pct = $500M). That sizing layers cleanly: NVDA $2b + GOOG $1b + AMD $500M + Cerebras = multi-fab demand stack.

- **Price targets: $400 this year, $1000+ long-term, with an acquisition tape at $600-800.** Joe expects acquisition is the likeliest outcome — NVDA wants the IP but works through preferred partners like ADI, TXN, MPWR, or Delta. The implied bear-case floor is whoever NVIDIA designates as buyer; the bull case requires Vicor to stay independent through the NVDA ramp.
