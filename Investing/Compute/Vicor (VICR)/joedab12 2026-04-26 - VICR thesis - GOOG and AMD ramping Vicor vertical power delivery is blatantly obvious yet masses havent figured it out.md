# joedab12 2026-04-26 - VICR thesis - GOOG and AMD ramping Vicor vertical power delivery is blatantly obvious yet masses havent figured it out

## Key Takeaways

- Joe's core thesis: $VICR ([[Vicor (VICR)]]) management has already told the market — across the Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 earnings calls — that [[Alphabet (GOOGL)]] and [[Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)]] are ramping Vicor Vertical Power Delivery (VPD) products, but the broader market is still waiting for sell-side (BofA, Goldman) to "officially" name the customers before re-rating the stock.
- The Q4 2025 transcript quote ("There's one in particular with tremendous opportunity in terms of volume. That one alone fill 2 fabs.") describes a single lead customer whose volume potential exceeds two Vicor fab footprints — Joe reads this as a direct reference to a hyperscaler ASIC engagement (Google TPU power-delivery being the most-discussed candidate among VICR longs).
- The Q4 2025 follow-up from Philip Davies (VP Global Sales & Marketing) confirms the next-step engagement model: Vicor's global FAE team is being deployed "in different locations where we have target hyperscalers and OEM chip companies located" — an active, multi-site customer-design-in motion, not exploratory pitches.
- The Q1 2026 transcript quote raises the Fab 1 capacity earmark from a ~$1B/yr run rate to **at least $1.5B**, with CEO Patrizio Vinciarelli confirming yes when asked whether the expanded capacity would be enough to satisfy "the OEM and the hyperscaler customers" mentioned in Q3 — explicit-on-the-record acknowledgement that named hyperscaler + OEM customers are real and capacity-driving.
- Buying-window framing: trend-up share price + management disclosure + missing sell-side coverage = period where retail / fundamental investors can re-rate ahead of consensus. Joe's rhetorical jab ("Are you guys really not smart enough to figure this out on your own?") is the standard "obvious-to-me, not-yet-to-the-tape" setup that recurs in concentrated VICR longs.
