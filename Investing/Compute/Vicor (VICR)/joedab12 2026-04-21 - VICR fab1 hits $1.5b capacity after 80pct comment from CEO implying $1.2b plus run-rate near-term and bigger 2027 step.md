# joedab12 2026-04-21 - VICR fab1 hits $1.5b capacity after 80pct comment from CEO implying $1.2b plus run-rate near-term and bigger 2027 step

## Key Takeaways

- **The 80pct "margin-of-error" rule from last call has to be re-anchored against the new $1.5b fab1 nameplate, not the old $1b.** On the prior earnings call founder-CEO Vinciarelli said fab1 is capable of $1b in product revenue running at full capacity by year-end, but told the Street to use 80pct of that ($800m) when modeling 2027 product revenue to leave margin for ramp slippage. With the [[Vicor (VICR)]] satellite-facility patchwork lifting fab1 nameplate to ~$1.5b (the 50pct expansion disclosed Apr 21), the same conservative 80pct discount now implies ~**$1.2b product revenue run-rate** — a 50pct bump to the conservative scenario, before IP royalties.
- **The 2027 step from this expansion is materially bigger than the sell-side currently models.** Any model still anchored to "$800m product + royalties" in 2027 is now 40-50pct light because it has not absorbed the satellite-facility throughput lift. The arithmetic is mechanical: 50pct more nameplate at the same utilization assumption = 50pct more revenue capacity, and full-utilization upside ($1.5b vs $1.2b at 80pct) compounds the gap. The IP royalty stream sits on top of all of this.
- **Three independent capacity initiatives now stack, not substitute.** The fab1+satellite expansion (near-term, capex-light, ~$1.5b nameplate) is distinct from fab2 (still under construction, 2028 long-term ramp) and from the third-party manufacturing/license deal (mid-term option, still being negotiated). See [[joedab12 2026-04-21 - VICR fab1 capacity expansion uses nearby satellite facility for simpler steps lifting capacity 50pct ahead of fab2 and third-party license deal]] for the operational breakdown — any single one of the three can carry the next leg of revenue growth.
- **The CEO commentary catalyst is asymmetric.** If on the Apr 21 call Vinciarelli either (a) hints fab1 can run at the full $1b+ original nameplate next year (which now means $1.5b after expansion) or (b) signals the third-party deal is close, Joe argues the stock re-rates above $300 and stays there. This is consistent with the customer-reveal read in [[joedab12 2026-04-21 - VICR customer reveal NVDA GOOG AMZN AMD all ramping vertical power delivery per founder-led management commentary]] — capacity is the binding constraint, not demand, so every capacity disclosure is a direct unlock of the bull case.
- **Investment implication: the 80pct framing is now bullish, not conservative.** When management gives a margin-of-error discount and then expands nameplate 50pct underneath that discount, the original "conservative" number becomes the new floor and the bull case re-bases higher. The discipline of using 80pct is no longer about hedging downside — it is now compounding upside as fab1 capacity scales.
