# SES (SESG.PA)

Euronext Paris-listed Luxembourg multi-orbit (GEO+MEO) satellite operator. Co-lead of the SpaceRISE consortium that won the EU's €10.6B 12-year IRIS² concession (December 2024) for a 290-satellite multi-orbit constellation, first launch 2029.
