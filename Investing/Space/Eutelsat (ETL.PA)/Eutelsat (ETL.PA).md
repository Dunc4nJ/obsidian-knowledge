# Eutelsat (ETL.PA)

Euronext Paris-listed French satellite operator; owns OneWeb LEO constellation post-2023 merger. Co-lead of the SpaceRISE consortium delivering the €10.6B IRIS² 290-satellite multi-orbit constellation alongside SES and Hispasat.
