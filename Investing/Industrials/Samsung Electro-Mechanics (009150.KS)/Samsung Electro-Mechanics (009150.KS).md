# Samsung Electro-Mechanics (009150.KS)

Samsung Electro-Mechanics (009150.KS, Korea Exchange, 'SEMCO') — Korean diversified electronic components: MLCCs (the #2 global supplier behind Murata), semiconductor substrates (FC-BGA), camera modules. NOT the same entity as Samsung Electronics (005930.KS HBM/DRAM/foundry, in Memory sector).
