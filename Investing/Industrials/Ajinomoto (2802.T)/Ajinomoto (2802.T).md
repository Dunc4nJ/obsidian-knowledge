# Ajinomoto (2802.T)

Ajinomoto Co., Inc. (2802.T, Tokyo) — Japanese conglomerate best known for seasonings, but the Fine-Techno business unit makes the Ajinomoto Build-up Film (ABF) used as the substrate dielectric layer in EVERY advanced AI / HPC chip package. Per Ming-Chi Kuo 2026-06-18 TSMC glass core substrate slide breakdown: TSMC's leaked CoPoS validation uses Ajinomoto GL107 mixed with ABF-GCP at 24-28 build-up layers — the mainstream ABF spec for AI chips 2027-2028. ABF is a chokepoint material in the AI chip BOM with effective monopoly position.
