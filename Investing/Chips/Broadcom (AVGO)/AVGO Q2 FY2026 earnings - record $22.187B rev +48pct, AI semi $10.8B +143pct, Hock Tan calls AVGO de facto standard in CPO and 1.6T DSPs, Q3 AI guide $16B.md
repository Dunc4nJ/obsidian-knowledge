# AVGO Q2 FY2026 earnings — AI semi $10.8B +143%, Hock Tan calls Broadcom the "de facto standard" in CPO and 1.6T DSPs

Broadcom reported fiscal Q2 2026 (quarter ended **May 3, 2026**, reported **June 3, 2026**): record revenue $22.187B (+48% YoY) on AI semiconductor revenue of $10.8B (+143%), with a Q3 AI guide of ~$16B. For the optical-DSP peer benchmark, the load-bearing item is Hock Tan's call statement that in **co-packaged optics (CPO), 1.6T DSPs, CW and EML lasers, Broadcom is "the de facto standard in the industry"** — and that **AI networking was ~40% of AI semiconductor revenue** (~$4.3B/quarter by implication). This sets the incumbent bar that [[MaxLinear (MXL)]] is chasing with its late-2026 Rushmore 1.6T ramp.

Companion peer reads for the 1.6T DSP race: [[MRVL Q1 FY2027 earnings - record $2.418B rev +28pct, data center $1.833B +27pct (76pct of total), 1.6T 200G-lane ramping after H2 FY26 production launch, interconnect guided +70pct YoY]] and [[CRDO Q4 FY2026 earnings - record $437M rev +157pct, FY26 $1.335B tripled, FY27 guided +80pct with optical >$600M but 200G-lane FY27 relatively light (a FY28 story)]]. Broadcom is the established CPO/1.6T leader (Goldman-named — see [[moc - Photonics]] `## Cross-sector`); MaxLinear is a late entrant at the DSP layer.

## Key Takeaways

- **Record total revenue: $22,187M, +48% YoY** (from $15,004M). GAAP net income $9,310M; non-GAAP $12,074M. Adjusted EBITDA $15,244M (69% of revenue). GAAP diluted EPS $1.91; non-GAAP $2.44. Free cash flow $10,262M (46% of revenue). Quarterly dividend $0.65/share.
- **Semiconductor Solutions: $15,009M, +79% YoY** (68% of total revenue). Infrastructure Software $7,178M (+9%), 32% of total.
- **AI semiconductor revenue: $10.8B, +143% YoY** — "above our forecast, driven by increasing demand for custom AI accelerators and AI networking" (Hock Tan). On the call, AI semiconductors were noted as ~49% of total revenue; AI bookings exceeded $30B in the quarter.
- **Q3 FY26 AI guide ~$16.0B** (+200%+ YoY). Total Q3 revenue guidance ~$29.4B (+84% YoY); non-GAAP operating margin ~67%. Hock Tan: "in Q3 we expect semiconductor revenue from AI to grow over 200 percent year-over-year to $16.0 billion."
- **AI networking ≈ 40% of AI semiconductor revenue (≈ $4.3B/quarter).** Verbatim, in response to analyst Jim Schneider's question ("About 40% of AI revenue this quarter"), Hock Tan: *"Networking represented almost 40% of our Q2 AI revenue."* (40% of $10.8B AI semi ≈ $4.3B/quarter — derived, not separately disclosed.)
- **"De facto standard" in CPO and 1.6T DSPs — the headline peer-benchmark statement.** Verbatim (Hock Tan): *"And in CPOs, which is co-packaged optics, 1.6T DSPs, CW and EML lasers, we are the de facto standard in the industry to extend AI clusters across data centers."*
- **Switching leadership.** Hock Tan: shipping "the industry's only 100 terabit Ethernet switch, the Tomahawk 6, for over one year," and "taping out our next-generation 200-terabit switch this quarter." Direct-attached copper based on industry-leading 200G and 400G SerDes; Jericho 3 / Jericho 4 fabric for cross-datacenter AI clusters.
- **FY27 AI outlook reiterated >$100B AI semiconductor revenue**; long-term multi-gigawatt compute supply deals cited with Google, Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta, with significant new deployments starting in FY27.

## Why it matters (vs MaxLinear)

Broadcom is the incumbent yardstick at the high end of optical connectivity: it self-describes as the CPO / 1.6T-DSP "de facto standard" and runs AI networking at ~$4.3B/quarter — bigger than the entire revenue base of [[Credo Technology (CRDO)]] or [[MaxLinear (MXL)]]. Broadcom's 200G/lane Sian-family DSP PHYs already sample/ramp (see [[AVGO Sian3 3nm and Sian2M 5nm 200G-lane DSP PHYs - sub-23W 1.6T, sampling Mar 2025, Sian3 production ramp Q3 2025, no CoWoS mentioned]]). Among the four named 1.6T DSP suppliers (AVGO, [[Marvell Technology (MRVL)]], CRDO, MXL — see [[Credo Bluebird 1.6T optical DSP under-20W full-DSP and LRO variants makes CRDO a 4th 1.6T DSP supplier vs MRVL AVGO MXL]]), MaxLinear is the late entrant; AVGO and MRVL are already shipping 1.6T in volume while MXL's Rushmore is still pre-ramp into late 2026.
