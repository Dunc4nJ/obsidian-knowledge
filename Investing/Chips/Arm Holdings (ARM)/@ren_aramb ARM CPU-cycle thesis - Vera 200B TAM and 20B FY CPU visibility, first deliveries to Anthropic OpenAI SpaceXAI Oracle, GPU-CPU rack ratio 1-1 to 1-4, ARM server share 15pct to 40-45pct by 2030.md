@Ren_aramb (Ren):
If you are wondering why $ARM is going vertical you have to understand the massive growth CPUs are having on this cycle.

The most important thing Jensen Huang said on the NVIDIA earnings call yesterday was not about GPUs. It was about CPUs.

"Vera opens a brand new $200 billion TAM for NVIDIA, a market we have never addressed before."

$20 billion in CPU revenue visibility this year alone. The first Vera CPUs were physically delivered this week -- Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI, Oracle Cloud. This is not a roadmap slide. This is production.

Here is why this matters structurally.

Agentic AI does not run on GPUs alone. Between every inference call, an AI agent is doing orchestration -- tool calls, API requests, memory management, task scheduling. The more agentic the workload, the more CPU-intensive the system becomes.

The Vera Rubin POD currently deploys at roughly a 1:1 ratio of GPU racks to CPU racks. Analyst Jeff Pu projects that ratio could shift toward 1:4 as agentic workloads scale -- four CPU racks for every one GPU rack. That is the bull case trajectory analysts are modeling based on where agentic compute demand is heading.

The server CPU TAM reflects this shift. 49% CAGR to $211 billion by 2030, driven almost entirely by agentic AI infrastructure buildout -- per analyst estimates from Computex 2026. UBS independently puts it at $170 billion by 2030 from $30 billion today. Either way the direction is the same.

Now here is where ARM benefits.

Every Vera CPU runs on custom ARM Olympus cores. Every Grace CPU that shipped inside every GB200 and GB300 rack over the last two years ran on ARM Neoverse cores. AWS Graviton. Google Axion. Microsoft Cobalt. All ARM.

ARM already held 50% of the cloud computing CPU market in 2025. Vera-Rubin deployments sustain and grow that. Standalone Vera CPU deployments come on top. ARM share is expected to move toward 60%.

UBS forecasts ARM's unit share in server CPUs goes from 15% today to 40-45% by 2030. Revenue share even higher at 50-55% because ARM-based AI CPUs carry higher ASPs than legacy x86.

ARM's own AGI CPU guidance was $1 billion in FY28 growing to $15 billion in FY31. ARM just doubled its near-term forecast -- now guiding $2 billion in customer demand across FY27 and FY28. OpenAI, Cerebras, Positron, and Rebellions are already integrating the ARM AGI CPU.

One nuance worth flagging. The royalty rate ARM collects on Vera is likely lower than on Grace, given the size of NVIDIA as a customer. But the volume math more than compensates. NVIDIA going from zero CPU revenue to $20 billion in a single year is hugely bullish.

$ARM is the architecture that every AI CPU is being built on, whether NVIDIA, AWS, Google, or Microsoft decides to build it.

Disclosure $ARM is a core position for me since the beginning of March and it’s been a top performer in my portfolio.
