# MXL 2026-05-28 Form 8-K — dismisses Grant Thornton for KPMG (Item 4.01)

[[MaxLinear (MXL)]] filed a Form 8-K reporting an auditor change under **Item 4.01 (Changes in Registrant's Certifying Accountant)**: the audit committee **dismissed Grant Thornton LLP** effective May 28, 2026 and **engaged KPMG LLP** for FY2026. This is the routine, clean-break flavor of an auditor change — the filing affirmatively states no adverse opinions, no disagreements, and no reportable events — but it landed in the same window as a [[MXL 2026-05-27 Form S-8 - 3.2M-share routine annual top-up to 2010 Equity Incentive Plan (~3.6 pct of shares out)|same-week S-8 share registration]], and coverage tied MXL's ~8% early-June selloff to the pair.

## Key Takeaways

- **Who initiated / nature of the change (dismissal, not resignation; audit-committee-approved):** The Company **dismissed** Grant Thornton — this was a registrant-initiated dismissal, not an auditor resignation. The "Dismissal of Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm" heading is explicit. The **dismissal was approved by the audit committee** of the board, and the audit committee also approved the engagement of the successor.
- **Stated reason for the change:** The 8-K gives **no narrative reason** for the switch. Like most clean Item 4.01 filings, it states only the mechanical fact of dismissal/engagement plus the required negative assurances; it does not cite fee, service-quality, rotation-policy, or any other motive. (Absence of a stated cause is normal for routine rotations but is exactly what fed governance/red-flag speculation given the same-week S-8.)
- **Whether prior reports were clean (the disclosure the market cares about):** **Yes — fully clean.** Grant Thornton's reports on MXL's consolidated financial statements for **FY2025 (ended Dec 31, 2025) and FY2024 (ended Dec 31, 2024)** "did not contain any adverse opinion or a disclaimer of opinion, nor were they qualified or modified as to uncertainty, audit scope, or accounting principles."
- **No disagreements / no reportable events:** During FY2025, FY2024 and the interim period through May 28, 2026, there were **(i) no disagreements** with Grant Thornton on accounting principles/practices, financial statement disclosure, or auditing scope or procedures (Item 304(a)(1)(iv)), and **(ii) no "reportable events"** as defined in Item 304(a)(1)(v) of Reg S-K. KPMG was also **not consulted** during that period on any accounting-application, disagreement, or reportable-event matter (the standard successor-clean assertion).
- **Effective dates / mechanics:** Effective Date of the dismissal = **May 28, 2026** (effective immediately). KPMG appointed as auditor for FY ending **Dec 31, 2026**. Grant Thornton's required Exhibit 16.1 letter to the SEC (stating whether it agrees with the filing's statements) is **dated June 1, 2026**; the 8-K itself was signed and filed **June 1, 2026** by CFO **Steven Litchfield**.
- **Why it mattered to the tape:** Despite the disclosures being textbook-clean, MXL fell **~8%** in early June. Coverage ([Simply Wall St](https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/semiconductors/nasdaq-mxl/maxlinear/news/maxlinear-mxl-is-down-81-after-auditor-switch-and-stock-plan)) attributed the move to the **combination** of (a) an unexplained Big-4-to-Big-4-tier auditor swap (a known governance-anxiety trigger even when boilerplate-clean) and (b) the simultaneous [[MXL 2026-05-27 Form S-8 - 3.2M-share routine annual top-up to 2010 Equity Incentive Plan (~3.6 pct of shares out)|S-8 equity-plan expansion]] raising dilution concerns. The fundamental read: nothing in the filing itself signals an accounting problem.

**Item 4.01 Changes in Registrant's Certifying Accountant.**

**(a) Dismissal of Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm**

On May 28, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), MaxLinear, Inc. (the "Company") dismissed Grant Thornton LLP ("Grant Thornton") as the Company's independent registered public accounting firm, effective immediately. The dismissal of Grant Thornton has been approved by the audit committee of the board of directors of the Company (the "Audit Committee").

Grant Thornton's report on the Company's consolidated financial statements for each of the fiscal years ended December 31, 2025 and December 31, 2024 did not contain any adverse opinion or a disclaimer of opinion, nor were they qualified or modified as to uncertainty, audit scope, or accounting principles.

During the Company's fiscal years ended December 31, 2025 and December 31, 2024 and the subsequent interim period through to the Effective Date, there have been (i) no disagreements with Grant Thornton on any matter of accounting principles or practices, financial statement disclosure or auditing scope or procedures, which disagreements, if not resolved to Grant Thornton's satisfaction, would have caused Grant Thornton to make reference to the subject matter of the disagreements in connection with its report, and (ii) no "reportable events" (as that term is defined in Item 304(a)(1)(v) of Regulations S-K).

The Company provided Grant Thornton with a copy of this Current Report on Form 8-K prior to its filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC") and requested that Grant Thornton furnish it with a letter addressed to the SEC stating whether or not it agrees with the above statements. A copy of Grant Thornton's letter, dated June 1, 2026, is filed as Exhibit 16.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K.

**(b) Engagement of New Independent Registered Public Accounting Firm**

The Audit Committee also approved the engagement of KPMG LLP ("KPMG") to serve as the Company's independent registered public accounting firm and appointed KPMG as the Company's independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026.

During the Company's fiscal years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, and the subsequent interim period through May 28, 2026, neither the Company nor anyone acting on its behalf has consulted with KPMG regarding (i) the application of accounting principles to a specified transaction, either completed or proposed, or the type of audit opinion that might be rendered on the Company's consolidated financial statements, and neither a written report nor oral advice was provided to the Company that KPMG concluded was an important factor considered by the Company in reaching a decision as to any accounting, auditing, or financial reporting issue, (ii) any matter that was the subject of a disagreement within the meaning of Item 304(a)(1)(iv) of Regulation S-K, or (iii) any reportable event within the meaning of Item 304(a)(1)(v) of Regulation S-K.
