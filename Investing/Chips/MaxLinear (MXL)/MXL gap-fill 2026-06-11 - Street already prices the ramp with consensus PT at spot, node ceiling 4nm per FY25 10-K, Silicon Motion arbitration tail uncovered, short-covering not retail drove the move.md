## How the verdict moves

**Strengthened: "no clean asymmetry at $69."** The biggest unknown (what's priced in) resolved against the bulls: consensus already models the ramp and the mean PT offers zero upside; the marginal bull case requires 65x CY27 earnings. The "sandbagging" pillar loses force — consensus sits at the top of the company's implied range, so even confirmed conservatism is partly pre-paid. The disaster band gains a new trigger (SIAC arbitration vs $61M cash). The 1–2% share datapoint (low evidence tier, but the only public number) and zero named Rushmore partners move the disclosed-win prior down, not up.

**Improved: the quality of the window.** The CPO S-curve (2.2% in 2027), FRO-first at 1.6T, LightCounting's 2027–2031 erosion dating, worsening EML lock-in (the dominant buyer hard-committing to pluggables), uniformly rising capex, Samsung 4nm yield maturity, the Luxshare channel-pull report, the $28.6M purchase-obligation guarantee, and a CEO who sold nothing into the run — all reinforce that the *window itself* is real, open through the horizon, and that MXL's costly-signal cluster keeps confirming.

**Net: the trade framing survives unchanged in structure, tighter in expectations.** Staged, falsifier-gated entry; the Q2 print (~late July) remains the decision node, with one sharpened test — consensus now sits at $657M FY26, so "beat and raise" must clear a *higher* bar than the company's own guide to move the stock. Additions to the trigger list: any SIAC arbitration ruling or accrual (hard de-risk), the Q2 13F batch (~mid-August — reveals the actual marginal buyer), and Samsung-4nm-capacity competition signals. The asymmetric-upside claim remains **not established**; what exists is a fully-priced execution story with a real window, a thin balance sheet, an unpriced legal tail, and optionality that now requires *upside surprises beyond the raised guide* to pay.
