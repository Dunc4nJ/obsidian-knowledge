# MXL 2026-05-27 Form S-8 — 3.2M-share routine annual top-up to 2010 Equity Incentive Plan

[[MaxLinear (MXL)]] filed a Form S-8 registering **3,204,107 shares** of common stock for issuance under its **Amended and Restated 2010 Equity Incentive Plan** (the "2010 EIP"). Despite headlines pairing it with the [[MXL 2026-05-28 Form 8-K - dismisses Grant Thornton for KPMG, audit-committee-approved, no disagreements or adverse opinions (Item 4.01)|same-week auditor switch]] as twin drivers of an ~8% drop, the filing itself is a **routine annual evergreen top-up**: it is the **eleventh** S-8 for the same 2010 EIP since 2017 (the cover incorporates ten prior S-8s by reference), and it simply registers the incremental shares stockholders approved at the May 20, 2026 annual meeting.

## Key Takeaways

- **How many shares / under which plan:** **3,204,107 shares** of common stock ($0.0001 par), all under the single **Amended and Restated 2010 Equity Incentive Plan**. (This is an equity-incentive-plan registration only — **no ESPP** shares are in this S-8.) File No. 333-296277.
- **Routine evergreen, not an unusual expansion:** The shares "were **added to the shares authorized for issuance** under the Registrant's Amended and Restated 2010 Equity Incentive Plan… upon **approval of the 2010 EIP by the Registrant's stockholders at the… annual meeting of stockholders held on May 20, 2026**." The cover **incorporates by reference ten prior S-8s** for the same plan (333-217021 in Mar-2017, then one essentially every year through 333-293051 in Jan-2026), making this the **11th** in an unbroken annual cadence. The stated purpose is "increasing the number of securities of the same class… for which a Registration Statement on Form S-8 relating to the same benefit plan is effective" — boilerplate top-up language. The underlying plan amendment (the actual share-pool increase) was disclosed via an 8-K filed May 21, 2026 (Exhibit 4.2), i.e., this S-8 just registers what shareholders already voted to authorize.
- **Registered shares as % of shares outstanding (computable):** MXL had **~89.529 million** shares outstanding as of March 31, 2026 (latest 10-Q). 3,204,107 ÷ 89,529,000 ≈ **3.6%** of shares outstanding. Modest and in line with a single year's equity-comp authorization for a ~$8–9B-equity-comp-heavy chip designer — not a step-change in the dilution trajectory.
- **Fee table (Exhibit 107.1) particulars:** Proposed maximum offering price per unit **$89.71** (Rule 457(c)/(h), the average of high/low MXL price on Nasdaq on May 19, 2026); **maximum aggregate offering price $287,440,438.97**; registration fee **$39,695.52** at the 0.0001381 fee rate; no fee offsets claimed. Rule 416(a) automatically extends coverage to additional shares from any stock split/dividend/recapitalization.
- **Selloff attribution:** Coverage ([Simply Wall St](https://simplywall.st/stocks/us/semiconductors/nasdaq-mxl/maxlinear/news/maxlinear-mxl-is-down-81-after-auditor-switch-and-stock-plan)) attributed MXL's **~8.1%** early-June drop to the **combination** of this "stock plan expansion" (read by the market as incremental dilution) **and** the unexplained [[MXL 2026-05-28 Form 8-K - dismisses Grant Thornton for KPMG, audit-committee-approved, no disagreements or adverse opinions (Item 4.01)|Grant Thornton → KPMG auditor switch]]. The standalone read here: the S-8 is mechanically routine and the ~3.6% dilution was already shareholder-approved on May 20, so the negative reaction looks more like sentiment/governance-anxiety overlap than a genuinely new dilution event.
- **Signatories:** Signed May 27, 2026 by CEO **Kishore Seendripu**, CFO **Steven G. Litchfield**, Corporate Controller **Connie Kwong**, Lead Director **Thomas E. Pardun**, and directors **Daniel A. Artusi, Carolyn D. Beaver, Gregory P. Dougherty, Kris A. Sennesael, and Theodore Tewksbury**. Counsel: Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati (Mark Baudler).
