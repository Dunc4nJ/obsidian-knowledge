## Summary

**MaxLinear** (MXL) elevated its full-year optical data center revenue forecast by $30 million-$40 million, now projecting $150 million-$170 million supported by hyperscale customer ramps and improved program visibility. All four business segments—Infrastructure, Broadband, Connectivity, and Industrial & Multimarket—are projected to achieve sequential revenue growth in the upcoming quarter, with Infrastructure continuing as the primary driver. Management highlighted ongoing investments in supply chain and wafer prepayments to secure capacity for expanding data center demand. The company anticipates production launches of its next-generation Rushmore and Annapurna platforms, targeting meaningful revenue expansion across 2026 and 2027. Panther SoC family revenues are expected to at least double year over year, enhancing diversification beyond optical. Future design win opportunities with key hyperscalers in XGS PON and AI system management components are set to enter ramp phases by 2027, contributing further to long-term growth prospects.

* Seendripu clarified market share strategy: "Keystone provides an affirmative statement of MaxLinear's ability to successfully get through interop, supply product at scale, and establishes our credibility as a world-class chip supplier."

## Industry glossary

* **PAM4 DSP**: A digital signal processor using four-level pulse amplitude modulation, enabling high-speed optical data links in data center transceivers.
* **XGS PON**: A 10-Gigabit symmetric passive optical network standard used for high-speed fiber connections in enterprise and hyperscale data centers.
* **AEC**: Active Electrical Cable, a high-speed electrical cable with integrated signal amplification and conditioning for data center interconnect applications.
* **CPO/LPO/LRO/XPO**: Refers to various advanced optical interconnect architectures (e.g., co-packaged optics, linear pluggable optics, linear receiver optics, and other evolving standards) implemented in data center network hardware.
* **TIA**: Transimpedance amplifier, a critical analog component converting optical signals to electrical in high-speed data communications modules.
* **TAM/SAM**: Total addressable market (TAM) and served addressable market (SAM); market sizing terminology indicating overall and addressable opportunity for a technology vendor.
