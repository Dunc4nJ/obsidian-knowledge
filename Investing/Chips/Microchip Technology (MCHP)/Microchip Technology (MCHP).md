# Microchip Technology (MCHP)

Nasdaq-listed broad-line MCU/analog semiconductor company. Makes the RT PolarFire rad-hard FPGA, one of the two canonical rad-hard FPGAs PhotonCap names for LEO supply chain. Primary thesis is the terrestrial semi cycle (>95% of revenue); flagged on the LEO map as a possible 'Hidden Card' where space revenue ramps in 2027–2028 but the market still prices it as pure terrestrial-semi recovery.
