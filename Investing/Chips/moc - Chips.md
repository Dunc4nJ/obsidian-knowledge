# Chips

Companies and research covering fabless chip designers — companies that design silicon and contract out the manufacturing to foundries (which live in `Semi Infrastructure/`). Spans GPU/accelerator vendors, fabless logic (CPUs/MCUs), networking ASICs, optical silicon, analog and mixed-signal, IP licensors, and the long tail of custom AI silicon. Distinguished from `Compute/` (companies that own chips and rent capacity) and `Semi Infrastructure/` (the manufacturing layer that supplies chip designers).
