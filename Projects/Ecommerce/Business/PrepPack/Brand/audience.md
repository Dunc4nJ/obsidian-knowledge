## Primary Personas

### Persona 1: The Meal Prepper
- **Demographics:** 25-40, health-conscious, active lifestyle, M/F
- **Motivation:** Save time during the week, eat consistently healthy, reduce food waste
- **Pain points:** Flimsy containers that leak, lids that don't match, microwave warping, fridge chaos
- **Buying triggers:** Starting a new diet/fitness plan, New Year's resolutions, moving into a new place
- **VoC language:** (actual phrases from reviews/social — to be filled)

### Persona 2: The Fitness Enthusiast
- **Demographics:** 20-35, gym-goer, tracks macros, M/F
- **Motivation:** Precise portion control, easy macro tracking, gym-bag-friendly containers
- **Pain points:** Containers without compartments, no portion markings, bulky designs
- **Buying triggers:** Starting a cut/bulk cycle, new gym membership, coach recommendation
- **VoC language:** (actual phrases from reviews/social — to be filled)

### Persona 3: The Busy Family Cook
- **Demographics:** 30-50, parent, time-strapped, feeding multiple people
- **Motivation:** Batch cook once, feed the family all week, reduce takeout spending
- **Pain points:** Mismatched containers, not enough sizes, kids can't open them easily, staining
- **Buying triggers:** Back to school, budget crunch, "I'm sick of cooking every night" moment
- **VoC language:** (actual phrases from reviews/social — to be filled)

### Persona 4: The Health-Conscious Cook
- **Demographics:** 25-45, into whole foods / clean ingredients, M/F
- **Motivation:** Store homemade meals safely, avoid plastic chemicals, keep food fresh longer
- **Pain points:** BPA concerns, containers that absorb smells, poor freshness sealing
- **Buying triggers:** Switching from takeout to home cooking, health scare, documentary inspiration
