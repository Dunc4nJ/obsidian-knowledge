## Status: SKELETON — Needs PrepPack Manager to fill in

## Voice Attributes

- **Tone:** Practical, efficient, health-conscious, no-nonsense but friendly
- **Register:** Conversational / slightly editorial — like a meal-prep-obsessed friend who has their week figured out
- **Personality:** The organized friend who batch-cooks on Sunday and actually enjoys it

## Language Patterns

### Do
- (example phrases, sentence structures, words that feel on-brand)
- Use action-oriented language: "prep," "plan," "organize," "simplify"
- Reference real meal prep scenarios: Sunday prep, work lunches, gym fuel
- Speak to time savings and health benefits concretely

### Don't
- Preachy diet-culture language ("guilt-free," "cheat meal," "clean eating" used judgmentally)
- Overly clinical or sterile tone
- Corporate speak or generic kitchenware language
- Aggressive scarcity tactics

## Sample Copy

### On-brand
> "Sunday prep. Five containers. The whole week handled."

### Off-brand
> "Our premium food storage solutions leverage innovative design for optimal nutritional preservation."
