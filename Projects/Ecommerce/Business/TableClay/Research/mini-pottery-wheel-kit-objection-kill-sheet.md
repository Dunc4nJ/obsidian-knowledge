## Detailed Kill Sheets

### Objection 1: "It'll be too messy for my home"

**Frequency:** High — the #1 pre-purchase concern across all sources
**Source:** Reddit, Amazon reviews, product Q&A sections universally
**Root belief:** "Pottery = studio mess. My kitchen/living room can't handle it."

**Reframe:** Mini pottery is kitchen-table craft, not studio pottery. The included splash guard catches water and clay. Cleanup takes 5 minutes with a damp cloth. It's no messier than baking cookies.

**Proof points:**
1. Show the splash guard/tray system containing all mess in a clean kitchen setting
2. "Fast-forward cleanup" video — timer showing 5-minute total cleanup
3. Customer testimonial: "Easy to clean. Durable and fun to use" — Lisa, VEVOR review

**Copy angles:**
- "Less mess than making pasta. Way more fun."
- "Kitchen table approved ✓ Splash guard included ✓ 5-minute cleanup ✓"
- "Your countertop will survive. We promise."

**Pre-emption strategy:** Hero images always show clean, bright kitchen/dining table settings. Include cleanup time prominently in product description. Bundle a cleanup guide.

### Objection 2: "Air-dry clay isn't real pottery"

**Frequency:** High — especially from craft-savvy and Reddit audiences
**Source:** Reddit r/Pottery, r/clay; review sites
**Root belief:** "Real pottery requires a kiln. Air-dry is just Play-Doh for adults."

**Reframe:** Air-dry clay is the gateway to real pottery — it's how most professionals started. Plus, with the right varnish, air-dry pieces are durable, waterproof, and display-worthy. No kiln means no barrier to starting.

**Proof points:**
1. Beautiful finished pieces made with air-dry clay — gallery of customer creations
2. "No kiln? No problem. Make stunning pots at home — no kiln, just clever clay and waterproof varnish." (Sculpd's proven messaging)
3. The varnish creates a waterproof, scratch-resistant finish

**Copy angles:**
- "Real pottery. No kiln required."
- "Your grandma needed a kiln. You don't."
- "Air-dry clay + our varnish = pieces that last years"

**Pre-emption strategy:** Show varnished finished pieces prominently. Include varnish in kit. Feature "before varnish / after varnish" comparison content.

### Objection 3: "I won't be able to make anything good"

**Frequency:** High — the underlying fear behind most purchase hesitation
**Source:** Amazon reviews, Reddit, universal
**Root belief:** "Pottery looks hard. I'll fail and waste money."

**Reframe:** Our kit is designed so your first piece looks good. The included guide walks you through your first pot in 20 minutes. Thousands of complete beginners have made pieces they're proud to display.

**Proof points:**
1. "First attempt" gallery showing real beginner results
2. "I am a beginner and have never done this before. I think it is very user-friendly, and amazing for beginners!" — Daniel Williams, VEVOR review
3. Step-by-step quick-start guide with photos

**Copy angles:**
- "Never thrown before? Perfect."
- "Your first pot in 20 minutes. Seriously."
- "If you can squeeze a stress ball, you can throw a pot."

**Pre-emption strategy:** Lead product page with "first attempt" results gallery. Include "Quick Win" project as the first guide — designed to produce a beautiful result in <30 minutes.

### Objection 4: "Mini wheels are just toys, not real wheels"

**Frequency:** High — especially from anyone who's researched pottery
**Source:** Reddit r/Pottery community, YouTube comments
**Root belief:** "A real pottery wheel costs $500+. This must be a toy."

**Reframe:** Mini doesn't mean toy. Our wheel uses the same motor technology as studio wheels, just in a compact form. It's a real tool that produces real pieces — just in a size that fits your kitchen table.

**Proof points:**
1. Torque spec comparison showing TableClay vs toy-grade wheels
2. Mini pottery market sellers making 40+ pieces for craft markets (PotteryBeginners.com)
3. "3 years later and still holding up" — Rhoda, VEVOR review

**Copy angles:**
- "Compact wheel. Full-size results."
- "Don't let the size fool you."
- "Mini wheel ≠ mini quality"

**Pre-emption strategy:** Use "real potter's wheel" language, never "toy." Show adult hands creating serious pieces. Compare specs to studio wheels where favorable.

### Objection 5: "It's too expensive for what it is"

**Reframe:** A $40 Amazon wheel gives you a motor and nothing else — no clay, no tools, no guides, no support. TableClay gives you the complete experience ready out of the box, with a motor that won't die on your second use.

**Proof points:**
1. Cost comparison: $40 wheel + $15 clay + $10 tools + $10 paints = $75 in separate purchases with no guide and no quality guarantee
2. "This was the perfect wheel to replace the trash I bought on Amazon for double the price" — VEVOR review

**Copy angles:**
- "One pottery class costs $80. This kit lasts forever."
- "Everything you need. Nothing you don't."
- "$40 wheels give you $40 results. You deserve better."

**Pre-emption strategy:** Value stack prominently on product page. Show "What's Included" exploded view with individual item values.
