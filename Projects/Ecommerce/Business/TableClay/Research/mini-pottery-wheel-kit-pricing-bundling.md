**Key insight:** The $70–$120 range is empty for complete wheel+kit bundles. This is exactly where TableClay should price.

## Price Positioning

TableClay should occupy the **"premium accessible"** position — above the Amazon commodity range ($30–55) and above the clay-only DTC kits ($59–70), but well below the premium wheel-only tier ($200).

**The framing advantage:** At $79–99, TableClay costs the same as a Sculpd kit BUT includes a real pottery wheel. Or: it costs $30–50 more than a bare VEVOR wheel BUT includes clay, tools, paints, varnish, and guided instruction.

**Anchor to classes, not to hardware:** A single pottery class costs $50–100. TableClay at $79 is "one class worth" but provides unlimited sessions. This is the strongest price justification in messaging.

## Perceived Value Analysis

### Value Drivers
- **Complete kit convenience** — "Everything in one box" eliminates price comparison shopping (can't easily compare to a bare wheel + separate clay + separate tools)
- **Guided experience** — Video tutorials and step-by-step projects add perceived value beyond physical goods
- **Premium packaging** — Gift-worthy presentation justifies premium over Amazon generics
- **Branded motor/wheel quality** — Safety certification and quality testing provide reassurance
- **Varnish inclusion** — Finishing solution included means results are display-worthy out of the box

### Value Detractors
- **"I can buy a VEVOR for $40"** — Direct price comparison to bare hardware is the biggest threat. Must be neutralized with value-stack messaging.
- **Air-dry clay association with "cheap"** — Some consumers view air-dry as inferior. Varnish quality and finished-piece demonstrations counter this.
- **Mini size** — "Mini" in the name may trigger "less value" perception. Frame as "compact/portable" not "small/limited."

### Price Sensitivity Signals
- Reddit users frequently discuss "$40 mini wheel" as the entry point
- "$200 gave me pause" about Small Ceramics — price ceiling for casual buyers
- "The price allowed us to try pottery without having to sell another child" — humor about affordability is positive
- Sculpd at $60–70 is widely purchased as gifts — gift buyers are less price-sensitive
- "Super overpriced" (about Sculpd at $60) — value-conscious buyers exist and vocal on Reddit

## Bundle Strategies

### Tier Architecture

| Tier | Contents | Price | Margin Target | Purpose |
|------|----------|-------|--------------|---------|
| **Starter** | Mini wheel + 1 lb clay + basic tools (5-piece) + quick-start guide | $59 | Low (25–30%) | Customer acquisition, "why not?" pricing |
| **Core** | Mini wheel + 2 lbs clay + full tools (10-piece) + paints + varnish + project guide + splash guard | $79 | Target (40–45%) | Revenue driver, default offering |
| **Deluxe** | Mini wheel + 4 lbs clay + full tools + paints + varnish + splash guard + apron + extra turntable + 10-project guide + video course access | $99 | High (45–50%) | AOV maximizer, gift market |
| **Date Night** | Core kit + conversation cards + 2nd set of tools + candle/ambiance extras | $89 | Target (40–45%) | Valentine's/gift niche |

### Bundle Concepts

1. **"Double Clay Bundle"** — Core kit + extra 2 lbs clay at a $10 discount vs. buying separately. Reduces "what happens when clay runs out?" objection. Increases session count per purchase.

2. **"Family Fun Bundle"** — Deluxe kit + kid-friendly apron + kid-size tools + family project guide. Targets the Intentional Parent persona. Premium pricing ($109–119) justified by family sizing.

3. **"Gift-Ready Bundle"** — Core kit in premium gift packaging + gift card + handwritten note option. No discount — the premium is the packaging and presentation. $89 target.

4. **"Refill Pack"** — 2 lbs clay + 3 paint colors + project card. $19–25. Subscription option at $17/month. This is the retention/LTV play — initial kit is the acquisition; refills are the profit engine.

## Promotional Strategy

### Launch Pricing
- **Recommendation:** Launch at full price ($79/$99) with a limited-time "Founding Potter" offer — first 500 orders get a free extra pound of clay and a limited-edition turntable color. Avoids training customers on discounts while creating urgency.

### Seasonal Promotions
| Period | Promotion | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Black Friday / Cyber Monday | 20% off Deluxe bundle OR free upgrade from Core to Deluxe | Highest-volume gifting period |
| Valentine's Day | Date Night Bundle launch + couple's discount ($10 off with code) | Date-night angle peak |
| Mother's Day / Father's Day | "Gift of Making" bundle + gift wrapping included free | Gift-buyer capture |
| Back to School | Family Fun Bundle featured + 15% off | Educational positioning |
| New Year | "New Year, New Hobby" — Core kit + free project guide upgrade | Resolution-driven self-purchase |

### Subscription/Repeat
- **Clay refill subscription:** $17/month for 2 lbs of clay + a new project card. Cancel anytime. This is the long-term margin play.
- **Seasonal project packs:** Quarterly themed packs (Holiday Ornaments, Spring Planter, etc.) at $25 each. Creates repeat engagement and content moments.
- **Paint expansion sets:** $15 for themed color palettes (Earth Tones, Pastels, Bold & Bright). Easy add-on.

## AOV Optimization

### Upsell Path
1. **At add-to-cart:** "Upgrade to Deluxe for just $20 more" (Starter → Core, Core → Deluxe)
2. **Post-purchase:** Clay refill pack offer on thank-you page (30% take-rate target)
3. **Email Day 7:** "Running low on clay? Refill packs start at $12" (timed to when first clay supply runs out)

### Cross-Sell Opportunities
- Extra turntables/bats ($15–20)
- Premium tool sets ($20–25)
- Aprons with TableClay branding ($15–20)
- Display stands for finished pieces ($10–15)
- Kiln-compatible clay upgrade packs for advanced users ($20–30)

### Price Anchoring Strategy
- Always show all three tiers side by side with "Most Popular" badge on Core ($79)
- Show "individual item value" breakdown: "$120+ value for $79"
- Compare to pottery class pricing prominently: "One class = $80. This kit = unlimited classes."
