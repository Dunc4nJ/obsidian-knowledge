# Messaging & Ad Angles: Ceramic Travel Mug

## Core Messaging Hierarchy

### Primary Message
**Your coffee finally tastes as good on the go as it does at home.** Handmade stoneware travel mug — every piece unique, fired at 2,300°F, made by hand.

### Supporting Messages
1. **Each one is one of a kind** — Hand-thrown and glazed, no two mugs are identical. Yours is truly yours.
2. **Real ceramic, not a coating** — Solid stoneware, not a thin ceramic layer sprayed on metal. No chipping, no peeling, no chemical smell.
3. **Made for ritual, not storage** — Designed for savoring your coffee, not keeping it in a vault. Natural cooling means perfect drinking temperature, faster.
4. **Handmade by a real human** — You can see the fingerprints. That's the point.

### Proof Points
- High-fire stoneware (2,300°F / cone 6+) — fused glaze, not surface coating
- Lead-free, FDA food-safe, dishwasher safe
- Each piece individually thrown on a potter's wheel
- Silicone lid for splash resistance + natural aesthetic
- S3 catalog shows multiple colorways (cloudline blue, nature-inspired)

## Unique Mechanism

**UMP (Unique Mechanism Proposition):**
TableClay's Ceramic Travel Mug is genuine hand-thrown stoneware fired at 2,300°F — unlike ceramic-COATED travel mugs (Fellow, Sttoke, YETI) that spray a thin ceramic layer onto stainless steel. This means zero metallic taste, zero coating degradation, and zero chemical smell — just pure clay, glaze, and fire.

**UMS (Unique Mechanism Story):**
"Most 'ceramic' travel mugs aren't really ceramic. They're stainless steel tumblers with a thin ceramic spray inside — and that coating chips, cracks, and sometimes develops a chemical smell after months of use. We asked: why not just make a real ceramic travel mug? Hand-thrown stoneware, the same material used in café mugs for centuries. Glazed at 2,300°F so the finish is fused into the clay — not sprayed on top. The result: coffee that tastes like it does in your favorite mug at home. Because it IS your favorite mug."

## Messaging by Awareness Level

| Level | Goal | Lead With | Avoid |
|-------|------|-----------|-------|
| Unaware | Create desire for elevated daily ritual | Pottery process videos, aesthetic lifestyle content, curiosity hooks | Product features, pricing, "buy now" |
| Problem-Aware | Validate taste/aesthetic frustration | "Metallic taste" problem, boring mug problem, disposable guilt | Technical specs, competitor comparisons |
| Solution-Aware | Differentiate handmade from coated/factory | Real ceramic vs. coating, handmade vs. mass-produced, unique mechanism | Generic "ceramic is better" claims |
| Product-Aware | Overcome specific objections | Durability proof, guarantee, customer testimonials, price justification | Repeating brand claims they've already seen |
| Most Aware | Close with offer | Limited glazes, bundle deals, free shipping, seasonal drops | Over-selling — they want the deal, not more convincing |
