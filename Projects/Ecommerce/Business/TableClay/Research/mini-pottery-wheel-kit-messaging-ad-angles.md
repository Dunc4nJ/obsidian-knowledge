# Messaging & Ad Angles: Mini Pottery Wheel Starter Kit

## Core Messaging Hierarchy

### Primary Message
**"Everything you need to make real pottery at home — wheel, clay, tools, and guides in one beautifully designed kit."**

### Supporting Messages
1. **Therapeutic transformation:** "Calm, focus, accomplishment — pottery is the creative escape you didn't know you needed."
2. **Beginner-first design:** "Never thrown before? Perfect. Your first piece in 20 minutes."
3. **Real tool, not a toy:** "A real pottery wheel that fits on your kitchen table."
4. **All-inclusive convenience:** "Open the box and start creating. No shopping list, no experience required."

### Proof Points
- Strong motor that doesn't stall when centering clay
- Included step-by-step guide with 5+ projects
- Premium air-dry clay + waterproof varnish for lasting pieces
- Compact footprint smaller than a dinner plate
- Safety-certified electrical components
- 5-minute cleanup with included splash guard

## Unique Mechanism

**UMP (Unique Mechanism Proposition):** "TableClay's Guided Creation System™ combines a real pottery wheel with structured step-by-step projects — so your first piece looks like your tenth."

**UMS (Unique Mechanism Story):** "Most mini pottery wheels give you a motor and wish you luck. Clay kits like Sculpd give you clay but no wheel. We asked: why can't beginners get the real pottery experience — wheel, clay, tools, AND guidance — in one box? That's why we built the TableClay Mini Pottery Wheel Kit. It's not a toy wheel. It's not a clay-only kit. It's the complete pottery experience, designed so your first piece is something you'll actually keep."

## Ad Angle Concepts

### Angle 1: "The Anti-Screen Evening"
- **Type:** Problem-solution
- **Hook:** "Put your phone down. Pick up some clay."
- **Angle:** Positioning pottery as the antidote to screen fatigue. Show the physical/emotional transformation from scrolling to creating.
- **Target persona:** The Burnout Creative
- **Awareness level:** Problem-Aware
- **CTA:** "Reclaim your evenings."
- **Platform fit:** Instagram Reels, TikTok

### Angle 2: "The $79 Pottery Class That Never Ends"
- **Type:** Comparison
- **Hook:** "A single pottery class costs $80. This kit costs $79 — and you keep the wheel forever."
- **Angle:** Value comparison against pottery studio classes. Unlimited sessions vs. one-time experience.
- **Target persona:** The Burnout Creative, The Intentional Parent
- **Awareness level:** Solution-Aware
- **CTA:** "Your forever pottery class starts at $79."
- **Platform fit:** Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Shopping

## Messaging by Awareness Level

| Level | Goal | Lead With | Avoid |
|-------|------|-----------|-------|
| Unaware | Create desire for creative outlet | Story, ASMR content, lifestyle inspiration | Product features, prices |
| Problem-Aware | Connect their stress/boredom to pottery as solution | Therapeutic benefits, screen-free messaging | Competitor comparisons |
| Solution-Aware | Differentiate from clay-only kits and generic wheels | Complete kit advantage, unique mechanism, quality | Generic "buy now" CTAs |
| Product-Aware | Overcome remaining objections | Testimonials, cleanup proof, first-attempt gallery | Repeating claims they already know |
| Most Aware | Close with offer | Price, bundle value, guarantee, urgency | Over-selling — they're ready, just need the nudge |
