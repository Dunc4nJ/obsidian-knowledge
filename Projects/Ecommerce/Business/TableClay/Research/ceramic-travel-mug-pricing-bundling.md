## Price Positioning

**Recommended retail price: $39-45** for the core travel mug.

Rationale:
- Above Nova Ceramics ($25-35) — justified by being truly hand-thrown vs. hand-glazed factory forms
- At parity with Fellow Carter ($35-40) and East Fork mugs ($30-40) — the premium craft/design tier
- Below Sttoke ($40-50) and Bru ($45-55) — accessible premium, not ultra-premium
- Significantly above Ello ($12-18) — different category entirely
- Within the range Etsy buyers already pay for handmade pottery travel mugs ($25-65)

**Key price psychology:** At $39-45, the mug sits in "affordable luxury" territory — expensive enough to signal quality, accessible enough for self-purchase (not just gifts). Reddit users noted $50+ creates resistance; $30-40 is the "worth the money" sweet spot.

## Perceived Value Analysis

### Value Drivers
- **Handmade craftsmanship** — Customers expect to pay more for handmade. "If you're looking at something handmade, expect a pretty good price jump." The making process IS the value.
- **One-of-a-kind uniqueness** — "No two are the same" inherently increases perceived value. Can't comparison-shop an identical item.
- **Taste experience** — Coffee enthusiasts validate paying $30-40+ for taste purity. "Worth the money" from r/JamesHoffmann.
- **Daily use frequency** — 365 uses/year. Cost per use is pennies. Better value than many $40 purchases.
- **Gift-worthy aesthetics** — Looks like a $60+ gift. Perceived gift value exceeds retail price.

### Value Detractors
- **No insulation** — Compared to $30-40 insulated mugs, the lack of vacuum insulation feels like a feature gap
- **Fragility perception** — "Could break" reduces perceived long-term value
- **Lid simplicity** — Silicone/cork lid feels less premium than engineered snap-lock systems
- **Brand unknown** — New brand lacks social proof that justifies premium

### Price Sensitivity Signals
- "$75 CAD for 16oz! A bit pricey for me" — hard ceiling around $55 USD for most buyers
- "Worth the money" common at $25-40 range
- Ello at $12 proves the budget market is well-served — no need to compete there
- East Fork's $30-40 mugs sell out in drops — proof that $35-45 handmade works

## Bundle Strategies

### Tier Architecture

| Tier | Contents | Price | Margin | Purpose |
|------|----------|-------|--------|---------|
| **Entry** | Single travel mug (12oz) | $39 | Standard | Acquisition, trial |
| **Core** | Travel mug (16oz) + silicone lid + cotton bag | $45 | Target | Revenue driver |
| **Premium** | Travel mug + matching home mug + silicone lid + gift box | $75 | High | AOV maximizer, gift |

### Bundle Concepts

1. **The Morning Ritual Bundle** — Travel mug + home mug in matching glaze ($75, save $10 vs. separate). For people who want the same beautiful ceramic experience at home and on the go.

2. **The Coffee Lover's Gift Set** — Travel mug + partnership with specialty coffee roaster (100g bag) + cotton carry bag in gift box ($55-60). Perfect gift-forward package. Coffee partnership adds perceived value at low incremental cost.

3. **The Pair** — Two travel mugs in complementary glazes ($72, save $8). For couples, best friends, or the person who wants a rotation. "His & Hers" or "Work & Weekend" positioning.

4. **The TableClay Starter** — Travel mug + small pour-over dripper (if in catalog) + measuring scoop ($65). Positions TableClay as a ceramics ecosystem, not just a mug.

5. **Seasonal Drop / Limited Glaze** — Limited-edition glaze available only as a seasonal release. Same base price ($45) but creates urgency and collectibility. "Spring Cloudline" / "Winter Ember" etc.

## Promotional Strategy

### Launch Pricing
- **Pre-launch:** Email list early access at $35 (15-20% off) to build reviews and social proof
- **Launch:** Full price $39-45 with free shipping threshold ($50+)
- **First 100 orders:** Include a handwritten thank-you card from the potter — zero cost, massive perceived value

### Seasonal
- **November-December:** Gift bundle push. Free gift wrapping. "Ships by Christmas" guarantee.
- **Mother's Day (May):** Gift set promotion with floral/spring glaze
- **Valentine's Day (Feb):** Pair bundle
- **New Year (Jan):** "New Year, Better Mornings" — self-purchase angle

### Subscription/Repeat
- Ceramic mugs are not consumable, so traditional subscription doesn't apply
- **Alternative:** "Glaze of the Season" club — quarterly limited-edition mug drop for subscribers at 15% off. Creates collectibility and recurring revenue.
- **Accessory repeat:** Replacement silicone lids ($8-10) as a low-cost repeat purchase

## AOV Optimization

- **Upsell at checkout:** "Add a matching home mug for $28" (normally $32)
- **Cross-sell:** Silicone replacement lid 2-pack ($12), cotton carry bag ($10), cork coaster ($8)
- **Gift upgrade:** "+$5 for gift box packaging"
- **Free shipping threshold:** Set at $50 to encourage add-ons. Core mug at $45 is just under threshold — natural incentive to add a lid or bag.
