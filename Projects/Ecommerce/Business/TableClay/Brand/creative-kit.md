## Mini Pottery Wheel Kit — Research-Backed Creative Angles

### Ad Angles (from research)
- **Anti-Screen Evening:** "Put your phone down. Pick up some clay." — therapeutic/wellness positioning for burned-out adults
- **$79 Pottery Class That Never Ends:** Value comparison vs. studio classes ($80/session) — unlimited sessions framing
- **First Attempt Gallery:** Social proof showing beautiful beginner results — kills "I won't make anything good" objection
- **Date Night Upgrade:** "Skip the restaurant. Make something together." — couples/gift positioning
- **ASMR Unboxing:** Silent sensory content — clay kneading, wheel spinning — viral TikTok format
- **Screen-Free Wins:** "My kids haven't asked for the iPad in 3 hours" — parent audience
- **5-Minute Cleanup:** Fast-forward cleanup timer demolishing the #1 mess objection
- **Stop Watching. Start Making:** Split screen TikTok scroll vs. real clay — converts lurkers to buyers

### Visual Concepts
- Kitchen table pottery setup in clean, bright apartment (not a studio)
- Multi-generational hands on clay (grandparent + parent + child)
- ASMR close-up: hands centering clay on spinning wheel
- Before/after: ball of clay → finished varnished piece
- Side-by-side: $40 Amazon generic vs. TableClay unboxing quality
- "What's in the box" flat-lay with value annotations

### Hook Frameworks
- POV transformation: "POV: You're a complete beginner and this is your first pot"
- Expectation subversion: "Pottery at home sounds messy, right? Watch this."
- Value comparison: "One pottery class costs $80. This kit costs $79 and lasts forever."
- Identity: "25,000 years of pottery. Now fits on your kitchen table."
- Social proof: "These were all made by complete beginners. On their first try."

See [[mini-pottery-wheel-kit-messaging-ad-angles]] for full details.

## Cloud Mug — Research-Backed Creative Angles

### Ad Angles (from research)
- **Morning Cloud Transformation:** "What if your morning started with a cloud?" — emotional ritual-opening angle
- **Same Shape, Different Soul:** Side-by-side comparison framing between novelty cloud mugs and craft quality
- **The Gift That Gets Used:** "Most mugs become decor. This one becomes your first sip." solves gift-graveyard objection
- **Not Another Mug:** "I don't need another mug" objection reversal with replacement in daily routine
- **The Pour Shot / ASMR:** Slow latte pour + mug closeups, high-intent social-first hook
- **Pair Ritual (Couples):** Blue + pink pair as a simple relationship/household story

### Visual Concepts
- Morning light + steam + first sip closeup
- Cloud mug + saucer in 3 home styling settings (minimal, bedroom, office desk)
- Packaging/unboxing with foam and protective care visuals (trust/content)
- Two-mug pair flat-lay for couples/housewarming context
- Mug-size comparison graphic vs common 6.5–7oz novelty mugs

### Hook Frameworks
- "What if your morning was better with one cup?"
- "You don't need a bigger mug. You need a better one."
- "A mug is cheap. A ritual is not."
- "Cute is fine. Daily-use is non-negotiable."

See [[cloud-mug-messaging-ad-angles]] for full details.
