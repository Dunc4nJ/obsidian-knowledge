# Creative Kit — TableClay

## Ceramic Travel Mug — Research-Backed Creative Angles

### Ad Angles (from research)
- **The Taste Test:** Side-by-side stainless vs. ceramic taste comparison — coffee enthusiast hook
- **The Making Of:** Pottery wheel process → finished mug reveal → coffee pour (proven viral TikTok format, 358K likes precedent)
- **The Fellow Refugee:** "Tired of your travel mug smelling like chemicals?" — targets dissatisfied Fellow Carter owners
- **The Gift Reveal:** Unboxing → reaction → morning use sequence — gifting-focused
- **The Anti-Stanley:** "I don't need my coffee to survive a nuclear winter" — tongue-in-cheek positioning against over-engineered tumblers
- **The One-of-a-Kind:** "No two exist. Once sold, that exact mug is gone forever." — scarcity + uniqueness

### Visual Concepts
- Pottery wheel close-up with clay spinning → mug emerging (process = content)
- Fingerprint/throwing mark macro shots — "This is what handmade looks like"
- Morning routine lifestyle: grinding beans → pouring into handmade mug → walking out door
- Side-by-side: factory mug (stark, identical) vs. handmade (varied, warm, alive)
- Cloudline blue glaze (from S3 catalog) featured against neutral backgrounds
- Cut-away comparison: solid stoneware vs. thin ceramic coating on steel

### Hook Frameworks
- "Your [expensive coffee] deserves better than [cheap/metal vessel]"
- "There are two kinds of ceramic travel mugs. Here's why it matters."
- "Watch your morning mug being made from a lump of clay"
- "The mug that gets me compliments every time I bring it to [location]"
- "Things I stopped buying once I got a handmade mug"

See [[ceramic-travel-mug-messaging-ad-angles]] for full details.

## Cloud Mug — Research-Backed Creative Angles

### Ad Angles (from cloud mug research)
- **Same shape. Different soul:** side-by-side positioning against mass-market cloud mugs (visual size/glaze/weight differences), avoiding direct competitor naming.
- **Gift that’s used, not forgotten:** unboxing + one-month-in use story to defeat “lazy gift” skepticism.
- **Cloud pair storytelling:** Blue + Pink pair angle for couple/friend gifting and seasonal bundles.
- **Collector framing:** “Cloud-line drop / color series” angle for repeat and social proof.
- **Packaging proof:** quick open-and-touch content proving premium packing before first sip.

### Visual Concepts
- Capacity proof shot: Cloud Mug next to a standard latte cup to signal usable size vs decorative tiny cloud mugs.
- Morning routine close-up: pour shot, steam, hand/handle ergonomics.
- Before/after desk setup: bland desk → stylized routine with cloud mug as focal object.
- Maker-story cut: wheel-forming, glaze, kiln, and custom crating moments.
- Paired mug reveal: blue/pink twin mugs in gifting frame.

### Hook Frameworks
- “Cute is easy. Daily use isn’t—until this mug.”
- “The same cloud shape at $12? Same photo, different reality.”
- “The gift they’ll actually use 365 days a year.”
- “Not another mug. THE mug.”
- “Big enough for your morning latte. Light enough to love.”
