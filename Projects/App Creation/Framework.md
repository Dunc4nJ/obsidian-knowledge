## 🎯 Core Thesis

- **B2C subscription apps = digital real estate** — build once, sell forever, no client calls
- Apps will create more millionaires than e-commerce in next 5 years
- Treat apps like rental properties, users like tenants (recurring revenue)

---

## 📋 The 8-Step Framework

### 1. Validate Before Building
- Find a painful daily problem
- Check App Store for competitors making $10k+/mo (proves demand)
- Download top apps, screenshot their onboarding flow
- Check TikTok/IG for content proving organic marketing works

### 2. Build Fast (3-7 days)
- Tool stack: **Rork + Cursor** (AI coding), ChatGPT, Superwall (paywalls), Firebase, Xcode
- MVP can be ugly — speed > polish
- Copy competitor onboarding structure (70% of app success; see also [[progressive disclosure filters force agent selectivity over what enters context]] for progressive disclosure and stepwise onboarding)
- **Hard unskippable paywall** at end of onboarding

### 3. App Store Launch
- $99/yr Apple Developer license (takes 3-5 days)
- Clear app name + subtitle that says what it does
- Screenshots: big text, one idea per screen, minimal UI

## 📣 Marketing Channels

| Channel | Cost | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| **UGC Creators** | $15/video + bonuses | 30-60 posts/mo, scale winning formats |
| **Influencers** | $1 CPM deals | One viral post = $20k (his first app) |
| **Faceless Content** | Free | Slideshows, screen recordings, 3-5x/day |
| **Founder-Led** | Free | Film yourself, copy viral formats |
| **Paid Ads** | $$$$ | Most scalable once you crack winning creatives |

### UGC Creator Payment Structure
- Base: $15/video
- 100k views = $150 bonus
- 250k views = $300 bonus
- 500k views = $600 bonus
- 1M views = $800 bonus

### Finding Creators
1. Platforms like Sideshift (not recommended)
2. Doomscroll TikTok + DM/email manually
3. Hire someone to send 100 DMs/emails daily

## 💡 Key Insights

1. **Onboarding is everything** — remind users why they downloaded, show the problem, present your app as solution
2. **Copy what works** — don't invent, validate with competitor data first
3. **$1 CPM influencer deals** — cap at $1k, no risk if they don't perform
4. **Faceless accounts need consistency** — brand + style, not random Pinterest vibes
5. **Paid ads are underrated** — scalable & predictable once you crack CPA

## 📱 Onboarding Structure

The optimal onboarding structure:
1. Remind users why they downloaded
2. Make them realize they have a problem
3. Present your app as the solution
4. Hard unskippable paywall at the end

> "If your onboarding sucks, nobody will pay for your app."
