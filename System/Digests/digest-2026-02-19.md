## Key Learnings
- **BananaBank has 224 acceptance-evidence bundles but no proven E2E production path** — the gap between "all tests pass in isolation" and "running pipeline" is the critical path. SPEC_3 MVP checklist is the gating artifact.
- **The BananaBank pipeline is code-driven, not agent-driven** — all 7 stages are TypeScript workers in Docker, not OpenClaw agents. BananaBanker is the sole operator agent. A multi-agent swarm design would require dedicated agents for strategy, QA, publishing, and analytics.
- **Skill graphs vs. skill trees**: Skill graphs are needed when the domain is interconnected, the entry point doesn't predict the traversal path, and multiple domains interact in one conversation (medicine, trading, law). Standard skills suffice for linear domains (PDF processing, code review).
