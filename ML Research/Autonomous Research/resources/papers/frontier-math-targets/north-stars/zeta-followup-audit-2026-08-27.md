## What the draft claims

The repository is an explicitly unreviewed research draft generated by GPT-5.6 Sol. It claims

\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac{N_0^s(T,2T)}{N(T,2T)}
\ge 0.673008527927\ldots,
\]

improving the Anthropic paper's `0.672500703679...` lower bound for the proportion of simple zeros on the critical line. The proposed improvement combines the Anthropic paper's analytic setup with new three-point and seven-point finite inequalities.

## Epistemic boundary

This reproduction establishes that, at the pinned commit and in this environment, the supplied code passes its tests and its interval-subdivision programs certify the two finite inequalities they are designed to check. It does **not** independently establish the headline zeta theorem.

Still unaudited are:

1. every analytic reduction connecting those finite inequalities to the asymptotic simple-zero proportion;
2. every imported hypothesis and estimate from the Anthropic paper;
3. whether the verifier's mathematical model exactly matches the quantities used in the paper proof;
4. the source implementation and Arb/FLINT trust base through a clean-room verifier;
5. expert review, formal proof transport, and literature novelty.

Accordingly, the defensible status is:

> **Finite verifier reproduced; global theorem unverified; research draft, not an established result.**

## Target-selection implication

The draft is evidence that the post-Anthropic zeta lane is moving extremely quickly and is already crowded with AI-generated follow-ups. It is an excellent benchmark for proof auditing and verifier transport. It is not a clean launch target for a new flagship campaign until the analytic proof is independently audited and the current literature/repository frontier is rechecked.
