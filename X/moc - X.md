# X

Knowledge about the X (Twitter) platform — how its ranking works and how to grow, launch, and distribute on it: audience-building, viral mechanics, launch playbooks, creator coordination, and copywriting for the feed.

## Growth & Launch Playbooks

- [[Making a launch trend on X is a four-stage system - swipe-file research, a claim-and-comment-gate hook, tiered-creator breadth, and spike conversion]] — @0xfJuan's full guide. Opens with the mental model: X promotes a post circle-by-circle (sample → bigger circle → strangers) and "trending" is the moment breadth reclassifies a post as a *subject*; three levers are **speed** (fast engagement looks like an event), **signal cost** (replies/reposts/bookmarks ≫ likes), and **conversation breadth** (many voices on one subject). Then a four-stage system to manufacture those on purpose: **(1) Research** — a Claude Code swipe file built from 50+ niche accounts, kept to above-average posts, annotated for why they won; **(2) Hook** — a sound-off 60–90s video with the magic moment in 5s, a *claim-not-description* first line, and a lead-magnet comment gate in the first reply (farms comments in the judging window); **(3) Get Attention** — tiered creators (big reach / niche-expert trust / hundreds of small "voices") briefed to post in their own voice on a timed schedule for 200+ posts in 4 hours; **(4) Conversion** — reply to everything, DM sharers same-day, deliver fast with a question, post milestones for 48h, then stay visible because buyers buy weeks later from whoever they've seen most
